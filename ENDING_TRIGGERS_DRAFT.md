# Secret endings: how they could start (draft for the owner to pick from)

Nothing here is built yet. Pick, change or cross out, and I'll build what you choose.

## How it would work

- **The office.** A door tagged with the editor's "Door: the City Administrator's office" (built, 2 November): locked to everyone but the Administrator and staff. The Administrator's guards are brought to their side when they're stuck on it (or anything else).
- **What a sealed directive is.** A page on the Administrator's device (the one they already carry; right click opens their office): "Sealed Directives", eleven envelopes, each with a wax seal and a rhyming riddle on the front (the " / " marks the line break), and a mark that warms from cold to warm to hot as the city gets closer to it. When one is unlocked, its seal breaks and the envelope glows, with a "Sign it" button.
- **What unlocks one.** Either the Administrator running the city in the strange way its riddle hints at, or the city doing it by itself in play (both are listed for each ending below).
- **Who decides, once one is unlocked** (the owner, 2 November):
  1. **An Administrator playing right now** (online, not away: away means 20 minutes idle, and that's when they're kicked too, built 2 November): it's theirs. The envelope glows on their device and they sign it or refuse it, within 20 minutes. If they do neither, it goes down the list (2, 3, 4).
  2. **No Administrator, but a superadmin online:** a popup for the superadmin: start it now, put it to the G-Man's vote, or let it pass.
  3. **No superadmin either, but an admin online:** the same popup for the admin.
  4. **Nobody at all** (no Administrator, superadmin or admin): the G-Man's vote, by itself.
  The G-Man's vote only ever comes from step 2 or 3 (when they choose it) or step 4.
- **A grace period for leaving** (10 minutes): when the Administrator (or the last superadmin or admin) leaves, nothing moves down the list for 10 minutes, in case they're rejoining or rebooting. If they (or someone higher on the list) join within that time, it's theirs again.
- **If the Administrator refuses** (the G-Man won't take no for an answer):
  1. The G-Man appears to the Administrator, says "We'll see about that" (his line from Episode One's opening), turns, and fades away walking off.
  2. **Word gets around.** For the rest of the map, about 60% of players get G-Man dreams when they sleep (and now and then while awake): the Administrator is keeping something from the people. Meant to stir up resentment, and maybe open rebellion.
  3. **He forces their hand**, in stages:
     - After about 10 minutes: supply crates appear around the city (press E, like the rebel supply crates) with SMGs, shotguns and fully charged vests.
     - After about 20 minutes, if that hasn't done it: the infestation spreads at a rate nobody can hold back.
     - (Room for more: the grid failing, the prison doors opening, rations running out.)
  4. After about 30 minutes the G-Man reappears to the Administrator and presents the same sealed directive again.
- **The G-Man subplot.** The riddles and hints reach everyone through him: dreams when you sleep, a torn page, a line in passing. He always turns up around whoever's about to matter.
- **Hints for everyone else.** Other players find hints in the world: a line from the G-Man in someone's sleep, graffiti, a Kleiner lab note, a dispatch call. So the Administrator has a reason to do odd things, and everyone else has a reason to wonder why.
- **Limits.** The usual cooldown between endings (`lc_ending_cooldown`, 180 minutes) still applies, plus at least 4 players online. Every number below is a setting.

## The endings

### THEY RETURN (`harpies`)
- **Riddle:** "Lock the streets for curfew, then kill every light; / set three prisoners loose, and they'll return tonight."
- **The city:** a bloodbath: 12 player-on-player kills in 5 minutes.
- **The Administrator:** calls a curfew, then during that curfew orders a blackout and pardons 3 prisoners. Mercy in the dark, gates open.
- **Hint in the world:** dispatch calls about "unidentified flying signatures" in the hour before.

### The party bus (`bus`)
- **Riddle:** "Cheap bread and full bowls and a bonus for all: / a city this kind, and the bus comes to call."
- **The city:** the server is struggling (badly lagging for 5 minutes, or near the engine's entity limit): the bus takes everyone away and the map reloads, instead of a crash.
- **The Administrator:** in one sitting sets prices to the lowest, rations to the highest and a job bonus on, with 6 or more people online. A city that's suddenly generous.
- **Hint:** "unauthorized transit vehicle" calls on the CP radio.

### Numa numa (`plane`)
- **Riddle:** "Three dawns of drilling, not one left undone, / and the sky opens up for a dance in the sun."
- **The city:** a morning drill with 5 or more players and no mistakes.
- **The Administrator:** calls the morning drill on 3 mornings in a row (in-game days), each one finished.
- **Hint:** Overwatch: "airspace violation, noncompliant aircraft" over the city.

### The rope (`standup`)
- **Riddle:** "Shame five in an hour while the city burns red: / the crowd wants a show, and the rope's overhead."
- **The city:** sociostability red for 45 minutes straight.
- **The Administrator:** censures 5 different citizens within an hour while sociostability is red.
- **Hint:** the Commissary runs out of rope (fishing line) for a while.

### Party hard (`hard`)
- **Riddle:** "Darken the grid and let nobody mend it: / someone parties in the dark, and nobody can end it."
- **The city:** a city-wide blackout nobody fixes for 15 minutes.
- **The Administrator:** orders a blackout (a new office power), and forbids repairs: no Maintenance Technician fixes a transformer for 10 minutes.
- **Hint:** the strobe: lamps flicker in odd rhythms during a long blackout.

### The hora (`shalom`)
- **Riddle:** "One kind word each night, for eight nights through, / and the city joins hands in a ring around you."
- **The city:** 6 or more players dancing (`/dance` or an act) in a ring.
- **The Administrator:** commends someone on 8 nights in a row (in-game nights).
- **Hint:** candles appear in apartment windows, one more each night.

### Fire (`fire`)
- **Riddle:** "Three firebombs fly, then you cleanse what you seize: / now everyone's burning, and not at the knees."
- **The city:** 15 or more things burning at once.
- **The Administrator:** calls a "Day of Cleansing" event (seized contraband burned in public) on a day when 3 or more firebombs have been thrown.
- **Hint:** smoke on the horizon; the Commissary's matches sell out.

### All your base (`base`)
- **Riddle:** "Open each cell while the rebels hold ground, / and somebody sets up us the bomb all around."
- **The city:** the master data core carried to a Resistance lab scientist.
- **The Administrator:** pardons every prisoner at once while the Resistance holds ground in the city (a block taken).
- **Hint:** terminals glitch: "MAIN SCREEN TURN ON" flashes on Civil Intake now and then.

### Marrow (`marrow`)
- **Riddle:** "Send the morticians home, let ten bodies lie: / the dead get up dancing beneath the night sky."
- **The city:** 40 deaths in 10 minutes, or the infestation over most of the map.
- **The Administrator:** suspends the morticians (a new office order) and lets 10 bodies lie uncollected.
- **Hint:** bones rattle in the bins at night.

### Bloom (`bloom`)
- **Riddle:** "Ban the poison, let the green take the street; / when the weeds are at full, every foot finds the beat."
- **The city:** every pot in rebel territory flowering at once.
- **The Administrator:** bans weedkiller (no Groundskeeper or cleanup spraying) until the weeds reach their maximum.
- **Hint:** flowers in the cracks of the plaza, more each hour.

### The flood (`water`)
- **Riddle:** "Give the priest his permit, give his faithful their bread; / the rain will not stop till the water's overhead."
- **The city:** as now: Father Grigori eats the master data core.
- **The Administrator:** permits worship (a permit to Father Grigori's flock) and holds a ration event in Ravenholm's direction.
- **Hint:** the rain gets heavier each time the flock meets.

## Decided (the owner, 2 November)
1. Both ways in (the Administrator's, and the city's own), and who decides is above.
2. Everyone sees riddles and hints, through the G-Man: dreams, a torn page, lines in passing.
3. No ending stays admin-only, for now.
4. Not signed or refused within 20 minutes: down the list (an admin's popup, then the G-Man's vote). Refused: the G-Man's escalation above.

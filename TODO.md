# Living City: to do later

- **Terminals with their UI on their own screens** (like Synapse's intake terminal). The menu is drawn on the prop's screen in the world, your crosshair is the cursor, E clicks. A reusable helper (where the crosshair meets the screen, highlight the button under it), then the Housing Allocation register and the Civil Protection Commissary on their own screens. Typing (the registration form's name and description) stays a small popup.
- **The kick, tuning.** The leg animation is bent in code (cl_livingcity_kick.lua, LC_CL.KickPose and LC_CL.KickAxes), so it may want adjusting by eye in game; likewise how far an Elite's door flies and how hard a Soldier's tips over (lc_kick_door_power).
- **Progression, next steps.** Sideways legacy perks for players with many lives behind them (looks, titles, a starting background for a new character), never power or money; items that wear out, so credits keep moving.
- **Faces.** More faces that look like real people without heavy textures: the Black Mesa citizens pack (Workshop 534130993) works without Black Mesa and adds face skins and glasses; set its looks up in Job outfits. Enhanced Citizen v5 Faces only sharpens the existing 15; its Playermodels add-on is about 590 MB, so skipped.
- **Pose Recorder 2: make it self-serve for the whole community** (pose_recorder/, before publishing it to the Workshop), so nobody needs a coder in the middle. First, in one update: (1) playback built in: `pose_play <name>` on yourself and a context-menu "Play animation" on NPCs, seen by everyone, loop, once, speed or hold the last pose; the server only sends "this entity plays this from now", each client eases the bones itself; (2) smoothing as buttons: easing per keyframe (smooth, snappy, bouncy, linear), mirror left and right, each keyframe's length, wrap the loop or stop; (5) sharing: animations as small files, packs bundled in other addons' `data_static/pose_recorder/` and picked up automatically, and `PoseRecorder.Play(ent, "wave")` for Lua coders. Then a second update: (3) a timeline strip (click a keyframe to jump back to it, reorder, duplicate, delete, scrub), and (4) putting a saved pose back onto a ragdoll (a pose library for screenshots and comics). The limit to say on the Workshop page: these are bone turns on top of the model's idle, not compiled animations, so they can't change walking or running; exporting real animation files (SMD) for modelers is a separate, later project.
- **The lost "v2.7" pieces** (built 2026-10-04 in a chat outside git, never shipped; TIMELINE.md, Disagreement 1). Rebuild, each asked about first: stamina (running and jumping tire you), weapons held lowered until raised (hold R) with no crosshair while lowered, brandishing a gun in public as a crime (a warning, then wanted), a citizen number (CID) on each character, an obituary line when someone dies, longer street detentions scaled by how long you were wanted (`lc_street_detention` is 20 now; v2.7 had 60), finding a player by CID in admin commands, colour grading and walk/breath view sway.

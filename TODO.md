- **Resistance Insider** (a Civil Protection unit secretly with the Resistance, like Barney; designed with the owner 2026-10-31, not built yet). **How you become one:** no whitelist and no F4 pick. A unit who shows mercy when they could be cold (the choices that already add to the hidden "sympathy" trait: handing back what a search found, the kinder options in Civil Protection jobs) a few times is approached by a Resistance contact through a short story on their board (F3), like the Overwatch Candidate story: accept, or turn the contact in (worth standing). **What they can do:** (1) tip-offs about planned sweeps, raids, BOLOs or patrol routes, sent quietly to Freq 7, but only from a room with nobody watching or near enough to hear: they have to sneak away to do it; (2) look the other way in a search, or let a prisoner slip on the march; (3) quietly lose a BOLO, a violation or a wanted status at the records terminal; (4) take supplies from the Commissary for a dead drop; (5) open a Combine door or cell block for a raid. Options 2 to 5 cost Civil Standing with Civil Protection, so the Insider has to balance their standing against what they do for the Resistance and come across as a little incompetent to the Combine, never openly disloyal; (6) the best intel, from the records terminal, which the quartermaster pays the most for (the owner: "really cool"). **Who knows** (the owner, 2026-10-31): ordinary Resistance players don't know who the Insider is. The Insider can reveal themselves to one person with a codeword. The highest Resistance ranks (the leaders) may know. **Caught:** wanted and hunted (no surrendering). That character is permanently out of the system: never Civil Protection or any Combine role again, never the city administration, never the CWU (Civil Workers' Union). They become a permanent Resistance member.

# Living City: to do later

- **Terminals with their UI on their own screens** (like Synapse's intake terminal). The menu is drawn on the prop's screen in the world, your crosshair is the cursor, E clicks. A reusable helper (where the crosshair meets the screen, highlight the button under it), then the Housing Allocation register and the Civil Protection Commissary on their own screens. Typing (the registration form's name and description) stays a small popup.
- **A Ration Kitchen job that fits the lore.** Rations are factory-made bars and gel, not cooked meals, so a cooking rush doesn't fit. Something on the production or distribution side instead (sealing ration packs on a line, counting allocations against the register, catching forged ration cards at the counter).
- **NPC staffing that scales with the city.** Today about 70% of the city's NPCs get a random job, and each picks work about two times in three (lc_npc_work_pct, 65, shaped by the clock); a task (a broken light, a mess, a body) is claimed by one NPC. Nothing guarantees every job is covered, or that a big city has more crews. Wanted: every occupation always has at least one NPC on it (one each in a city of about a dozen people), scaling to three or four per occupation in a city of a hundred, chosen to fill gaps rather than at random; NPCs with a job spend their day working it (not idling), so the city maintains itself.
- **The kick, tuning.** The leg animation is bent in code (cl_livingcity_kick.lua, LC_CL.KickPose and LC_CL.KickAxes), so it may want adjusting by eye in game; likewise how far an Elite's door flies and how hard a Soldier's tips over (lc_kick_door_power).
- **Half-Life: Alyx Combine models as the default for units.** First choice: **Workshop 3449180606** (all five HL:A-style Classic Combine soldier variants: regular, shotgunner, prison guard, shotgunner prison guard and Elite, plus a stripped soldier; a compilation by Volkolak of models by jQueary and others). Mapping: regular/shotgunner for Overwatch soldiers, the two prison guards for Nova Prospekt's guards near the prison, its Elite for Elites; HL2's metrocop stays (none in the pack). Check it has both playermodels and NPC models (and c_hands); if it's playermodels only, NPCs fall back to **Workshop 2041528484** (HL:A soldier and red guard, PM + c_hands + NPC) or HL2's. Detect model paths from what's installed, with an editor setting to override each; resource.AddWorkshop for whichever is used so joining players download it; fall back to HL2's models when missing. Never edit or re-upload either.
- **Far more variety in citizens, without the lag.** Synapse has many faces, hats, coats and clothes but is heavy. Do it cheaply: HL2's citizens already have faces (skins) and bodygroups we barely use; add a Workshop citizen pack with more faces and outfit bodygroups (heads and bodies mixed, not one big model per person), chosen per person and remembered, so a crowd looks like different people. Keep the counts and texture sizes modest; no high-poly packs. **The owner's pick: Workshop 534130993** (Black Mesa scientists' faces on HL2's citizen, refugee, rebel and medic bodies; playermodels and NPCs, 22 male and 7 female skins, glasses and beanie bodygroups; needs no other game). Use it for player models (and their ragdolls) and NPC citizens, choosing a body by role (citizen, refugee for the poorest, rebel, medic) and a random skin and glasses per person, remembered. resource.AddWorkshop("534130993").
- **Clothes that show the job.** Each occupation gets its own outfit (a bodygroup or skin set, or a model from the pack): sanitation overalls, medic whites, factory coveralls, the mortician's apron, a courier's bag. At a glance you can tell what someone does, NPCs and players alike.
- **Progression, next steps.** Sideways legacy perks for players with many lives behind them (looks, titles, a starting background for a new character), never power or money; items that wear out, so credits keep moving.
- **Features you discover by playing, not by reading.** A short, plain "Ways to get by" page in F1 > What now? listing what you could do right now (and what it takes), each with a button that marks where to go: run a market stall (a business license at the Commissary, Civil Standing 50), a Union job, a Resistance contact, and so on. Unlocked ones first; locked ones say the one thing missing.

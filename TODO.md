# Living City: to do later

- **Terminals with their UI on their own screens** (like Synapse's intake terminal). The menu is drawn on the prop's screen in the world, your crosshair is the cursor, E clicks. A reusable helper (where the crosshair meets the screen, highlight the button under it), then the Housing Allocation register and the Civil Protection Commissary on their own screens. Typing (the registration form's name and description) stays a small popup.
- **A Ration Kitchen job that fits the lore.** Rations are factory-made bars and gel, not cooked meals, so a cooking rush doesn't fit. Something on the production or distribution side instead (sealing ration packs on a line, counting allocations against the register, catching forged ration cards at the counter).
- **NPC staffing that scales with the city.** Today about 70% of the city's NPCs get a random job, and each picks work about two times in three (lc_npc_work_pct, 65, shaped by the clock); a task (a broken light, a mess, a body) is claimed by one NPC. Nothing guarantees every job is covered, or that a big city has more crews. Wanted: every occupation always has at least one NPC on it (one each in a city of about a dozen people), scaling to three or four per occupation in a city of a hundred, chosen to fill gaps rather than at random; NPCs with a job spend their day working it (not idling), so the city maintains itself.
- **A kick, as a real weapon (SWEP) for units.** Like Entropy: Zero 2: a boot you see swing in first person (and others see in third), that knocks people and NPCs back, breaks props, and kicks locked doors in. A few kicks to a locked door bursts it open off its hinges (the door turned into a physics prop flying off the frame, the way HL2's door breaching works), with a crash and dust: made for raids and the breach game. Unlocked doors just bang open. Prison doors and Combine doors stay shut. Kicking a door in during a raid is lawful; anyone else doing it is a crime. (The owner's Workshop kick addon, ID 2987967945, is on the server, but its first-person leg is a citizen's only: either use it and swap the leg model to match whoever is kicking (a unit's boot), or build our own the same way.)
- **Half-Life: Alyx Combine models as the default for units: Workshop addon 2041528484** (ported from HL:A by Cultist_kun and Schwarz Kruppzo: playermodel + c_hands + NPC, 4 skins; a Combine soldier and the red shotgun prison guard only). Use its soldier for Overwatch soldiers (players and NPCs) and its red guard for the Nova Prospekt guards near the prison; keep HL2's metrocop (the pack has none) and HL2's white Elite (HL:A has no Elite; a darker skin of the HL:A soldier is the fallback if the owner prefers the new look). Detect the pack's model paths from what's installed (player_manager's list, plus the NPC models next to them), with an editor setting to override each, make joining players download it (resource.AddWorkshop("2041528484")), and fall back to HL2's models when it isn't there. Their permissions: no editing or re-uploading the port; using it from the Workshop is fine.
- **Far more variety in citizens, without the lag.** Synapse has many faces, hats, coats and clothes but is heavy. Do it cheaply: HL2's citizens already have faces (skins) and bodygroups we barely use; add a Workshop citizen pack with more faces and outfit bodygroups (heads and bodies mixed, not one big model per person), chosen per person and remembered, so a crowd looks like different people. Keep the counts and texture sizes modest; no high-poly packs.

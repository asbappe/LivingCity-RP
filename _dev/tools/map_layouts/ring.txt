# east side to the gate in the first canal's south wall (x 5888..6400 at y 1856).
#
# The road and the railway run between the city and the canal (the owner, 23 November: "if we have a road between
# the city and the canal then ... the canal will be a physical deterrent and divider between the road and the 'rest
# of the world' ... I think we need to have the road AND the railway between the city and the canals"): "upper left"
# all the way round, so every module's side rooms are on the outside. To make room for the road the north run lies
# 960 farther out than the first ring's, the west run 960, the south part's west side 768 and its east side 960.
# The cave forest (west) moved out with them. (The Resistance's town north of it was taken off the map on 24 November,
# the owner: "it wastes too many brushes and kind of ruins the concept of a Resistance when they have a town; they need
# to be more underground": its branch is plain canal now, its ground the backdrop's ruins; the town is kept, map_town.py.) The west run comes straight down to the south
# part; the owner's west road meets the street by a road climbing to it ("road": it replaces their brushes 14734 and
# 15630, as roadend did). The last jog into the first canal has no street: the first canal hugs the city there;
# the Combine wall stands on the jog's own west bank ("wall bank"), and on the first canal's city banks (map_build.canal).
# Between the city and the street stands a Combine wall ("wall combine"; the owner, 23 November: "City > combine wall >
# road > railroad > canal > wall/scenery"), made by map_combine's recipe along the street's whole far side, as the
# Propper++ kit's models (map_propkit.py, _dev/propkit/; "wall combine brushes" would build it as brushes).
# The trains (the owner, 23 November: they "come from out of view ... through a tunnel or something with a black void
# that the player can't pass through and cross over top the canal via a bridge"): "left back" on a turn carries the
# track of the run after it straight back past the corner, through the square where the two streets cross, over a
# bridge across the canal of the run before it and into a tunnel in that canal's outer wall; "left over" carries the
# run before's track on over the next one's canal. A train waits unseen beyond the tunnel's black end, comes out,
# runs along its run and backs in again (with a tunnel at both ends of a run it would run through). Every other track
# end is a buffer stop short of the crossing square (map_corridor.rail_runs).

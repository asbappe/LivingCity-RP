# City 18 reference and the lessons for map pieces

Generated by `python3 _dev/tools/map_lessons.py --md` from `_dev/reference/lessons.json` (the lessons) and `_dev/reference/city18/pictures.json` (the pictures): edit those, not this page. The owner asked for both, 20 November: "Take 30 reference pictures of the city 18 map. Make a database or reference for lessons and concepts learned".

**Before building or changing a map piece:** `python3 _dev/tools/map_lessons.py --tag KIND` (doors, stairs, streets, facades, interiors, lighting, grime, sound, combine, props, geometry...) and look at the pictures of the same kind of place below. **When the owner corrects a piece:** add a lesson to `lessons.json` and rerun `--md`.

The pictures are drawn by `map_view.py` (flat colours; props as boxes of their true size, so orange slabs are lampposts, teal and orange boxes furniture): they show shape, massing, light and layout, not textures. Each costs usage to look at: read only the ones for the kind of place at hand.

## The lessons

Tags: `props`, `owner`, `compiler`, `check`, `performance`, `doors`, `stairs`, `geometry`, `bug`, `entities`, `lighting`, `city18`, `grime`, `sound`, `streets`, `facades`, `layout`, `interiors`, `combine`, `process`, `gameplay`, `canal`, `viewer`

### A thing is a prop, never brushes (`props-not-brushes`)

Doors, bars, gates, beds, tables, shelves, lamps, speakers, fences, railings, pipes, signs, machines, vehicles, guns, tools: find the Half-Life 2 model and place it. Brushes are for architecture (walls, floors, slabs, steps, frames, kerbs, roofs) and for things no game has a model of (say which in the module's 'Left as brushes' line).

- **Why:** "You use almost entirely brushes to make things when there are perfectly good props ... it literally looks like something from 1998 ... cell doors out of brushes, but there is a whole selection of cell doors and prison bars from HL2 Nova Prospekt ... a loudspeaker out of brushes but there is literally a loudspeaker prop" (20 November).
- **How:** grep -i WORD _dev/tools/dl/ValveQCs.txt | grep -iE 'source/(hl2|episodic|lostcoast)/'; sizes with map_assets.size(model). docs/dev/map-modules.md, 'Things'.
- **Checked by:** review (no automatic check)
- **Tags:** props, owner

### The prop must be the right thing, not just a thing (`prop-fits-its-job`)

Use the model made for the job: valve wheels (props_pipes valvewheel001/002) not a cart wheel, steel pipes for pressure mains not concrete culvert sections, the Combine's own crates (models/items/item_item_crate) and ration packages (models/weapons/w_package) in a Combine depot, weapon world models (models/weapons/w_*) on a gun rack.

- **Why:** Review, 20 November: a cart wheel on a flood valve, drain culverts as service mains, brush guns on a rack.
- **How:** Search the lists by what the thing is, not by what is near in size.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** props

### A physics prop is never a prop_static (`physics-props-dynamic`)

A model with prop_data (tables, chairs, shelves, lockers, boards, pots, crates, mattresses...) is deleted by the compiler as a prop_static. Place it as prop_dynamic_override (standing still) or prop_physics (loose).

- **Why:** The owner's compile of the town, 20 November: "almost none of the props are showing"; vbsp printed "prop_static using model X, which must be used on a dynamic entity (i.e. prop_physics). Deleted." for every one.
- **How:** m.static does it by itself (map_assets.is_static / is_physics_prop).
- **Checked by:** map_assets.py fails a physics model used as prop_static or plain prop_dynamic
- **Tags:** props, compiler, check

### Keep networked props to what a place needs (`networked-budget`)

Every prop_dynamic(_override), prop_physics and prop_door_rotating is an entity the server networks (the game has about 8,000 for everything). About 60 a module at most; clutter that does not need to move should be a model built static.

- **Why:** Follows from physics-props-dynamic: the town went to about 300 such entities.
- **How:** grep -c the classnames in the built VMF.
- **Checked by:** review (counts in the build)
- **Tags:** props, performance

### A door that opens is the city's own, fitted as the owner's (`real-doors`)

Doors players use are prop_door_rotating with door01_left, set up as the owner's 134 are, in an opening 50 wide (from 2 behind the hinge) and 110 high, the door's middle 55 up. The jambs fill the reveal so nothing is seen past the door.

- **Why:** "The apartments' door frames are slightly too large for the doors and I can see past the door into the apartments" (20 November). Measured round 42 of the owner's doors.
- **How:** map_town.real_door and door_hole.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** doors, owner

- **See:** `05_flat.png`

### A doorway nobody can use never shows a real door (`no-fake-doors`)

Never put the door01_left model (or any door that looks openable) where it does not open: board the doorway up (planks, wood_board props), chain it, or brick it.

- **Why:** "The door prop you used for all these unopenable doors is the same prop ... for actual doors that are supposed to open, so it's confusing and I can't tell where I can actually go in" (20 November).
- **How:** map_town.facade boards unused doorways; heavy doors that never open (lock, swing bridge) still to fix.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** doors, owner

### Steps about 16 high and 24 deep (`stairs-16-24`)

Fewer, larger steps: rise about 16, tread about 24 (the same slope as 8 and 12, half the steps).

- **Why:** "It's like a stairway meant for toddlers or old ladies? these steps should be fewer and larger" (20 November).
- **How:** m.steps does it; hand-made stairs should match.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** stairs, owner

### A stair along a wall has a solid open side (`stair-open-side`)

Give a wall stair an upstand (a parapet about 32 high following the flight) on its open side and fill under it to the floor, so it reads as one built stair, not a row of blocks hung on the wall.

- **Why:** Review, 20 November: from the canal the quay, arcade, lift and pipes stairs read as a sawtooth.
- **How:** To add: a wall_stair helper in the tools.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** stairs

### Pieces meet: no gaps between walls, banks and steps (`no-gaps`)

Where something stands at a cut in the canal's banks, it fills to the cut's edge: the banks are cut a whole 64 square at a time by m.no_bank. Side walls of steps go down to the floor.

- **Why:** "The geometry in the canal entrance thing has a gap for the sloped walls and the grey concrete brush underneath the railings, there is a gap on both sides" (20 November).
- **How:** map_town.landing: cheeks 64 wide, solid to the floor.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** geometry

### Nothing floats, nothing is sunk, nothing clips (`nothing-floating`)

Every prop touches a floor, a ceiling, a wall or another prop; lamps sit on their ceilings; bodies lie on the ground they are on (work out a mound's height from the build's bumps).

- **Why:** Floating dome lamps and props in walls found in several modules (20 November); the owner saw floating ropes in the garden.
- **How:** m.static takes the floor z the thing stands ON; map_assets sizes.
- **Checked by:** map_floating.py (props touching nothing), the build's LOOK lines (props in walls, through roofs, under floors)
- **Tags:** geometry, check

### No ropes hanging in the air (`no-floating-ropes`)

A rope or cable is tied at both ends to something that is there (a pole, a wall, a hook), or it is not placed.

- **Why:** "Please remove the floating ropes in the garden" (20 November).
- **How:** m.rope: check both ends.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** geometry, owner

### No open pipe end shows its inside (`cap-open-ends`)

Cap every pipe end players can see (props_pipes pipe02_connector01, pipe_cap005, a cone or plate brush), and never leave a nodraw face where it can be seen.

- **Why:** "At the end of the pipe for the distillery we need a cone brush or something since the pipe has nodraw on the exposed end" (20 November).
- **How:** The still: a cap and a funnel (map_mods_b.frustum).
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** geometry, owner

### Water never shares a plane with a floor (`water-plane`)

A water brush's top sits at least 2 under any floor it lies beneath, or the two flicker through each other.

- **Why:** Review, 20 November: the flood room's drained water lies exactly at its floor.
- **How:** Fix in map_mods_c.py (the func_water_analog's box to FL - 2).
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** geometry, bug

### A fire in a drum stays in the drum (`fire-in-drums`)

env_fire falls to the ground under it unless spawnflag 16 (Don't drop) is set: set it for any fire placed above the ground (drums, braziers, stoves).

- **Why:** "The burn barrels flanking the podium have fire on the ground inside the barrel instead of the fire coming out the top" (20 November).
- **How:** map_mods_b.fire(..., drop=False).
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** entities, owner

### The loose mattress lies on its back unless turned (`mattress-turned`)

Turn furnituremattress001a over (roll 180) when placing it.

- **Why:** "The mattresses in the clinic are all rotated 180 degrees on their side" (20 November).
- **How:** map_mods_b.mattress().
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** props, owner

### What Half-Life 2 made move, moves (`moving-things-move`)

Swings, roundabouts, hanging things: physics props on phys_hinge (or ropes parented to them), not frozen models.

- **Why:** "HL2 had a working merry-go-round, and working swings" (20 November).
- **How:** map_mods_b.swing; the playground's roundabout.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** entities, owner

### A gate goes in its frame (`gates-in-frames`)

Nova Prospekt's interior_fence002e is the frame for the gate interior_fence001g: hang the gate in the frame (or use the gate alone), never the frame as the gate.

- **Why:** "The gates you used are the gate/door frames for the real gate interior_fence001g; so they have to be fitted inside of the gate frames, or just used instead" (20 November).
- **How:** The pit: frame static, the gate riding an unseen swing door.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** props, owner

### A lamppost's arm reaches over what it lights (`lamppost-arm`)

lamppost03a's arm runs along the model's +y (90 degrees left of its yaw): turn it so the arm is over the street or the place, never over a wall or a drop behind.

- **Why:** "The lamppost at the top of the canal entry stairs is backwards and should be facing the street not the stairs" (20 November).
- **How:** Yaw so model +y points at the street.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** lighting, owner

### A lit lamp uses the lit model (`light-model-matches`)

Where a light shines, the fixture is its _on model (light_cagelight02_on, not _off with a light under it); a dead lamp has no light.

- **Why:** Review, 20 November: overflow, thumper, railbridge and armoury.
- **How:** m.lamp uses real fixtures.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** lighting

### Light as City 18 does: many small fixtures, some with beams (`city18-lighting`)

Light a room with two or three fixtures, not one; point some (light_spot, point_spotlight); use the city's own fixtures: dome lights, lab fluorescent tubes indoors, cage lights in service places, the Combine's light on Combine places, lampposts outside.

- **Why:** The owner's map: 597 lights, 391 light_spot, 174 point_spotlight; dome light 151, lab fluorescent 149, Combine light 75, lamp fixture 52, cage light 47.
- **How:** m.lamp, m.spot, m.light.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** lighting, city18

### Lay grime on: stains, cracks, trash, posters, signs (`grime`)

### Building fronts, not brick walls (`facades`)

Dress a building line as houses: lots of different heights, a plinth, a string course, a cornice, pilasters between neighbours, windows recessed with sills and lintels, downpipes, real doors where there is a way in.

- **Why:** "There's no design, windows, building facades, nothing! I was hoping you'd make something more similar to the city just on a much smaller scale" (20 November).
- **How:** map_town.facade.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** facades, owner, city18

### Not every window the same glass (`glass-variety`)

Use several of the owner's glass materials, each laid exactly as their map lays it, a different one per house.

- **Why:** "The window texture fits right but it's the same texture and it looks awful because every window looks like that; we need to use the varieties of glass" (20 November).
- **How:** map_town.GLASS: six fits read from the owner's map.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** facades, owner

### Cut corners and a broken skyline (`skyline-corners`)

Corner buildings get a cut (chamfered) corner, often with a doorway; rooflines get chimneys, aerials, dishes, the odd pitched roof; facades get balconies, shutters, shopfronts with signs and awnings.

- **Why:** City 18's massing (octagonal columns, cut corners, stepped blocks); review of the town, 20 November.
- **How:** To add to map_town.facade.
- **Checked by:** review
- **Tags:** facades, city18

### A town is not one straight line (`town-shape`)

Lay a town as an L or a U (corners), keeping the two arms' modules apart behind a corner (a stretch of house fronts alone, 1280 long, on the inner side).

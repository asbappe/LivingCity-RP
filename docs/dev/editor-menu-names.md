# The editor's list: every name, before and after (14 November)

The owner, 14 November: the editor menu's names "need a naming scheme" and are "not sorted under their respective categories in any discernable or common sense fashion".

**The scheme:** a label is `Category: name (hint)`. The list shows the name with a capital first letter, A to Z inside each section. A thing that belongs to one side starts with that side, always spelt the same way: **Administrator's**, **Civil Protection**, **Combine**, **Resistance**, **Union**. A thing for everyone has no side in its name.

Only the words shown changed. Every point keeps its internal name, so saved layouts are untouched. Hints in brackets are left off below.

## Arranged by what it's for: what moved

- **The Resistance and rebel bases:** the locker is out (it is the citizens', for Civil Standing 150 and up). Everything of the Resistance's now starts with "Resistance": spawn, lookout post, bunk, graffiti wall, the five way marks, territory.
- **Civil Protection and the Combine:** the Combine landing zone and the Combine suppression device are in; the Administrator's broadcast studio and office door are out (they are in her folder).
- **The Administrator** (was "The Administrator's last stand"): her desk, power, speech stand, speech crowd, broadcast studio and office door together.
- **Arriving and leaving the city:** the landing zone is out (it is the Combine's).
- **Power and the street:** the small Breencast screen, the small TV and the street bin are in, beside the transformer, streetlight, loudspeaker and giant screen.

A medium Breencast screen, when it is added, is `Street: Breencast screen, medium`: it then sits between the giant and the small one.

Added 15 November: `Fixture: Resistance locker` (under "The Resistance and rebel bases") and `Fixture: Civil Protection locker` (under "Civil Protection and the Combine"), beside the citizens' `Fixture: locker`; and the medium screen, `Street: medium Breencast screen`.

Added 15 November: `Fixture: Combine monitor` and `Point: Combine camera` (the camera monitors), both under "Civil Protection and the Combine".

Added 15 November, later: `Fixture: Combine monitor, hanging` beside `Fixture: Combine monitor`, both under "Civil Protection and the Combine". **Later still (the owner: "The model is wrong, just delete it and keep the hanging combine monitor entity as the only one"):** the wall monitor is gone; the hanging one is now `Fixture: Combine monitor` (its internal name stays `monitor_hang`), and a wall monitor saved in a layout is dropped when the layout loads.

Added 15 November, late: `Point: Administrator's APC` (kind `adminapc`), under "The Administrator": the APC that stands by the stand during her address and takes her out if she is attacked.

Added 17 November: `Point: Administrator's helicopter` (kind `adminheli`), under "The Administrator", beside her APC: the attack helicopter that stands by the stand during her address, flies her to her office door if she is attacked, and fetches her to the stand when she calls her address from outside that door.

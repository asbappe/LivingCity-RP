# Handoff: the 7 November playtest fixes

Everything done for the owner's 7 November list, across two chats. It's merged into `main` along with the session branch `claude/adoring-rubin-8hsh69`. **Nothing here has been checked in game.** Each item has a line in `PLAYTEST.md` marked "(7 November)". The owner is handling the pending items, which are listed at the end.

## Tests

- The client smoke test passes.
- Every group's test units pass on the merged branch. The results are under "Tests on the merged branch" below.
- The full 40-minute suite was not run, because the owner asked for less testing.
- The core snapshot check fails as expected, because the core changed (see "Core changes"). `_dev/core_snapshot.txt` is not updated yet.

## Done

**Breathing, sleep, weather, the dream (first batch)**
- **Panting stops:** the out-of-breath panting stops once stamina is back, after 8 seconds at most, and on respawn.
- **Sleeping again:** you can sleep again 18 in-game hours after waking, and the bed says how many hours are left.
- **Weather in chat:** removed.
- **The G-Man dream:**
  - He is drawn solid, so there's no black square over his mouth.
  - He fades in over about 3 seconds, and the riddle follows.
  - The dream lasts 26 seconds (it was 9). His other visits last 12 seconds and a summons 14.
  - His voice is a random `sound/livingcity/gman/dream*` recording if there is one, otherwise the new setting `lc_gman_line_dream` (his Episode One inhale).

**Stalkers**
- **Things a stalker can't use:** no beds, toilets or baths, and no HOME or "report to your block" on the curfew bar.
- **The processing scene:** the machine whirring plays once and stops. The breathing stops after 5 seconds. The music cuts to `music/hl2_song26.mp3`. The scream is at 0.3 volume.
- **Processing:** a technician waits at the waypoint holding the harness (the galmastu vest if it's on the server, else a wrench) and fits it when the stalker arrives.
- **The broom:** it's held in front, right way up, and positioned by the new setting `lc_broom_hold`. It sways while walking and while holding E on grime, and never vanishes.
- **Grime:** it lies along slopes. Each spot is checked so it can be reached, and after 75 seconds without sweeping, the last spots move near the stalker.

**Civil Protection and the Overwatch**
- **F1 order:** 1 Voice lines, 2 Unit tools, 3 Kick, 4 Call for backup, 5 BOLO, 6 Send my 10-20.
- **Sociostability:** only the Administrator can set it (F1, C > The city, the records terminal). The server refuses anyone else.
- **Ranks:** a Recruit can no longer take Overwatch Commander. Overwatch roles need the conditioning. The Scanner Operator role is gone: any unit flies a scanner from a terminal.
- **Turrets and power banks:** the Overwatch and admins only.
- **"Freeze":** plays "Don't move!".
- **Backup:** answering units join your squad for 5 minutes and fight whoever you fight.
- **Killing a rebel:** +5 standing, +2 credits and a box of rounds.
- **Vests at a power bank:** units hold E on a live bank to recharge. For anyone else it's "Tampering", and the bank says so up close.
- **Running:** the first and second time in an hour, units cuff you, beat you, laugh and say "you can go". The third time is prison.
- **No shoving:** no "Stop running" and no "move back" shove in the infestation zone, and a unit never shoves the player it's escorting.
- **Prison:** guards keep out of cells and doorways, so a cell door isn't held open.

**Infestation cleanup**
- **The escort:** two Overwatch soldiers, one with a shotgun and one with a pulse rifle, with 4x damage. They're spawned if none are free.
- **No more stuck soldiers:** they spawn at least 60 units apart. A soldier steps aside when the worker walks into them, and moves behind the worker if still stuck.
- **No more shooting through walls:** they attack only what they can see and otherwise go round to it.
- **The quarantine post:** the sign, lamp and barrel are kept off the route and out of walls.
- **Growths:** 3, then 5, 7, 9, up to 11 per job, and new growths spawn out of sight.
- **Bags:** they no longer block NPCs or players.

**Union jobs and tools**
- **Getting tools back:** the hoover and weedkiller come back on rejoining and after prison, while you still have the job. Weedkiller also comes with each shift and when the sprayer runs empty, and the Union Commissary sells it.
- **Losing the job:** becoming a unit takes the job, the hoover and the weedkiller. Becoming an anticitizen takes the job and the hoover, but keeps the weedkiller.
- **Job changes:** once every 15 minutes (`lc_job_cooldown` 900). The refusal says how long is left.
- **The sanitation worker:** their work clothes stay on all shift.
- **The hoover:** it's drawn about 2 feet further out, with the airboat motor under its own sounds.
- **Weeds:** they're planted on the floor.
- **The incinerator:** its fire shows from the start.
- **Commissary signs:** both Commissaries have a floating sign in the intake style.

**Rations and beds**
- **The ration line:** the front citizen walks to the dispenser, collects and leaves. During a distribution, nearby idle citizens are called to the line.
- **Your ration:** a new character's first ration is never the docked one. A player at the counter holds the line only while being served.
- **Ration line supervision:** citizens now come, so the job can finish.
- **"Someone's already asleep":** a bed counts as taken only while someone lies in it or is walking to it.
- **Sleepers:** they lie on the bed as a still copy with eyes shut and no gun, and nobody stands on beds.
- **Getting up:** people get up on the bed's own floor, so nobody ends up in the hallway or with feet in the ceiling.

**Vortigaunts, rebels, citizens**
- **Collared vortigaunts:** they sweep with a broom, step aside when walked into, and keep out of doorways.
- **Shackled vortigaunts:** they never zap. They run from headcrabs and zombies.
- **Rebels:** they avoid doorways and narrow halls. A zone holds only as many as its open floor fits, and the extras are removed out of sight.
- **Citizens under fire:** they run from gunfire, and some crouch and cover their heads first (`lc_gunfire_fear`).

**Food**
- **Spoilage:** raw and cooked headcrab, bird and fish spoil after one city day. A countdown shows in the pockets and in storage.
- **Bird meat:** a crow leaves a raw crow, a gull a raw gull, and a pigeon a raw pigeon.
- **Fish models:** raw fish is a grey bait pod (HL2 has no fish model) and fried fish a takeout carton.
- **Fishing line:** 4 to 8 casts, then it snaps.
- **Birds:** they only appear under open sky.

**Shops, items, the disguise**
- **Shop buttons:** the price always shows, and a red note gives the shortfall. Selling keeps the scroll position.
- **The bypass charge:** its description says what it does. The door is re-checked and reopened for its first 10 seconds.
- **Cuffs:** the description is clearer.
- **The call box:** its tip points to a new "Call for help" button in F2.
- **Pockets full:** the message now says which limit you hit, large items or weight. This changes the message, not the limit itself.
- **The CP disguise:** it uses an HL:Alyx metrocop playermodel when one is installed or named in `lc_pm_metrocop`.

**Popups, quests, gestures, the Administrator**
- **Popups:** they show one at a time, with a gap and a "+N more" count.
- **The wedding ring:** a new character no longer gets one.
- **"Your story" quests:** they appear only after 2 to 4 ordinary jobs.
- **Gestures:** they play as the player's own animation.
- **Hands up:** you no longer slide forward and back. A unit's search tells you to put your hands up.
- **The Administrator:** looks like Mossman (women) or Breen (men), and gets their own face back on stepping down.
- **"Gone dark" story:** it says headcrab blood, not shells.
- **"Pick up that can":** only near a bin, never in the infestation zone or on a job, and the bin is marked.

**Also done this chat**
- **The dealer and the scientist:** they move. They switch between idle animations, turn their head to the nearest player, and reach when someone deals with them. The dealer cowers when busted.
- **The credits and standing audit:** it's in `docs/dev/economy-audit.md`. Infestation Cleanup pays the flat 15-credit shift wage, the same as a litter sweep.
- **The perf pass from the other chat:** merged in.

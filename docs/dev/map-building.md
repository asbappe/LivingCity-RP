# Building pieces of map (`_dev/tools/map_build.py`, `map_forest.py`, `map_ring.py`, `map_raven.py`)

The owner, 19 November: "It is SO labor intensive to do all the menial map building ... I wanted to add more of an 'edge of city' or even outside of city area to the map for the resistance to hide in the woods outside the city, or in the canals." They use **Hammer++**. Their map in progress is `c18_lc.vmf` (27 MB, a decompile of `rp_c18_ug` they are changing; on their Google Drive, see `docs/dev/map-survey.md`).

**Start from Valve's maps where they have the thing** (the owner, 21 November: "I am totally ok with them just
copy/pasting sections, rooms, etc out of the HL2 and episodes VMF files and adapting them to the gamemode"): lift the
room, street or set piece with `map_prefab.py extract` and adapt it (CLAUDE.md, "Map work"; the lesson
`owner-copy-valve-rooms`; the pieces already lifted: `docs/dev/prefabs.md`).

**The rule:** the tools write a NEW, separate VMF for each piece. They never change the owner's VMF. Since 20 November `map_merge.py` (below, "One file") also writes ONE new VMF with the owner's map and all the pieces in it, at the owner's asking; the owner's own file is still only read. The owner brings a piece in with a `func_instance` at 0 0 0 (a new version is then just a new file) or with Paste Special at no offset, looks, and compiles. A chat cannot compile or see the map in 3D: the owner is the eyes, and only their compile proves a piece is sealed. **Nothing here has been opened in Hammer or compiled yet** (19 November): the first load of each file is the real test.

```bash
python3 map_build.py canal                                   # lc_canal.vmf
python3 map_build.py ring                                    # lc_ring.vmf: the canal round the north of the city (map_ring.py)
python3 map_build.py ring --against c18_lc.vmf               # ...and check nothing in it runs into anything in the owner's map
python3 map_build.py forest_bunker --preview bunker.png      # lc_forest_bunker.vmf, and a picture from above
python3 map_build.py forest_cave --preview cave.png          # lc_forest_cave.vmf, cave.png and cave_cave.png
```

`--origin X Y` moves a piece, `--floor Z` sets a forest's gate floor (400: the canals' floor), `--first-node N` its first path node id (4000 canal, 4400 and 4600 the forests, 5000 the ring).

**`--test` writes `lc_PIECE_test.vmf`: the piece made to compile and be walked BY ITSELF** (a spawn point, the map's own sun (its `light_environment`, and since 22 November the `env_sun` that shows the sun in the sky, at the light's own angles), and for the canal a sky wall where the road comes in), all in a visgroup "for testing alone (never in the real map)". The owner's first compile (19 November, vbsp++ on `lc_canal.vmf` alone) leaked at the quay's path node, 3808 1952 672: not a fault in the piece, the canal is open to the road by design and was compiled without the city. The tool's flood check gives the same leak when the road's opening is not stopped up, and now also fails if any entity is outside the sealed space. What that compile did show: vbsp++ read the file and its brushes without complaint.

## How a piece is made

- **Boxes on a grid (`Plan`):** a piece is drawn on 64-unit squares from above (`Plan.paint`): each square is open under the sky, open under a ceiling ("low"), or nothing. From that come a floor block under every open square, a lid over it, and a solid block in every square touching an open one (corners too), so it is sealed by construction; alike squares are joined into the biggest rectangles. Walls have looks by height (`bands`). Used for the canal and the bunker.
- **Displaced ground and skin (`Vmf.box_text(disp=...)`):** a box face can be displaced, every point of its grid moved to any place. Box corners are written clockwise seen from outside, with Hammer++'s `vertices_plus`; a displaced face's rows run from its first corner to its second and its columns to its fourth. **This was checked against the owner's own VMF:** with that reading, all 473 edge points that its 74 displacements share meet exactly; with rows and columns swapped, 98 do not.
- **Gates:** every piece ends in the same culvert, 512 wide and 256 high (`GATE_WIDE`, `GATE_HIGH`), on the plane where the next piece begins, closed by a black wall and bars in a visgroup of its own ("... gate's cap (delete to join)"). To join two pieces, delete the cap on each side. Each piece is sealed by itself with its caps in.
- **Checks before writing:** every corner on the 1-unit grid; a flood from the air inside must not reach outside (taking away any sealing brush makes it fail: tried); for forests, every ground patch meets its neighbours, the ground stays inside the shell, the cave's skin meets itself everywhere it should, and folded quads are counted. `map_survey.py` on the pieces and the owner's map together showed no piece overlapping another or the city (apart from the two brushes the owner is to delete).
- **Craft, as the file is written** (`map_build.craft`, 21 November): lightmapscale by where people walk, the canal's concrete at texture scale 0.5 and every wall's texture starting at its foot, as Valve builds (`docs/dev/map-modules.md`, "Valve's building craft"). Every piece, the forests and Ravenholm too.
- **Assets:** every model and material name in the three files was checked against file lists of Half-Life 2, Episode One and Episode Two (the Garry's Mod spawnlists, and decompiled QC and VMT trees: `github.com/Facepunch/garrysmod` `settings/spawnlist_default`, `github.com/Pinsplash/ValveQCs`, `github.com/Pinsplash/ValveVMTs`), and every `prop_static` model was checked to be compiled as a static prop. Canal and bunker surfaces use materials already in the owner's VMF. How any of it looks is unchecked.

**A module is a stretch of canal with one thing in it.** Every module starts and ends on the canals' own cross-section (512 wide, floor z 400), so any module follows any other, in any order, heading any of the four ways. **The list of modules, what each names for the game and what each says of itself: `docs/dev/canal-modules.md`** (30 KB: grep it, `grep -n '^| .cistern' docs/dev/canal-modules.md`). How to write a module: `docs/dev/map-modules.md`.

**To move things, edit a layout** (`_dev/tools/map_layouts/*.txt`, text files): `start X Y` and `heading` place the whole chain; then one module on each line in walking order. A chain is one VMF: gates (capped, with an areaportal) only at its two ends, open canal between modules. ## Combine structures and a Combine scene from the study's recipe (`map_combine.py`, `combine/`; 21 November; the owner compiled the scene on 22 November)

python3 map_combine.py check FILE.vmf                                # the study's numbers for the Combine in any VMF
python3 map_combine.py perimeter --out lc_combine_perimeter.vmf [--length 3072] [--height 768] [--seed N]   # the city's perimeter wall, a template

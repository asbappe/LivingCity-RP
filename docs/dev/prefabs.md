# Prefabs: pieces lifted from City 18 and Valve's maps

The owner, 21 November: "don't be afraid to more or less rip off and copy the design principles, textures, etc from city 18; the modules are really bad with texture choice and architecture". This library holds real pieces of the owner's own map, City 18 (`c18_*`, 28 pieces), and of Valve's Source SDK sample maps (`valve_*`, 39 pieces, 13 of them `valve_canal_*` from Route Kanal, 7 `valve_eli_*` from Black Mesa East and 7 `valve_citadel_*` from the Citadel, the last two kinds carrying their logic too), so a generated map piece can be dressed with their architecture and their textures, laid exactly as they laid them.

Each piece is one JSON file in `_dev/reference/prefabs/`: every brush wholly inside a box of the source map (world brushes and detail alike), each face with its material and texture alignment, and the props, lights and decals inside the box. The tool is `_dev/tools/map_prefab.py`.

## Using a piece

In a module (`_dev/tools/map_mods_*.py`):

```python
from map_prefab import place
place(m, "c18_entrance_a", u, v, z, turn)
```

The piece's anchor goes to (u, v, z) of the module's frame, turned `turn` quarter turns anticlockwise seen from above (0 to 3), on top of the frame's own turning. Its brushes go in as one func_detail (a piece never seals: the module's own plan does that); its props, lights and decals go in moved and turned with it. `place(..., props=False)` leaves the props out.

Every piece's anchor is at z 0 on the floor or ground it stands on, and on the plane of the wall it dresses, so it can be set on a wall line. The table says which way each piece faces before it is turned. Turning by quarter turns:

| faces | turn 1 | turn 2 | turn 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| -y | +x | +y | -x |
| +x | +y | -x | -y |
| +y | -x | -y | +x |
| -x | -y | +x | +y |

Commands (from `_dev/tools`):

```
python3 map_prefab.py list                          the library, with each piece's box and note
python3 map_prefab.py show NAME --out a.vmf         a VMF with only that piece (anchor at 0 0 0), for map_view.py
python3 map_prefab.py extract MAP.vmf NAME X1 Y1 Z1 X2 Y2 Z2 --anchor X Y Z --note "what it is" [--wire-box X1 Y1 Z1 X2 Y2 Z2]
```

Each JSON's `note` says what the piece is, which way it faces, its size and anything to watch for. Read it before placing.

`extract` keeps displaced faces (since 21 November): a side with `disp` holds its dispinfo, its start moved with the anchor; `map_prefab.disp_side_text(disp, turn, d, indent)` writes it turned and moved (its grid's vectors turned with it). `place()` writes a piece as detail, which cannot carry displacements: place such a piece's brushes as world brushes, as `map_silo.Escape.cave` does with the cache cave.

## Things to know

- **Doors and lamps come along.** A piece with a doorway carries the source map's door (`prop_door_rotating`) and lamp; `place()` gives each copy's names (targetname, parentname and the like) their own ending, so two copies' doors never open together.
- **Moving brushes taken shut.** `c18_garage_a` holds the roller door (a func_door in City 18) and `valve_shop_a` Valve's breakable glass and shelves (func_breakable and func_breakable_surf), and `valve_canal_sluicegate_a` and `valve_canal_lockgate_a` the canal gates (func_movelinear), all taken as fixed slabs.
- **Black panes.** City 18 puts a `tools/toolsblack` pane behind shop and house windows whose rooms are not built; `c18_window_b`, `c18_bay_a` and `c18_shopfront_b` keep theirs.
- **Content outside Half-Life 2:** `c18_culvert_a` uses `concrete/concretewall056c`, a Counter-Strike: Source material (City 18 uses it, so the owner's server mounts it). Every other piece passes `map_assets.py`. The env_sprite glows are written `sprites/light_glow03.vmt` (City 18 and Valve write `materials/Sprites/...`, which the game reads the same but the assets check does not).
- **Valve's stairs** are 8 high per step (`valve_stairwell_a`, `valve_metalstair_a`); City 18's outdoor stair `c18_stair_b` is 16 high and 16 deep, steeper than our 16 by 24 rule.
- **Pieces with logic.** The `valve_eli_*`, `valve_citadel_*`, `valve_ep2_silo`, `valve_ep2_bunker_door` and `valve_prison_teleport_charge` pieces also hold their entities' wiring (keys `wiring`, `outside`, `fired_from_outside`), which `place()` places with the piece (21 November, later; `place(..., wiring=False)` leaves it out; `map_forest.py`'s `pf_entities` does the same with a prefix instead of an ending, as the bunker's silo and door use it): read 'Pieces with their wiring' at the end before using one. (The assets check once called `props_lab/freightelevator.mdl` and `props_interiors/furniture_vanity01a.mdl` missing; `asset_facts.json` is complete since 21 November.)
- **What City 18 does not give:** its cornices, parapet copings and string courses are long single brushes running a whole street front, so none could be cut into a piece; most of its facades are flat walls with windows painted in the texture (`building_template`, `brickwall037a`), and its kerbs are long strips. For those, copy the materials (listed in each note) rather than a piece.

## Pieces with their wiring (Black Mesa East)

The owner, 21 November: "airlock, the turrets, the lift, base work, all of them please lift for use in the future with the modules". These pieces carry their logic as well as their brushes. Besides `solids` and `points`, each JSON has:

- `wiring`: every other entity of the piece (relays, triggers with their brushes, doors, sounds, NPCs, scripted sequences, scenes), with its keys and outputs (`connections`, Valve's comma form `target,input,parameter,delay,times`), origins and brushes relative to the anchor, angles as in the source (turn them with the piece). Props and lights that fire outputs keep theirs in `points[i]["connections"]`.
- `outside`: each name the piece's outputs or keys point at that is not in the piece, where it is in the source map and who names it. `fired_from_outside`: what outside the piece fires into it.
- Moving brushes are also in `solids` as static slabs (`"live"`: their name), so `place()` gives the look. **`place()` places `wiring`** (21 November, later): every name of the piece gets the copy's own ending (in outputs and in any key naming it, such as `parentname`, `target`, `LightningStart`; an output to `name*` goes to each name of the piece it matches), the static slab of each moving brush placed live is left out, outputs and keys naming what the piece does not hold are dropped (`keep_outside=True` keeps them, to wire the copy to things of the builder's own), `!player` becomes `!activator`, every output may fire again, and areaportals and AI nodes stay out. The ending is in the returned piece's `placed_as` (fire `NAME + placed_as`). `keep=` chooses which wiring entities go in; `wiring=False` places the piece as before, slabs and all.
- Lifted with `map_prefab.py extract` and then a scratch script that added the three keys. `extract` keeps them itself since 21 November (later): every entity in the box but props, lights and decals (those go in `points`, with their outputs in `connections`) and the classes in `NOT_WIRED` (areaportals, nodes, cubemaps, overlays, the sun and fog); a moving brush also in `solids` as a slab taken shut (`live`: its name, `wired`: its place in `wiring`); `--wire-box` takes the logic from a bigger box than the brushes. `python3 map_prefab.py check` lifts a small made-up map with logic and places it twice.

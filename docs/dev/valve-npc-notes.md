# How Valve scripts and directs NPCs (23 Valve maps, 21 November; reference)

# Valve's NPC scripting and directing, read for Living City

Measured 21 November from 23 Valve map sources: d1_trainstation_01 to 05 (ts01 to ts05), d1_town_03 (Ravenholm), d2_coast_12, d3_c17_13, d3_citadel_01, sdk_vehicles, d1_canals_01 to 13 (c01 to c13); counts over all 23 unless a map is named. The general wiring (makers R7, counters R8) is in `docs/dev/valve-logic-recipes.md`. Scripts: `scratchpad/npcwork/s*.py`.

## 1. Ambient life

**A busy spot is a hint with one named pose.** 75 `info_node_hint` of hinttype 12 (act busy) in 6 maps (ts01 24, ts02 30, ts03 14, canals 7). `hintactivity` names an entry of the game's actbusy list: ACT_BUSY_QUEUE 27, ACT_BUSY_STAND 10 (Civil Protection posts), ACT_BUSY_SIT_CHAIR and SIT_GROUND 1 each, and one pose per person from map sequences: two plaza sits alternating along the benches (d1_t02_Plaza_Sit01_Idle, Sit02), watching the clock, arms crossed, hands in pockets, clutching a fence, looking out of a window or door (d1_t03_LookOutWindow), a seated drinker (drinker_sit_idle), a couch or bed (laycouch1, sitcouchknees1, d1_t03_*); guards Crouch_idle_smg1_A (4), Idle_harrassidle_A; fear spots cower_Idle, fear_reaction_Idle (5). Bench seats come in pairs 48 to 50 apart, benches 170 to 225 apart (ts02). Posts outnumber the men: 4 plaza posts for 2 cops, 3 at the terminal, 3 on the catwalk; each set is a hint `group`, matched by the unit's `hintgroup` (29 cops) and kept to with `hintlimiting` 1 (19).

**ai_goal_actbusy** (31) gives busy hints to its `actor` (a name, often with a *): `busysearchrange` 512 (13), 256 (5), 8000 or 9999 (7), 64 to 300 with `visibleonly` 1 for guards (6). Mostly the hint is named: `ForceNPCToActBusy` "npc hint [teleport] [$move] [max time]" from triggers, map start (teleport, unseen), relays, makers, scene ends; `$run_protected_all` (arms over the head), `$run_all`, `$plaza_walk_all` set the walk. `OnNPCFinishedBusy` sends the same person to the next hint (ts03: couch, then cower when the raid comes). `OnNPCStartedBusy` adds a speech context (customs_queue:1, terminal_benchsitter:1) so what they say fits the place. Guards drop it: `Deactivate` from OnHearPlayer, OnDamaged, a door's OnOpen (2.5 s later) or a trigger.

**The plaza (ts02, 34 placed NPCs).** Passers-by: one maker, at most 2 alive, never spawned in view (`CriterionVisibility` 1, flags 32 + 128) at `info_npc_spawn_destination` "npc_exit_point" (6, `reusedelay` 1) beside hint 903 (NPC exit) doorways; on spawn `ForceThisNPCToLeave` walks them to another exit, where they vanish; `OnNPCLeft` spawns the next 10 s later. Busy individuals: 4 makers by the exits, one picked by a logic_case; each new citizen takes a free busy hint (range 9999), stays, then leaves by an exit; 10 s later the next comes. Never empty, never crowded, nobody pops in.

**Queues** (ai_goal_actbusy_queue, 2): 14 slots each, 46 to 60 apart (median 50), snaking, each slot with its own facing; the front slot has its own pose (food_queue_front), the rest ACT_BUSY_QUEUE. The front person waits 30 to 60 s (`min_move_time`, `max_move_time`) or a timer moves the line (ts01, every 5 s). A small trigger per slot: a player standing in slot N sends `PlayerStartedBlocking(N)` and the line stops behind him. On leaving: the dispenser animates (`OnNPCStartedLeavingQueue`), the citizen's context switches (food_line:0, plaza_citizen:1 at 5 s), they walk to an exit 92 (ts01) to 427 (ts02) away, `MoveQueueUp` 20 s later; a hidden maker at an exit refills (1 alive, every 4 s). ts01's customs: a counter and a logic_case send each one leaving left or right in a set order; the director points (two short scenes) and the exit guard steps between two police posts to open or bar the exit.

**Citizens** (71): citizentype Default 65, expressiontype Random 67; unarmed 38 (rebels ar2 8, smg1 3, rpg 2); `responsecontext` 27; flags: not commandable (45), alternate collision (35), random head (17), render bounds for sitters (10). **The city is quiet:** ts01 to ts04 each have an `ai_speechfilter` on citizens with `neversayhello` 1 and `idlemodifier` 0 (no greetings, no idle chatter). They react instead: a trigger_multiple round a citizen starts LookAtPlayer.vcd (it pauses on its first event, resumes, and OnCompletion restarts it), cit_lookatcops.vcd, lookatbreencast.vcd; `OnPlayerUse` (19) starts a one-line scene or a "nag" relay.

## 2. Scenes

**scripted_sequence** (223; cops 63, citizens 52, manhacks 14, zombies 11): `m_fMoveTo` run 76, instant 51, none 49, walk 31, custom 11 (pace_all, run_protected_all, sprint_all, Walk_all_HoldBroom), turn only 5. Flags: override AI 64 (186), no interruptions 32 (156), priority 512 (66), loop post idle 256 (54), no teleport at end 128 (45). Pre-idle 49, play 120, post-idle 65, `m_iszNextScript` chains 22. Props follow the animation: `OnScriptEvent01` to 05 (31 lines) open the door at the kick's frame (with a timed fallback 0.95 s after OnBeginSequence) and fire an env_physimpact on the can.
Cop sequences used: Plazalean (wall lean, play and post idle), Plazahalt (halt at a door), spreadwall (frisk), itemhit (knock a can off), ts_luggageShove_All (paired with the citizen's luggageidle), arrestpunch, stopwoman, batonidle1, APCidle, SMGcover, adooridle and adoorkick, adoorknock and adoorenter, buttonfront. Citizens: apcarrestidle, arrestcurious then arrestidle, spreadwallidle, cower, Fear_Reaction then Fear_Reaction_Idle, scaredidle, d1_t01_Interrogation_Idle.

**logic_choreographed_scene** (213; trainyard 92, canals 68, k_lab 29): `busyactor` 1 "wait for the actor to finish" on 122 of the 129 that set it; actors 0 (53 scenes), 1 (115), up to 5; targets are NPCs (citizens 119, cops 31), `info_target` marks to walk to and face (41) and !player (16). A scene stops itself on its first event (`OnTrigger1 > Pause`, 31) and is resumed when the player arrives; `InterjectResponse` (18) gives a bark without stopping the AI; `StartScripting` and `StopScripting` hold actors (24, 9); `onplayerdeath` never set.

**ai_script_conditions** (35) start a moment only when the player can see it: the spot within 128 to 1000, in a 25 to 60 degree cone with line of sight; the luggage shove waits for the player within 500 with the citizen in a 50 degree cone (at most 3 s); `requiredtime` 0.4 to 2.5 s; a 15 s timeout as fallback.

**aiscripted_schedule** (109): goal !player 52 or a path_corner 42; run along a path 30, run to goal 26, set enemy 21, set enemy and run 20; forcestate combat 30; interrupted only by death 42; 51 started by makers' OnSpawnNPC.

**ai_goal_assault** (45; canals 27, c17_13 12, coast 5) with 112 `assault_rallypoint` and 147 `assault_assaultpoint`: one goal, its rally points by wildcard (1 to 6), each man his own rally and assault point; rally to assault median 467 (quartiles 24, 998). Cue: wait for BeginAssault (29) or go at once (16). Assault points: `assaulttimeout` 3 (74) or 0, `assaulttolerance` 36, `clearoncontact` 0, `allowdiversion` 0, flag 1 clear on arrival (55), `nextassaultpoint` chains (six chains of 6: the c01 track cops advance in bounds of 450 to 1000, zigzag 160 across, lanes about 130 apart). Started from OnSpawnNPC or OnAllSpawned: Activate at 0 to 0.2 s, BeginAssault at 0.2 to 0.5 s. Rally OnArrival > SetReadinessHigh (allies); OnAssaultClear > SetCommandable (rebels join the player).
Also: ai_goal_standoff 9, ai_battle_line 5, ai_goal_follow 4, ai_goal_lead 0, ai_ally_manager 9 (3 allies, 2 medics), ai_changehintgroup 6 (15 s after OnFoundPlayer).

## 3. Spawning and waves (beyond R7 and R8)

npc_template_maker 197: 194 start disabled and are driven by `Spawn` (164 inputs) more than `Enable` (66); 1 alive (105) or 5 (51); radius 256; out of view by placement (visibility "don't care" 191) or flag 128 "not while seen" (51); fade corpses 16 (73). Every NPC gets its job at once (0 to 0.5 s): OnSpawnNPC > StartSchedule 46, ApplyRelationship 23, Activate or BeginAssault 19, BeginSequence 11, actbusy 10, BeginRappel 6, flight path 5. `info_npc_spawn_destination` (24): c01a's `renamenpc` names each cop by where he stood.
- c17_13 street battle: soldiers from small makers (1 alive each, flags 144), a wave at a time; rebels from makers with 1000 to 10000 in stock but 1 to 4 alive, topped up by ai_ally_manager.
- Ravenholm: 4 street makers (16 each, 2 to 3 alive, every 5 s) set each zombie's enemy to the player; 6 roof makers send fast zombies on a scripted run over the roofs, most removed at the end; the last ones like Grigori (rank 10) so they go for the player.
- Coast: one antlion maker (4 alive, every 2 s, radius 5000 round the player) over 72 burrow hints (about 132 apart), 67 disabled at start; trigger_onces enable the next set and disable passed ones. Thumpers: a button Enables and Disables; antlion flee hints 1040 to 1325 from the first two.

## 4. Combat direction

- Cover: hint 101 (low, 107) and 100 (medium, 105): c17_13 has 82 cover-low hints about 96 apart (quartiles 56, 128), almost half in named groups (rooftop_hintgroup 19, bldg1_hintgroup 5); the coast 67 cover-medium 117 apart. No pinch, guard or "enemy disadvantage" hints anywhere.
- Relationships (139): hate 68, neutral 43, like 26, fear 2; target !player 47. Zones: trigger_multiple OnStartTouch > ApplyRelationship (27), OnEndTouch > RevertRelationship (14); each new NPC gets its relationship from its maker (23). Cops ignore the player (neutral, rank 99) through staged moments; citizens fear cops (rank 9999, radius 1024) in ts05. Missing on purpose (c01): cops hate a bullseye by the player while he crosses a zone, reverted when he hurts one of them.
- Suppression (coast): soldiers hate an npc_bullseye (health 35) riding a track by the player's cover: fire 4 to 8 s, pause 0.5 to 3 s, only while the player is in the 3072 by 1408 zone.
- npc_enemyfinder (17) aims mounted guns: OnFoundEnemy > func_tank SetTargetEntity, OnLostEnemy > Clear.
- NPC outputs: OnDeath 159, OnDamaged 35, OnPlayerUse 19, OnFoundPlayer 13 (alarm, siren, assault, new hint group), OnStunnedPlayer 12, OnFoundEnemy 8, OnLostEnemy 7, OnHearPlayer 1.
- Gear: cops pistol 89, smg1 80, stunstick 40, pistol out 119 of 152; soldiers smg1 37, ar2 23, shotgun 7. Squads median 3 (soldiers up to 7).

## 5. The Combine at work

- **Police posts** (`ai_goal_police`, 24 in ts01 to ts04): a unit is sent `SetPoliceGoal` (20 lines); `policeradius` 32 to 128 (median about 60); flag 4 "do not leave post" (15), flag 2 knock out past the plane (5). He warns three times (OnFirstWarning, Second, Last) and shoves; beating only while the player stands in a trigger box behind him (80 by 128 to 304 by 348): OnStartTouch > EnableKnockOut, OnEndTouch > DisableKnockOut; OnKnockOut: fade, back outside, reset (ts04: OnStunnedPlayer sets the player's health back to 100: never kills). Warnings move the world: the first has a scanner photograph the offender, the second has the cop play Plazahalt at the door.
- **Pick up the can** (ts02): police post radius 32; scene, then itemhit whose script event knocks the can off (env_physimpact 100); a nag scene every 10 to 20 s; picking it up (OnPhysGunPickup) switches to the "put it in" nag; a trigger in the bin (filter: the can) passes; throwing it, walking off or 120 s fails: EnableKnockOut, beating, fade, a "fail" scene; either way the cop goes back to his busy post.
- **Frisk at a wall** (ts02): two citizens 52 apart in spreadwallidle, a cop 39 behind them plays spreadwall at each (chained scripts), then walks off. **Alley arrest:** one citizen against the APC (apcarrestidle), one on the ground (arrestcurious > arrestidle), two cops on police posts, a scanner photographs the one on the ground. **Shove** (ts01): ts_luggageShove_All on cop and citizen together, started when the player is watching. **Interrogation glimpse** (ts01): a citizen looped in d1_t01_Interrogation_Idle; a player within 150 of the door and the cop shuts its slot (door03_slot, SetAnimation close).
- **Raid** (ts03): residents busy (window, couch, bed, a drinker); one relay starts two door sequences (adooridle > adoorkick, the door unlocked and opened at the kick's event; adoorknock > adoorenter); a node link goes off; police posts hold the stairs; residents run (`$run_protected_all`) to cower and fear hints. The beating is heard, not seen: a one-shot sound on two random timers (0.4 to 1.5 s) for 6 to 8 s while physics explosions throw chairs and tables (2.1 to 7.1 s).
- **Scanners** (39): `InspectTargetPhoto` 15 lines (the player from triggers or at a patrol's third path_corner, a citizen under arrest, a jumbotron), SetFollowTarget 6 with SetDistanceOverride 16 (close inspection).
- **Camera to manhacks** (c07): npc_combine_camera (inner 576, outer 832) OnFoundPlayer > a cop runs to a console (buttonfront) > four manhacks unpack a second apart, each with a glow.
- **Dropship** (c06): crate type 1, three cop templates, `LandTakeCrate(3)` from a path_track, a dust-off info_target per trooper 198 to 288 from the landing, then OnFinishedDropoff > out by another path. Cops rappel (17).

## 6. Navigation

- 4392 ground nodes: nearest neighbour median 96 (quartiles 64, 141); rooms and sewers 66 to 73 (ts03 68, ts05 73, c05 66, c07 72, c11 68), open ground 126 to 141 (coast, c10, c12); about 2 per occupied 256 by 256 cell. prop_door_rotating: nearest node median 65. Our canal `Channel.nodes` (map_ring.py) lays a diagonal lattice about 181 apart (64 cells, odd cells with i + j divisible by 4); the forest nest 160.
- Air nodes 1015 (+79 air hints) where things fly: c07 184, c03 165, c13 104, ts02 78; strider air hints 61 (c17_13); crow fly-to and perch hints 41.
- Hint groups name places (rooms, rooftops, bunkers). info_node_link (14) and info_node_link_controller (9, mostly thin boxes 16 deep, 96 to 208 wide, 32 to 84 high, across a gateway) are switched by the gate itself (func_door_rotating OnFullyOpen > TurnOn, OnClose > TurnOff), by a scene, or turned off to keep units back (ts03 link_prevent_cop_movement). Climb nodes 4 (Ravenholm); jump-override hints 18.

## For Living City

**[game]** gamemode Lua (any entity here can be made at run time with ents.Create, as `MakeSequence` in sv_livingcity.lua does for scripted_sequence). **[map]** our map modules (`_dev/tools/map_*.py`). **[owner]** changes what an existing feature does: ask first. **[new]** new feature or map practice.

1. **More busy poses** (editor points loiter, sit, bench, lean; `BUSY` in sv_livingcity.lua has only sit, sitchair, lean). Named-sequence kinds (entry, loop, exit): window or door watch, arms crossed, hands in pockets, seated drinker (drinker_sit_idle, for npcvices), couch, bed, two consoling, queue idle (ACT_BUSY_QUEUE). New point kinds [game][new]; alternating the two plaza sits and pairing seats on existing benches [game][owner]. Modules could mark the spots [map][new].
2. **Units hold a post at a door** (security: Combine-only zones; checkpoints; garrison). A unit on an `ai_goal_police` (radius about 64, do not leave post) at each Combine-only door warns in his own voice and shoves; the countdown starts only past him, and a person replaces the chat line "This is a restricted area. Leave immediately." (house rule 10). [game][owner]. A facing post spot 32 to 64 in front of each Combine door [map][new].
3. **The Combine at work with Valve's sequences** (cp, patdown, mindsact brutal, street detention). Plazalean for idle units at walls, Plazahalt at a guarded door, spreadwall for a street frisk of two citizens, arrestpunch for brutality, the alley poses for people held in the street, itemhit and a dropped can as a compliance test (pass: Civil Standing; fail: a beating). `LC.PlayGesture` already skips names a model lacks. New moments [game][new]; changed frisk or brutality animations [game][owner].
4. **Raids staged like ts03** (raids: units walk stops and frisk). Two units at the block door (adooridle > adoorkick, the door opened at the kick's event; adoorknock > adoorenter); residents run (run_protected_all) to a hiding spot and cower; a search out of view is heard (one-shot sounds on 0.4 to 1.5 s random timers) and furniture moves. [game][owner]. Block doors with a kick spot and a knock spot outside, a hiding spot or two in each flat [map][new].
5. **Stage it where a player is looking** (mindsact breaks, director "approach", street crime). Wait for a player within 500 to 1000 with the spot in a 30 to 60 degree cone and in sight for 0.5 to 2 s, with a timeout; the act and the voice then carry it and the line ("** Olga Volkov screams in Unit 30's face.") can go (house rule 10). [game][owner].
6. **Guards relax until alarmed** (garrison, checkpoints). Units on post idle (Crouch_idle_smg1_A, Idle_harrassidle_A, Plazalean, batonidle1) and drop it on gunfire, damage or a door opening; 1.5 to 2 posts per unit so they shift between them. [game][owner].
7. **Squads move in lanes** (response level 3, Overwatch landings, the war for ground). Each man his own rally and target spot, then bounds of 450 to 1000. Runtime `ai_goal_assault` or our own `GoTo` lanes. [game][owner]. Named lanes in modules where fights are expected [map][new].
8. **Nodes and cover in our modules** (the navgraph reads the .ain). Ground nodes about 96 apart outdoors, 64 to 72 in rooms, one each side of every door NPCs use (our canal: 181); cover-low hints (101) behind waist-high cover, cover-medium (100) behind chest-high, about 100 apart in named groups; air nodes where scanners fly. [map][new]. Mind the engine's node cap (4096, from memory).
9. **Gates switch their node links** (navgraph learns blocked links only after failures). A thin `info_node_link_controller` across each gate NPCs path through, off if it starts shut, TurnOn from OnFullyOpen and TurnOff from OnClose. [map][new].
10. **Scanners photograph** (ambient scanners follow a random citizen every 30 to 60 s). Now and then `InspectTargetPhoto` on the citizen followed, and on a player a unit warns, stops or wants. [game][owner].
11. **A quieter crowd** (TryChat every 25 to 60 s; barks when players brush past). In ts01 to ts04 citizens never greet or chatter; they look at you, at units, at the screen. A glance instead of a bark. [game][owner].
12. **Ration line** (LineThink is close already). Valve adds: a player standing in a slot holds those behind him; queuers idle in ACT_BUSY_QUEUE; slots 48 to 52 apart, each with its facing. [game][owner].
13. **Pinning fire** (war, rebel ground, Overwatch hunts). Soldiers fire 4 to 8 s at an npc_bullseye beside a rebel's cover, pause 0.5 to 3 s: suppression before a kill. [game][owner].
14. **Infestation touches** (map_raven, infestation spawns). Zombies slumped (slump_a) that rise (slumprise_a2) when hurt or approached; fast zombies glimpsed running over roofs; spawn points that follow the front. Module side [map][new]; spawned zombies [game][owner].

## Check in game first

- `!player` in a goal entity is player 1 in multiplayer (from memory of Source): `ai_goal_police` needs the trespasser's own name as `policetarget`; ai_script_conditions is single-player minded, so test "a player is looking" in Lua (our `LC.IsSeen` is the start).
- A runtime ai_goal_assault or ai_goal_actbusy fights our `SetSchedule` loops: the unit needs a state of its own until OnArrival, OnAssaultClear or OnNPCFinishedBusy.
- Map hints never reach the .ain our navgraph reads; at run time they may be `ai_hint` entities (check `ents.FindByClass("ai_hint")`), else read the BSP's entity lump.
- `Entity:PlayScene` with Valve's LookAtPlayer.vcd may fail where it names !player.

## What did not work

- The .vcd files and the actbusy list are not in the map sources: what a scene says, and each busy entry's timings, are not measured.
- Sequence names come from Valve's maps playing them on that NPC class; `_dev/tools/dl/ValveQCs` has no animation QCs to confirm each model has them.
- Flag and choice meanings (maker 128, busyactor 1, assaultcue 3, interruptability 2) are the FGD's as remembered.

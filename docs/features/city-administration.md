# The city: street furniture, broadcasts, doors, operations, custody

**Street furniture and screens** (the editor's "Street" section): **bins** (searchable, somewhere to leave things, and stops on the sanitation crew's bin runs), **streetlights** (light the street around them), **loudspeakers** (the PA and broadcasts come out of these), a **giant Breencast screen** and a **small TV** (both city screens, like the wall one). Streetlights and loudspeakers break when shot (a unit who sees it makes you wanted for vandalism) or now and then by themselves (`lc_fixture_wear`). A broken streetlight goes dark; near a broken loudspeaker, nobody hears the PA or a broadcast (the banner says so). The maintenance crew's "Lights out" shift and the radio repair crew's "Loudspeakers" shift go to the city's own streetlights and loudspeakers, broken ones first, and fix them; so does the citizens' streetlight work order. With none placed, those shifts work as before.

**Broadcasts and the Director of Propaganda.** The City Administrator faction has a special, **Director of Propaganda** (one at a time), who carries a **microphone**. Left click with it to go on air: for up to `lc_broadcast_seconds` (60) their voice (hold your voice key) and anything they type go out to the whole city, every screen shows CITY BROADCAST with their name, and everyone sees who's speaking. Then the transmitter needs `lc_broadcast_cooldown` minutes (15) before anyone can go live again. A Combine broadcast that runs at least half a minute settles the city: rebel ground spreads more slowly for ten minutes. **Anyone holding the microphone can use it**: it drops when its holder dies, and can be taken off someone whose hands are up or tied (C menu > the person > Take their microphone). From anyone but the Director of Propaganda (or an Administrator) it's a **pirate broadcast**: screens show PIRATE BROADCAST, units are told to find the microphone, and half a minute of it makes rebel ground spread faster and slips sociostability to yellow. The Director of Propaganda gets a new microphone on respawn; searches take one from anyone else. **The Administrator** (the player) carries the same microphone (the owner, 7 November) and goes on air without waiting for the transmitter's cooldown (`lc_broadcast_cooldown`, which everyone else waits for: the Director, a thief, her recorded addresses); her broadcasts still start the cooldown for the rest. Going on air is a left click; then hold your push-to-talk key to speak, or type in chat (for players without a microphone). There's no echo on the voice: Garry's Mod can't put an effect on one player's voice alone.

**The Administrator.** (Dormant: off unless `lc_breen_broadcasts 1`, until there are enough recordings in `administrator/ambient/`; `lc_breencast` still plays one as a test; a player Director of Propaganda is unaffected.) With no player as Director of Propaganda, every `lc_broadcast_cooldown` minutes (15, give or take) the Administrator addresses the city herself: her recordings go out over every loudspeaker (not near a broken one, or one whose district has lost power), and every screen shows CITY BROADCAST: THE ADMINISTRATOR. If the map has a **broadcast studio** (an editor point), she's there in person (Dr. Mossman's model by default), and the camera scanner films her live for every screen. A player Director of Propaganda can cut in on her; nobody else can go on air over her. **Kill her on air** and the transmission dies, her microphone falls to the floor, and the transmitter is free: pick it up for a pirate broadcast. Attacking the Administrator is a serious crime. She's back for the next one. Players set how loud her addresses are for them with `lc_breencast_volume` (0.5); anyone within earshot of her in the studio hears her there instead. `lc_breen_broadcasts 0` turns her off; `lc_breencast` (superadmin) puts her on air now.

**The broadcast camera.** When someone goes on air, a city scanner comes down out of the sky and films them, and **every city screen and TV shows its live picture**, with CITY BROADCAST or PIRATE BROADCAST and the speaker's name over it. It films whoever holds the microphone and nobody else: it can't tell the Director of Propaganda from the rebel who took it off him. It hangs in front of them at head height and keeps their head and shoulders in frame, drifting after them when they move rather than snapping to every step. Its eye blinks red, slowly, like a phone's record light. Shoot it down and the picture dies (the voice carries on). Off air, it flies back up and is gone. `lc_broadcast_camera 0` turns it off; players can set `lc_livecam_fps` (12; 0 turns the picture off for them). The picture reaches every screen wherever you are in the city, not just near the studio.

**The menus.** Press **F1** for the quick menu (nine things on the number keys), **F2** for the Administrator's own (sociostability, a broadcast) and **C** (outside build mode) for the full menu: everything you can do right now, for who you are and what you're looking at. At the top: **Guide / manual**, your pockets (F3), your board (F4 > Jobs) and factions (F4 > Factions). Below that: the person in front of you (recognize, inform, give a work order, cuff, cut ties, drag, search), the door in front of you (rent, pick, hack), yourself (hands up, holster, struggle, surrender, poses, talk to an interrogator, leave a scanner, stuck), getting by (stash, fish, salvage, radio, request, foreman), Civil Protection's tools (backup, 10-20, records, voice lines, BOLO, sociostability) and the Resistance's (clothes, donate, decode, hack). Things that need words (the radio, a request, a BOLO) ask for them in a box. A body in front of you can be searched or hauled. Every chat command still works as a backup. `lc_actions` opens the full menu and `lc_quick` the quick menu, to bind elsewhere.

**Getting through Combine doors.** Keycards and bypass charges open them as before. A Resistance member (Member and up) can also hack one with nothing but time: the **multitool** on a locked Combine door (point it at the door and left click; the C menu and `!hack` work too, with a multitool in your pockets). The Resistance's **Hackers** (a Resistance special, Member and up, two at a time) carry one; anyone can buy one from the black market dealer for 250 credits. It's contraband: a search takes it, twelve seconds, and it's open for thirty before it shuts and locks itself again. A third of the time it trips an alarm ("door breach", marked for units). Rebel NPCs on an operation hack the doors in their way by themselves.

**Hacking vending machines and ration dispensers.** Aim the multitool at a water vending machine or a ration dispenser (one with power) and fire: ten seconds, keep still, and it spits out a random handful, `lc_machine_hack_out` to `lc_machine_hack_max` (5 to 8), cans of water or ration packets onto the floor in front of it: a risky crime, worth the risk. No credits, no coupon, no record. A unit who sees you at it makes you wanted for tampering with Union property. Then it's empty for `lc_machine_hack_cooldown` minutes (10).

**The Resistance's operations.**
- **Prison break.** When one of the Resistance is in a cell, the others get **Break them out** on their Operations board: get to the cells (keycard, bypass charge or `!hack` on the doors), then hold the cell door for twenty seconds while the alarm goes. Units get **Prison break**. The prisoner comes out free, and wanted.
- **Supply runs are watched.** Once the crate is taken on a Resistance supply run, every unit sees SMUGGLER on their screen where the carrier is, refreshed every few seconds, and gets **Intercept the smuggler**. Arresting or killing the carrier stops it. Delivered, it fills the rebel supply crate and everyone on the run gets a box of ammunition.
- **Hijacking a city screen.** Anyone with a multitool wires into a city screen (left click with it, E on it, or the C menu; ten seconds) and for a minute it shows the lambda while the whole city hears the Resistance's message. Units get **Retake the city screen** and cut it with E on the screen. If it runs its minute, rebel ground spreads faster and sociostability slips to yellow for ten minutes.
- **Ambushing an Overwatch landing.** See Overwatch drops below: the Resistance get **Ambush the landing**.
- **Freeing a vortigaunt.** **Free a vortigaunt** (Member and up): a shackled vortigaunt is being worked at a Combine site under guard. Get there, cut its shackles (twelve seconds beside it; a unit who sees you makes you wanted), and lead it home. It stays in rebel territory and charges your powered vest when you ask it (E on it: 25, once every `lc_vort_charge_minutes`, 10; see Powered vests). Up to two at a time.
- **Rebel NPCs answer what the Combine do:** an Overwatch landing or a supply drop gets a team of fighters sent at it `lc_rebel_react` of the time (0.5), straight away, whenever no other fight is on: an ambush on the landing party, or a raid on the supply crate (if they hold it, two loads are gone and the zone's supply crate gets food).
- **Rebel NPCs run operations too** (`lc_rebel_ops`), but sporadically: at most one every `lc_rebel_ops_minutes` (30, give or take), the first well into a session, so the city never turns into a running battle. The Resistance's fighters go out on one by themselves: a raid on a Combine data terminal (if they hold it long enough, every Resistance player sees every unit for five minutes), a prison break when one of the Resistance is in a cell (half the time), a screen hijack, or (as above) an ambush on an Overwatch landing. They hack the Combine doors in their way, the alarm goes off halfway (units get the matching directive), and they have to hold the objective to pull it off. The Resistance hear about it on the radio and can lend a hand; units can stop them.

**Snitches among the citizens (the owner, 10 November).** Some of the city's ordinary citizens (`lc_snitch_chance`, 12%) are secret informants: nothing about them is different, only a flag nobody can see. When one sees a player (never a unit) do something illegal in plain view (a gun out in the open outside rebel territory, or contraband being used: moonshine, a rolled cigarette and the like), they slip off to the nearest Civil Protection unit and tell him; with no unit about, they make the call from a doorway after about 25 seconds. The player is booked ("Reported by a citizen: carrying a gun"), outlined for the units for a minute, and told only that somebody told on them. A snitch killed or caught up in a fight first tells nobody. The same act by the same person isn't seen twice within a minute. `lc_snitches` 0 turns it off.

**Burning an informant (the owner, 10 November).** A Resistance member (Member rank or above) who suspects a citizen of informing can burn them: F2, looking at them, close ("Burn (name) as an informant"; one burning every 20 minutes). Burned for `lc_burn_hours` (2), they show **RAT** over their head to every Resistance player, the quartermaster refuses them ("I don't deal with rats"), the Resistance won't take them in whatever their sympathy, and in rebel territory the rebels slam them against the wall now and then (never below 15 health). If they really were an informant (informed in the last two hours, a Block Warden, or on The Informant's story) the burner earns 15 experience; if not, word spreads that the Resistance burned an innocent: every citizen in the city loses a point of sympathy for them and the burner loses 20 experience, but the mark stays. A burned person who is cuffed, down or has their hands up can be **scarred**: F2, "Scar (name) (mark the rat for good)", five seconds. A crude geometric rat is carved into their right cheek (the owner: "as if they tried to carve a very simplistic geometric rat onto their cheek"): a few red, uneven cuts, a pointed snout, an ear, a lumpy body, two legs and a tail, drawn on their face for everyone to see (where it sits: `lc_ratscar_pos`, forward, right, up from the head and how far it turns to the side, tunable in game), and "A crude rat carved into one cheek." is added to the description strangers see. It's for good: **a scarred character can never join the Resistance** ("The Resistance will never take someone with a rat carved into their face"), burned or not. A new person starts clean. `lc_burn` 0 turns it off.

**Informants.** A citizen can quietly report someone: hold C while looking at them and choose Inform (or `!inform <name>`), once every five minutes. If they really are Resistance, wanted or carrying contraband, units get the tip and see them outlined for a minute, it goes on their record, and the informant is paid 25 credits and some standing. A false report costs standing. But a Resistance member who sees you do it marks you a **traitor** for half an hour: the Resistance see TRAITOR over your head, and their fighters shoot you. `lc_informants 0` turns it off.

**Overwatch drops.** When sociostability stays red (or the city is in unrest) for a minute and a half, Overwatch sends soldiers down (`lc_drop_soldiers`, 4) to a landing zone (the editor's "Point: Overwatch landing zone", or a street), no more than once every `lc_drop_cooldown` minutes (30). They come by **dropship**, the way Half-Life 2 does it: the **troop container** stays in the dropship's claws the whole time. It comes in low over the rooftops, kicks up the dust as it comes down until the container hangs just off the ground, the container's door opens and the soldiers jump down out of it one by one, then the door shuts and it all climbs away together. **When the city is on red** (which is what brings a drop), the first one out is an **Overwatch Elite**, with three soldiers behind him. (Under a roof, where no dropship can come down, they arrive on foot at a run, from out of sight.) Units are told and get **Secure the landing zone**; the Resistance's own fighters go for it half the time (`lc_rebel_react`); Resistance players get a radio warning and **Ambush the landing**: be at the landing zone before the dropship, and bring down the landing party itself (any other soldier doesn't count). The soldiers take up posts round the landing zone and hold it for five minutes, then go. `lc_overwatch_drops 0` turns it off.

**Union foremen.** A Trusted citizen (150 Civil Standing) can become a foreman (C menu > Getting by; again to stop). C menu on another citizen > Give them a work order (or `!assign <name>`) hands them a Union work order (streetlights, rubble, pipes, litter): it goes straight onto their board, and when they finish it the foreman takes a cut of credits and a little standing.

**Custody: what it costs the worst.** A longer sentence isn't much of a deterrent, so the city doesn't just add time. How it treats you depends on how much of an anticitizen you are (Civil Standing, being a hunted fighter, how often you've been brought in, and whether it was for violence):
- **Release.** Units get their issue back. Everyone else walks out without anything the Combine would call a weapon: carried guns, homemade guns in the pockets and any radio stay in the evidence locker (other belongings come back), and everyone gets their fists back.
- **Interrogation.** Rebels get a visit (a Resistance fighter, or anyone hunted as one; a citizen locked up for a fight knows nothing, so is never questioned or crippled): a unit comes into the cell and asks questions, with a stun baton between them (it hurts; it won't kill). The interrogator offers it up front ("If you comply, I will grant you 50 social credits"). Choose Talk (the choice comes up on screen; it's in the C menu too) and it stops: you give up a Resistance hideout, marked for every unit, and gain 50 standing. If you're Resistance, the cell hears you talked and marks you a traitor. Say nothing and take it (Resistance members earn XP for that).
- **The interrogation, question by question (the owner, 10 November).** The interrogator asks three questions, one at a time: where the hideout is, names, and where the weapons and supplies come from. For each the prisoner chooses: **tell the truth** (real intel: a hideout or the black market's place marked for every unit for 5 minutes, or a real Resistance member's name; the first truth pays the 50 he offered, each one after 15, and the Resistance hears they talked), **lie** (a false lead made to sound right; the city's interrogator sees through a lie 35% of the time, 15% more for each one after: two more blows and 2 more minutes in the cell; a lie he swallows goes to the units as if it were true, and earns the liar 10 experience once it's over), **say nothing** (the baton; each silence wears down their resolve, which starts at 3; with none left, silence breaks and the truth comes out), **bargain** once (the truth, for a third off the sentence), or **tell them everything** (as before: a hideout, and it's over). No answer in 12 seconds is silence. The baton never takes anyone under 10 health. Saying nothing (no truths, no lies swallowed) still earns a Resistance fighter 15 experience.
- **A unit asking in person.** A Civil Protection player can run the interrogation themselves: F2 > "Interrogate (name)", close to someone in a cell (anyone in a cell, not only rebels; a citizen who knows nothing says so when they tell the truth). A window lists the three questions, the baton (each blow wears resolve down, at most 8), the answers so far, and Done. The answers come only to that unit, true or false, and nothing says which: they find out by acting on it. Their own interrogation takes over from the city's. Walking well away ends it.
- **Broken feet.** The worse ones also have their feet broken: no running for `lc_cripple_minutes` (15), even after release, even after death or rejoining.
- **Stalkers.** The very worst aren't released at all (`lc_stalkers`): they're taken below and made into stalkers. **Only killers:** the Resistance's own (or anyone hunted as one) who have killed a unit, a soldier or a citizen in this life, or are brought in for murder; a citizen locked up again and again for small things never is. **How it happens:** the cell door opens and two units come in, batons out; a couple of blows, and everything goes black; in the dark, machinery, a long while of it (the machine's whirring plays once through and stops: it used to loop on), and a scream; you come to as one, and your music cuts to the saddest piece there is (`music/hl2_song26.mp3`; the owner, 7 November). Combine property: slow, unarmed (no guns can be picked up), unable to speak (what they type comes out as a wordless scream: their mouths aren't sewn shut; commands still work; E on a person only gets that scream, at 0.3 of full volume: the owner, 7 November, found 0.5 still 40 percent too loud), on the city's work orders, and treated as Combine by everyone: no unit warns, searches, beats or books a stalker, or tells it to pick up a can, and it can be anywhere in the Combine's ground (it wakes there, never at the station). Its papers are stamped STALKER across them. Whatever they were doing (a job, a quest) and whatever they had in their pockets goes with who they were; a stalker doesn't eat, drink or get tired, doesn't sleep (a bed refuses it: "A stalker doesn't sleep"), and has no use for a toilet or a bath (the owner, 7 November); the curfew bar never tells it to report home or shows it a HOME marker; its board (F4) has its own work on it and nothing else; it can't take up another faction (not even as a superadmin) or switch to another character (`!characters`). `lc_unstalker <name>` (superadmin) lets someone off. **There is no way out but death**: no term runs out, and leaving the server doesn't end it. A stalker's death is the end of them, close calls or not (with permanent death off, they come back as a citizen on probation).
  - **Their body.** Stalkers wear a stalker playermodel: set `lc_stalker_model`, or leave it blank and the gamemode picks any installed playermodel with "stalker" in its name or path. Players who join are sent Workshop 268895264 (a stalker playermodel), so subscribe the server to it too. Without one, a stalker looks like a corpse.
  - **The Stalker's Lot.** A story on the board (F4) only for a stalker, worked below in the Combine areas (else the prison, else the checkpoints): processing (an Overwatch technician walks up and fits the harness to your spine while you stand), sweeping the holding floors with a push broom (E on each patch of grime), rewiring two power banks (the circuit-tile board), then building a Combine ball energizer: carry its four containment cells into the frame and weld its four seams (E on each), then its first charge (the board again, a bigger one). Half-Life 2's ball charger is part of its maps, not a model, so the energizer is a Combine generator with the ball glowing in it. At the end you choose: step into the light, and that person is gone for good (a new character, even with `lc_permadeath` 0), or turn away and go back to work, still a stalker. The energizer stays where you built it, humming: walk back to it any time and press E twice to step in after all.

**Radio jammers.** The Resistance lab's scientist makes one from a radio (the Freq 7 kind), 2 scrap and a bottle of chemicals, and the black market sells them (contraband). Set it down from your pockets and it hisses for `lc_jammer_secs` (240). Within `lc_jammer_radius` (1000 units): units can't call a 10-20 or backup or talk on the CP band, what reaches them on it is static, call boxes don't get through, and a scanner's photographs never reach Civil Protection (the pilot's view says SIGNAL JAMMED, and nobody standing inside the jammer's reach can be reported). Units in it see RADIO JAMMED on their HUD. It beeps and blinks: a unit who finds it smashes it with E (15 XP), and anyone can shoot it. Being seen setting one down makes you wanted.

- **The stalker's processing:** a technician (a unit) waits at the waypoint holding the harness (`models/galmastu/prop_tom/male_vest.mdl` if the server has it, else a wrench), turns to the stalker and fits it once they're close; he leaves after.
- **Sweeping:** the push broom is held in front, right way up (`lc_broom_hold`, "34 -6 2 -35 0 0": forward, left, up, pitch, yaw, roll), swings while walking and harder while holding E on grime, and never vanishes. Grime lies along the slope it's on. Every grime spot is checked (on a floor, not in a wall, walkable from the waypoint); after 75 seconds without a sweep the last one or two move near the stalker, so the job can always be finished.

**The office with nobody in it (the owner, 11 November).** With no player as the City Administrator and a key set for the hosted model, the city's own Administrator uses the office herself: events, rations, prices, a job bonus, commendations, censures and pardons, with the same waits and announcements, and no bodyguard orders, housing or permits. `lc_brain_admin` (1), `lc_brain_admin_minutes` (12). The whole of it: `director-and-free-talk.md`.

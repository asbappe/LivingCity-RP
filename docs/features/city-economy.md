# The city: getting by, making things, the Resistance's money

**Getting by.** Press E on any bin or dumpster (props, or the map's own) to go through it: scrap, rags, cleaning chemicals, empty cans and bottles, tins of food, seed packets, and now and then gun parts or a spray can. A searched bin is empty for `lc_scavenge_refill` seconds (600), a fridge for `lc_fridge_refill` (240), and it's only ever the one you're aiming at (not the fridge beside the sink); rebel territory's bins are better. Units don't go through bins. **Fishing:** a fishing line from the Commissary; stand in the canal or look at the water, Fish (F2, or `!fish`), and keep still. Raw fish wants cooking.

**Feeding the Resistance.** Every rebel zone you placed has a **supply crate** and two **pots**. The crate gains a meal every `lc_cache_trickle` minutes (5), up to `lc_cache_max` (12); a Resistance member takes one tin every four minutes, anyone can `!donate` food into it, and a unit who finds it confiscates the lot. A **Supply run** (Resistance job) steals a crate of Union food from the ration dispenser, a checkpoint or the ration line and brings six meals home. Pots take a seed packet (black market, or bins) and ripen in `lc_garden_minutes` (8) into three lots of vegetables; anyone can harvest a ripe pot, yours or not, and units uproot them.

**Graffiti.** A spray can (black market, three for 25) puts the game's own lambda-in-a-circle spray decal on a wall (each player's game paints it; `lc_tag_scale` on the client sets its size, 1 by default; if an install has no lambda spray decals, a drawn lambda stands in), for everyone to see, for `lc_tag_minutes` (60). A unit seeing you do it makes you wanted for vandalism; tags near rebel ground make it spread sooner. Units scrub them off with E for XP, loyal citizens for standing.

**Making things.** By hand, from F2, anywhere: **bandages** (2 rags) and **lockpicks** (4 scrap). That's all. Everything with more to it (ammunition, pistols, shotguns, SMGs, the revolver and its rounds, armour vests, crowbars, smoke bombs, bypass charges, jammers, a radio tuned to Freq 7) comes from **the Resistance lab** if you're Resistance, and from **the black market dealer** if you're anyone else (the dealer now stocks the finished goods). Units make nothing: they're issued it. Gun parts come off dead units, out of bins now and then, from the black market, or from a smuggler.

**The Resistance lab** (editor: "Fixture: Resistance lab"; a layout's old workbenches are labs now). One of Dr. Kleiner's own lab workstations (his monitors and kit, from HL2's `props_lab/workspace` models; a plain desk with his kit on it if those aren't there), and a scientist (Kleiner's model) beside it, the Resistance's own (not a statue, the owner, 7 November: he idles, fiddles with his bench every 20 to 50 seconds, follows whoever is nearest with his head, and reaches for his work when someone deals with him; the ration dispenser already plays its own dispensing animation when it serves). **He decodes Combine data chips**, and he's the only one who does: **Decode it** at the top of his window (or use the chip from your pockets beside him); he tells you if you've only got half a chip. Press E on him: everything he makes, each with its materials and his price. **Use my materials** and he makes it for nothing; **Pay** and it's `lc_lab_price` (60%) of the dealer's price, never less than the quartermaster would pay for it (so there's no money in buying from him to sell on). He takes a few seconds to a few seconds more (stay in the lab, or he hands it all back; with full pockets it lands at your feet), one job at a time. Die while he's at it and the materials are left on his bench for the cell (paid credits died with that life). Move the bench with the physgun and he goes with it. **Only for the Resistance**: anyone else is shown the door. **Every rebel base needs one** for its guns. A unit who finds the scientist shuts the lab down for `lc_lab_shutdown` (600) seconds: he's taken away (30 credits, 15 XP and standing for the unit), the cell hears about it on Freq 7, and nothing is made there until he's back. Quick menu (Resistance) > Resistance > Find the lab marks it on your screen. Each lab has its own scientist: Dr. Kleiner, Eli Vance or Dr. Magnusson, a different one at each. **A new face.** Papers in a dead man's name don't stop a unit who knows your face: the scientist can change it (**Change it**, at the top of his window; `lc_surgery_price`, 300 credits). Twenty seconds under (stay on the table, or he stops and hands your credits back), and you wake with someone else's face, bandaged and unable to run for three minutes. Nobody's looking for that face: your wanted status, any BOLO on you and the scanners' pictures are gone, a wanted notice won't match it, and a Civil Standing below `lc_surgery_standing` (-50) comes back up to it (the records can't put your old name to the face). Get wanted again and they learn the new one. Once every `lc_surgery_minutes` (60).

**Where materials come from.**
- **Bins, dumpsters and fridges:** E to search (scrap, rags, chemicals, now and then gun parts). On a map short of real bins, the editor's "Point: searchable bin" makes any spot one: a rubbish pile, a skip, a crate.
- **Salvage:** hold a crowbar, look right at a piece of junk and press E (or `!salvage`); the message names what you're prying apart. Junk: wrecked cars, vans and trucks, engines, generators, radiators, pipes, tyres, crates, pallets, fridges, washing machines, old computers, from the map's own props or from a "Point: salvage pile" placed in the editor. Six seconds of prying (nine for a car or an engine) gets two or three things: mostly scrap, plus rags and chemicals (cars give more chemicals), and now and then gun parts. Each piece then has nothing left for `lc_salvage_refill` seconds (900). Breaking a crate open, with anything, spills something about a third of the time. **It's loud:** units within earshot get a "metal being pried apart" call with where, and the nearest Civil Protection NPC on the street walks over to look. `lc_salvage 0` turns it off. **In rebel territory, rebels guard their own things:** a rebel who sees a civilian prying at or smashing a crate (the Resistance's supply crate, garden pots, any junk) warns them twice, then shoves them back, then hits them harder, and only if they carry on after that turns on them. Resistance members are never stopped. `lc_rebel_guard 0` turns it off.
- **Downed scanners:** a city scanner shot down comes apart in the street as two or three pieces: always some scrap, sometimes chemicals or gun parts. Reason enough for the Resistance to bring them down. A scanner flown from a Combine console leaves nothing at all when it's shot down, no debris and no scrap (it just winks out in a pop of energy), so nobody can farm them and nobody risks their neck for parts that aren't there. You can tell one apart: **a scanner someone is flying has a glowing red eye** that throws a little red light around it.
- **Union work orders:** citizens' Assignments sometimes include odd jobs that pay in materials: fix a streetlight (scrap and chemicals), clear rubble (2 scrap and rags), patch a leaking pipe, crouched beside it (chemicals, rags and scrap).
- **The black market** sells gun parts, chemicals and crowbars; **dead units** drop gun parts; the **Reclamation Crew** job lets you pocket some scrap; fishing pulls up junk now and then.

**The crowbar is a weapon.** It's contraband: a search always finds one, and a unit (NPC) who sees one in your hand makes you wanted for `lc_crowbar_wanted` seconds (120), outside rebel ground. Keep it holstered on the street and draw it to work.

**Union reclamation points** (editor fixture): press E to hand in scrap metal, empty cans and bottles. Scrap pays 2 credits, cans and bottles 1, and every four things returned is a point of Civil Standing, up to `lc_reclaim_cap` credits (40) an hour. The loyal way to use junk; the Resistance would rather you kept it for ammunition.

**The Resistance's money** (besides raiding supply drops):
- **Intel.** F1 > Resistance > Write down what you see (or `!scout`), with a sheet of paper in your pockets: fifteen seconds near a checkpoint, a unit on his beat, or the Combine's own ground, where no unit sees you doing it. The notes are a report in your pockets (contraband) that only the quartermaster buys: a patrol's beat 20 credits, a checkpoint 25, the Combine's ground 35 (and a little XP). The same place again is old news for `lc_intel_minutes` (30). A unit who sees you at it takes the paper off you and notes your face (10 standing).
- **Skimming the line.** On the factory line, R while holding a good part slips it into your coat: Union machine parts, which the dealer buys for 8 and the quartermaster for 12 (on the ration plant's sealing line, a sealed ration). A unit who sees it makes you wanted, and the foreman counts what goes through: take more than one and he may find the count short at the end of the shift (it comes out of your pay, 2 a part, and your standing, 5 a part).
- **Forged ration cards.** The lab's scientist makes them five at a time (3 paper and 1 chemicals, or credits). The dealer buys them (4 each) and the dispensers take them. Every forged card that turns up (sold to the dealer, or used at a dispenser) heats up the search for the forger: at `lc_forgery_heat` (15) cards, cooling by one every `lc_forgery_cool` minutes (3), a sweep is planned on the rebel territory nearest the lab that made the last batch (Civil Protection get their "Join the sweep" job), the city hears that counterfeit cards are about, and the Resistance are warned on Freq 7.

**The arms trade.** Homemade guns and ammo are contraband items in your pockets (stop-and-search finds them) until you draw or load them. The black market buys them; the Resistance quartermaster pays `lc_quartermaster_arms_bonus` times as much (1.5). Citizens and the Resistance get **Gun run** jobs (carry a crate of guns to the quartermaster, 60 credits) and **Parts pickup** jobs (bring a smuggler's gun parts home). E on the crate breaks it open, and **the supplies** go in your pockets (contraband, two and a half kilos) the whole way.

**Grenades.** Units are issued them by rank (smoke from Metrocop, a flashbang and chlorine gas from Division Leader; the Overwatch get a second gas) and can buy more at the Commissary. Issued grenades are Combine issue: a unit throws them, keeps them or hands them to another unit, but can't sell, drop, stash or pass them to anyone else (they come back with every respawn). The **Grenades** weapon throws them (R switches type). **Smoke** hides anyone inside or behind it from units and rebels for 20 seconds. A **flashbang** whites out and deafens players who see it, and blinds NPCs who see it (or are right beside it) for 4 to 9 seconds each: hands to their eyes, stumbling about, not shooting (they used to stand still for four seconds and fight on). **Chlorine gas** chokes everyone without a mask (`lc_gas_damage` per second, 6) for 18 seconds and drives NPCs out of it; units, Overwatch and anyone in a CP uniform are masked. The Resistance can make smoke bombs.

**Disguises.** Resistance members come back to life in **plain clothes** (`lc_resistance_plainclothes`), with their own face, and change with `!gearup` / `!plainclothes` (or F2). Hunted fighters in plain clothes aren't hunted, until they do something wanted, or a unit sees a gun in their hand. Clothes are loot: dead citizens leave their clothes (20%), dead units their uniform (25%), dead rebels their **Resistance gear** (35%), and bins turn up citizen clothes and Union overalls (and rebel territory's bins, Resistance gear). A stolen **CP uniform** fools units on the street (with the existing risks). **Resistance gear** works the other way: the fighters in rebel territory take you for one of them, though one standing close may ask awkward questions (a blown cover means they open fire without a warning), and units treat anyone but a real unit dressed as a fighter as one. Real Resistance members staring at a fake one see "NOT ONE OF OURS". Changing in front of a unit always gives you away. Disguises now use player models, keeping your face where the clothes allow it.

**Getting people out.** F2 > Give, or `!give <item>`, hands one of something to the person you're looking at. Passing a prisoner something with a guard watching is a crime and it's confiscated. Unseen, a prisoner with a lockpick can work their own cell door (14 seconds, looking at it). If a guard sees, they lose the pick and two more minutes go on the sentence. Combined with the existing keycards and bypass charges, and the Jailbreak job, you can now get people out from either side of the bars.

**A lab can be shot up** (the owner, 7 November). The lab's bench takes `lc_lab_health` (250) damage from anyone who isn't the Resistance's: each quarter blows a part of it (a bang, sparks, a piece of kit gone) and it smokes; at the end it's wrecked, smoking and sparking, and nothing is made, decoded or done there until the Resistance fixes it ("Get the lab running" on their board; jobs and quests). The scientist stays, and tells a rebel why. The unit who wrecks it gets 15 XP and 20 credits. A bench that's only been knocked about still works, and the scientist patches it up himself after `lc_lab_shutdown` seconds without being hit. Taking the scientist away (E on him as a unit) is separate: it shuts the lab until he's back, with nothing to repair.

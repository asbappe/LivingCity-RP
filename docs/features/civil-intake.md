# Civil Intake: arriving with nothing

### Civil Intake: arriving with nothing

**On a map with a Civil Intake desk, a new person arrives with nothing and has to ask for each thing.** That's the first time you join, every time you start over after a permanent death, and a new character slot. You step into the city at an **arrival point** (editor: "Point: arrival"; failing that, the train platform), and a line at the bottom of your screen, with a marker over the terminal, says what's next and how far.

1. **Civil Intake** (editor: "Fixture: Civil Intake (registration)"). Press E and your registration form opens: your name (typed in by you, not handed out; **Suggest a name** offers an ordinary one if you're stuck), your face, and a description for the register, how a stranger would know you (8 to 120 characters; it's what people who don't know you see). There's no "keep what I was given". Submit it to the terminal and it processes you: `lc_intake_secs` (8) seconds with a bar on screen. **Stay beside it**: walk away and it stops (come back and it carries on, without the form again). Then you're on the register, with a citizen number, and your **identity papers** and **ration card** are in your pockets (F3). The screen marks the way to Housing Allocation next (or, on a map with one, to **Labor Assignment** first: see below).
2. **Housing Allocation** (editor: "Fixture: Housing Allocation"). Press E and the terminal's **housing register** opens: every block, how many people live there, and how far it is. Present your papers (you need them on you) and apply for a block, or "any block". It searches the register for as long again, then gives you a room and marks the way there. **Any block** is approved at once (the least crowded). A **crowded block** (two or more above the least crowded) is only granted to someone with Civil Standing 50 or more; otherwise you're put in the least crowded one and told why. On a map without this desk, a room comes with your papers.

**Terminals show their menu on their own screen.** Civil Intake's and Housing Allocation's menus, and the Civil Protection Commissary's unit counter, appear on a screen standing in front of the terminal instead of a window: your crosshair is the cursor (the button under it lights up), E or left click presses it, and long lists have Next page and Previous page. Walk away and it closes. Typing (the registration form) is still a small popup. Anyone who prefers the window: C > Settings > "Terminals' menus" (`lc_screen_ui 0`, each player's own).

**Housed already?** The same screen shows where you live and **how you got it** (allocated here, transferred, assigned when you first arrived, came with your papers, or set by staff), marks the way home, and takes **transfer requests** to another block: Civil Standing 25 or more, once every half hour, and a crowded block needs 50 again.

**Your papers are things you carry.** Everyone on the register has identity papers in their pockets (people who were here before are handed them the next time they spawn). They weigh nothing, take no room, and can't be dropped or handed over (you can stash them at home, at your own risk). **Show your papers** to whoever you're looking at (quick menu: Show them my papers, or F3 > Identity papers > Show): a card pops up on their screen with your name, number and block, and they learn your name. A unit who stops you asks for them: carrying none costs a little standing. Lost them? **Civil Intake replaces them**: E at the desk shows your record and what's missing, `lc_papers_fee` (10) credits for papers.

**Citizens sleep in beds.** At curfew, in a lockdown and at night, citizens walk home to a free bed inside a habitation block ("block" zone: the editor's Fixture: bed, bunk points, or the map's beds), their own block first, lie down on it and sleep till morning, then get up a few at a time. (Half-Life 2's citizens have no lying-down animation, so a still copy of the sleeper, their own model, face and clothes, eyes shut, is laid flat along the bed while they sleep; shoot it and the sleeper wakes. `lc_bed_npc_lift` (9) is how high above the mattress their back lies.) With no bed free they go indoors and vanish as before (`lc_citizens_sleep` 1). Players who sleep (E on a bed) lie down on it, seen by everyone, with the camera at their head on the pillow; Jump gets them up beside the bed. `lc_bed_head_flip` 1 turns everyone on an editor bed round if heads end up at the foot.

**Short rations below Trusted** (the owner: an incentive to be good, or to join the Resistance): a citizen with less than `lc_ration_full_standing` (150) Civil Standing gets a **short ration** from the dispensers, a third less food (31 instead of 45), which cuts it close: birds, headcrabs and the Commissary make up the rest. Trusted citizens and units get the full packet.

**Ration cards are earned by work, never handed out** (the owner: "workers need to WORK!"). Each Union shift you finish gives you `lc_shift_ration_cards` (1) ration card, and each ration at a dispenser takes one card. No shift, no card, no ration (a forged ration card still works). A ration comes with no credits, only the usual point of standing. Civil Intake doesn't issue or replace ration cards; its record screen and your papers show how many you have. Civil Protection units eat at their Commissary and need none.

**One person at a desk at a time:** anyone else gets "Processing another citizen. Wait your turn." New arrivals who come in on the train queue at each desk too, in a line straight out from the screen, and each is processed in turn before they head home (a unit pressing E at a desk sees how many are waiting).

**Until you're registered:** you can't work, so you can't earn a ration card (a forged ration card from the black market still works), the Union won't employ you, and the Commissary wants a number you haven't got. A unit who stops you sends you to Civil Intake; after `lc_intake_grace` (10) minutes it's **Failure to register** and you're wanted. Someone arrested before they register is processed in custody. **Until you have a room,** a curfew finds you waiting it out near the desks or where you arrived: units leave new arrivals there alone. The quick menu's **My papers** says where you stand.

Everyone who was in the city before Civil Intake counts as registered and housed. **A map with no Civil Intake desk works as before** (people are registered and handed a room as they arrive), take the desks away and anyone still waiting is registered, and `lc_intake 0` turns it all off. Editor panel: **Civil Intake**. Staff: `lc_register <name>` (on the register now), `lc_unregister <name>` (back through all of it: no papers, no room, register again). Put a checkpoint point beside the desks and a unit will stand there, checking papers.

**Labor Assignment** (editor: "Fixture: Labor Assignment"), when the map has one, comes between the two: a newly registered citizen is given Union work there, and Housing Allocation only houses citizens with work. It's described in full under Union jobs (union-jobs.md, "Labor Assignment").

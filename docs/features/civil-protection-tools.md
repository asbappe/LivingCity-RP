# Civil Protection's tools

**The unit's quick menu** (F2; the owner, 7 November, moved it off F1, which now has what everyone has) puts what a unit does most on the number keys (voice lines first, they need the fastest answer): 1 Voice lines, 2 Unit tools, 3 Kick, 4 Call for backup, 5 Issue a BOLO, 6 Send my 10-20, and on 7 and 8 search, cuff, escort or take a microphone for whoever you're looking at (or walk a prisoner to the cells), else My directives (F4) and the records terminal. Dragging and uncuffing someone cuffed are on F1, as for anyone. The Administrator's F2: Sociostability and a broadcast.

**Supplies: the Civil Protection Commissary** (editor: "Fixture: Civil Protection Commissary"). Units don't make anything, so F3 has no crafting for them: it says to visit a Civil Protection Commissary, with a button that marks the nearest one on your screen (gone when you get there). Press E there and you're issued what your rank allows: grenades, ammunition for the issued weapons you carry (back to a full load), armour (50; officers 75, Overwatch 100), up to two bandages, and your ration once every `lc_cpsupply_ration` (600) seconds. Once every `lc_cpsupply_cooldown` (120) seconds; then the unit counter opens for anything more. Whatever the wait (7 November): each **ration voucher** you carry is exchanged for a ration packet; once every city day (`lc_day_minutes`) you get **today's kit**, a health vial (on the floor in front of the Commissary) and a bandage, on top of the issue; and **standing at it charges your vest**, `lc_cpsupply_recharge` (5) a second, up to your issue (50, officers 75, Overwatch 100), not with its power out. **The unit counter is the only place units buy** ammunition, grenades, cuffs and suit batteries: the Union Commissary terminals sell citizens' things and nothing else. Citizens are turned away. A city with none placed: the Union Commissary terminal issues units their supplies, and sells them those, instead.

**Unit tools** (C > Unit tools, at the top of the full menu for units; or bind a key to `lc_unittools`) puts every one of these in one window, each button with a line saying what it does: **Dispatch** (call for backup, send your 10-20, issue a BOLO), **Records** (the terminal), **Your mask** (voice lines), **In front of you** (search, cuff, cut ties, drag, escort, take a microphone, for whoever you were looking at when you opened it), **Checkpoints** (a turret, a power bank), **Escorts** (workers waiting for one, and handing yours back), **Post office** (reading the mail), **Sociostability** (officers) and **Your work** (your directives, F4). The full menu's Civil Protection section has the same, as a list. The chat commands below still work as shortcuts.

- **The records terminal** (`!records`, or bind a key to `lc_records`; units only, and a stolen uniform doesn't get you in). Every citizen has a record kept by CID: violations, which the city writes in itself as they happen (wanted, custody, contraband found, escape), notes units add, and their **loyalty points** (Civil Standing). Units can log a violation, add a note, and award or deduct loyalty points: 10 at a time, 25 for officers, once every two minutes per citizen. Records survive restarts (`garrysmod/data/livingcity/records.json`), and people who aren't on can be searched by name or CID. Someone living on **forged papers** has a record under that number instead, and it starts clean.
- **Scanner reports.** When a scanner flown from a Combine console photographs a rebel, a free vortigaunt, the wanted, a BOLO subject, an anticitizen, a zombie or rebel ground, every unit gets the report and the people it caught are outlined in red through walls for a minute (see Flying a scanner, under The city). A unit can fly a scanner from a console too.
- **BOLOs** ("be on the lookout"): issued from a record, or on a description alone. Every unit hears it, and a citizen with one out on them shows it on their nametag to units. They expire after `lc_bolo_minutes` (30). One unit can issue one every 30 seconds (staff any time).
- **Cuffs and walking people along.** `!cuff` (or `!tie`), looking at someone up close, cuffs them in three seconds, if their hands are up (`!handsup`), they're down, a unit has stopped them, or you're behind them. Units carry their own; anyone else needs cuffs (Commissary for units, the black market for everyone else). Tied: no weapons, nothing in your pockets, walking pace. `!drag` takes them by the arm and walks them along with you (`!drag` again lets go); the baton's arrest takes them in, and their things go with them. `!uncuff` (or `!untie`) (instant for a unit, four seconds for anyone else). Left alone and unseen, a tied person can `!struggle` free in 30 seconds (`lc_struggle_seconds`).
- **Sociostability**, city-wide: green (nominal), yellow (marginal: half as many stops again), red (judgment waiver: twice as many, and every metrocop has his pistol out instead of his stick until it's lifted). The city sets it from what's happening (curfew, sweeps, raids, unrest, a skirmish, anyone at alert 2: yellow; a lockdown, an outbreak's infected loose in the streets (only while they're out of the infestation, at most five minutes), anyone at alert 3: red), with the PA and a line in chat when it changes, which says why ("Sociostability: Marginal. (curfew)"). Headcrabs and zombies inside an infestation don't touch it. Only the Administrator sets it (the owner, 7 November: "CP Recruits should not be able to change the sociostability; only the Administrator"; before, any officer, Division Leader and up, and any admin could, which is why a recruit who was a server admin saw it), for a while: F2 > Sociostability, C > The city > Set sociostability, the buttons on the records terminal, or `!socio <green|yellow|red> [minutes]`, `!socio auto`. Anyone else is refused ("Only the Administrator sets sociostability"), and the controls aren't shown to them. Everyone sees it at the top of the screen when it isn't green.
- **The mask.** Units (and anyone in a CP uniform) see the city through the mask's lenses: darker edges, a cold tint, and a readout with their designation, sociostability and the number of BOLOs out. It stays on in build mode, and goes when you change faction. `lc_mask_overlay 0` turns it off for you.
- **Duty allocation.** Units don't queue at the ration line. Every allocation (`lc_ration_interval`, ten minutes) a unit on duty is issued a **ration voucher** and their ration coupons as credits: `lc_cp_coupons` (8) plus one per rank. No packet out of thin air (the owner, 7 November): the voucher is a ration packet at a Civil Protection Commissary or a ration dispenser (E on either). It's the same allocation a dispenser gives, so a unit with no voucher waits for the next. Overwatch don't eat, and get the coupons only.
- **`/request`.** A citizen with a **Civil Protection call box** (was "call box"; Union Commissary, 10 credits) calls Civil Protection to where they are: `/request <what's wrong>`. Units get the message and a marker on screen for two minutes, and it's listed on the terminal; with no units on, the nearest Civil Protection NPC walks over. Once a minute (`lc_request_cooldown`). A false request is a violation a unit can log.
- **Voice lines.** Units speak in the mask's voice: `!vo` (or bind a key to `lc_vo_menu`) for a menu of about fifty lines, `/vo <word>`, or say a line's exact words in chat ("Move along."). Overwatch have their own. The list is in `gamemodes/livingcity/gamemode/modules/shared/sh_livingcity_cp.lua`.

`lc_cp_tools 0` turns all of this off.

**Changes from the 7 November playtest.**
- **Running isn't prison:** caught running the first or second time in an hour, a unit cuffs you, gives you three blows of the baton, laughs and says "All right, you can go." No cell and no standing lost. The third time in an hour is prison as before. Giving yourself up for running goes the same way.
- **No shove for your own:** a unit never shoves the player it is escorting or who called it for backup, and nobody is told to stop running or move back in the infestation zone.
- **Turrets and power banks are the Overwatch's** (and admins'): ordinary units and recruits no longer deploy them, and the buttons are hidden from them.
- **No Scanner Operator role:** any unit flies a scanner from a terminal (F2 > Unit tools).
- **"Freeze"** plays "Don't move!" (the clip it pointed at isn't in the metrocop voice set).
- **Backup joins your squad** for 5 minutes (was 2): chat says as each unit arrives, and they attack whoever you hit or whoever hurts you.
- **Killing a rebel** gives a unit +5 standing, +2 credits and a box of rounds for the gun in hand, with a notice (as well as the XP).
- **Vests at a power bank:** hold E on a live bank to add 15 charge a press. Anyone who isn't a unit commits "Tampering" if a unit sees it; up close, the bank says which applies to you.
- **Prison guards** keep out of cells, the release spot and the prison doors, so a door is never held open by a guard standing in it.

**Arrests in the open** (the owner's playtest, 7 November; `sv_livingcity_arrest.lua`). The Arrest Baton's right click no longer sends anyone straight to a cell.
- **Who:** players (not Civil Protection), citizens, and now **rebels** (NPC rebels used to be refused with "only citizens can be arrested").
- **A struggle first.** A player always gets the chance (not with their hands up, down, or beaten down by all `lc_subdue_hits` blows); a citizen NPC sometimes (`lc_arrest_struggle_chance`, 25 percent, twice that for a sympathizer); a rebel NPC always. For `lc_arrest_struggle_time` seconds (4) both sides press E as fast as they can, with a bar on both screens (the mouse is left alone; E is a press, not a use, while it lasts). The one resisting needs `lc_arrest_struggle_ratio` (1.25) times the officer's presses, plus two, to break free; a tie goes to the officer. Against an NPC the officer plays alone (a rebel fights harder than a citizen). `lc_arrest_struggle 0` turns struggles off.
- **Broken free:** they shove the officer back (the unit's shove, and a moment dazed) and run. A player is wanted for "Resisting arrest"; an NPC runs flat out, straight away from the officer and on to a spot well off on the far side, and the nearest free unit goes after them (not a job's suspect: that one is left for the player to catch). They can't be grabbed again for a couple of seconds.
- **Held:** cuffed where they stand. The officer can walk them in himself (a player: F1 > Drag them along; an NPC: F2 > Walk them to the cells / let go) and at the cell they're locked in; or leave it, and the nearest free unit walks over and walks them to the cells (a player the way a unit walks in anyone it arrests, still counted as the officer's arrest). With no unit free, an NPC prisoner follows the officer. Nobody to come for `lc_arrest_wait` seconds (60), no way to walk there, or a walk taking longer than `lc_march_max`: taken in, as before. `lc_march 0` puts everyone straight in a cell, as before.
- **Units arresting a citizen at an inspection** walk them in too. A rebel in custody has no gun (given back on release). Arrests a job or a story makes off screen (a neighbour picked up by evening, a prisoner to break out) still put them straight in a cell.
- **The Apprehend job:** a failed Authority check, and the suspect shoves you back and runs, the whole way, at a run.

**Changes from the 7 November playtest (part 2).**
- **Backup comes armed to a gunfight:** call for backup with a gun in your hand, shot at in the last 30 seconds, or on a violent job, and the units come with pistols and SMGs (half and half), and keep them in hand while they're with you (calming down after a fight, or Half-Life 2's own weapon choice, used to put the stunstick back in their hand). Dispatch says so: "Weapons free: they're coming with pistols and SMGs." Units already with a player for a job aren't pulled away by someone else's call.
- **My career** (F4 > Career, or C > "My career: rank, XP and the way to the Overwatch", which opens that tab): your rank and the XP to the next one, every rank and what it takes (which need the conditioning), and the way up in plain words: where the Overwatch Candidate story stands for you (waiting on a few more jobs, on your board now, a failed part coming back and when), the Man Inside if it's yours, and that nothing you fail locks you out. Every faction with ranks has the page; the way to the Overwatch is Civil Protection's.

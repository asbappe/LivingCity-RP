# The collapse: sociostability BLACK, the G-Man, and the Administrator's last stand

Built 9 November, as the owner asked: "Black sociostatus means the server is devolving into chaos and Gman intervenes to influence the administrator to do something, like give up or kill herself or resign or something, and if she refuses then he starts interferring to accelerate the end so the administrator is killed by the populace." The boss fight is "in her office in the combine HQ, so a squad of elites would be protecting her and likely a blackout would be needed to prevent her from protecting herself too much in a breen bubble". The heroes: "like star wars battlefield where a 'Hero' is played by one of the soldiers ... Barney introduced as a first, milder hero and if he doesn't succeed within a certain amount of time, then Freeman is brought in". Code: `sv_livingcity_collapse.lua`, `cl_livingcity_collapse.lua`, entities `lc_gman` and `lc_adminpower`. This is the endings setup the G-Man was set aside for: the old sealed directives (`lc_directives`) stay off and untouched.

**Chaos.** The city's chaos fills while sociostability is red, a quarter as fast at yellow, and drains at green. After `lc_black_minutes` (30) of red, net, the city collapses. The sociostability readout on screen shows how close it is ("collapse 45%") once it passes 5%. Not with fewer than `lc_black_min_players` (4) on, during a dream ending, or within `lc_black_cooldown_hours` (3) of the last collapse.

**BLACK.** "BLACK: CIVIL COLLAPSE", purple on screen. Everything red does stays in force underneath (stops, shoot to kill, soldiers out), and it lasts until this is over. Nobody can set it by hand. Overwatch says `socio_black` if there is a recording for it (`sound/livingcity/overwatch/socio_black1.wav`); otherwise the notice only.

**The G-Man, in person (10 November: "not only a window popping up, but the Gman appears before the administrator himself and tries to force the issue").** He steps out in front of the sitting Administrator (`lc_gman`, a real figure in the world that only she is sent: nobody else sees him, and nothing can touch, push, shoot or kill him). She can't move while he talks (about 10 seconds), then his window opens: step down, end it yourself, or refuse. He stays in front of her, turned to her, and presses her at half time and with 30 seconds left. `lc_black_answer_minutes` (5) to answer; no answer is a refusal.
- **Walking away is refusing:** shut his window ("Walk away") or walk more than about 300 units from him and he says "Get back here...": she has 10 seconds to go back and press E on him (looking at him, close), which brings his window back. Not back in time: the same as refusing.
- **Step down:** "A wise choice." They leave the post (a citizen again, off the whitelist). The city calms.
- **End it yourself:** "... As you wish." They die and leave the post. The city calms.
- **Refuse:** "Not for long..." and he walks off. He hastens her end, a step every `lc_black_phase_minutes` (5), coming back to her each time with a line: (1) his weapon crates all over the city (`lc_gman_crates`, 6); (2) people waking from sleep with a weapon in their hands (once each, never Civil Protection); (3) the grid failing, the whole city dark for 3 minutes; (4) the last stand.
- **With no player as the Administrator** (the owner, 11 November: "she should refuse gmans offers at random ... at least 1 time, up to maybe 3"), the city's own has one, two or three refusals in her, at random. She always refuses the first offer. He asks again before his first step and before his second: with refusals left she refuses ("He asked her again. Again she chose ... poorly.") and the step comes; with none left she steps down or ends it herself (even odds), the city calms, and there is no last stand. Three refusals: he stops asking and it goes on to her last stand, as it always did. So the last stand comes about one collapse in three.
- **His voice:** every line is a setting pointing at a clip (Half-Life 2's own for the first two; the rest the owner splices from real G-Man lines, nothing newly recorded), and a `.txt` beside a clip gives its exact words on screen. The full list: `VOICE_LINES.md` (section 3), rows 11 to 23.

**BLACK: curfew and the suppression device (10 November).** For as long as the city is BLACK a mandatory curfew is in force (martial law: put back if anything else replaces it, a lockdown aside; lifted when it ends). **The suppression device** (Half-Life 2's Overwatch Nexus mortar, from Follow Freeman!) shells rebels (the Resistance's players and fighters) who are outside under open sky within `lc_black_suppressor_range` (16000 units, about 1000 feet) of a Combine zone: one shell at a time, a glowing ring fading in on the ground where it will land with Half-Life 2's incoming whistle, then the blast (`lc_black_suppressor_damage` 100, `lc_black_suppressor_radius` 200, `lc_black_suppressor_warn` 2.5 seconds, `lc_black_suppressor_gap` 6 seconds between shells). It's rebuilt in Lua: Half-Life 2's own mortar shells only work wired to a mapper's brushes. Place **"Fixture: the suppression device"** with the editor (the Nexus mortar's own model, `models/props_combine/combine_mortar01a.mdl`) and the shells come from it (a flash and its launch sound); rebels can blow it up (`lc_black_suppressor_health` 800, sparks and smoke as it goes) and the shelling stops till the collapse is over. None placed: the shells come from out of sight. Off: `lc_black_suppressor` 0.

**Announcements (10 November: "voice lines for the announcements, both administrator and overwatch voice").** The collapse has its own triggers, recorded by file name like every other announcement, in either voice (hers with nobody in the post, Overwatch's with a player there): `socio_black` (BLACK, open rebellion and the shelling, all in one: said when it starts and every 5 minutes while it lasts), `black_stand` (her last stand begins), `black_fallen` (she's dead: always Overwatch), `black_crushed` (the uprising crushed), `suppressor_down` (the device destroyed). Suggested words: `VOICE_LINES.md`.

**The last stand (10 November: her shield, her transformers, her guards).** Place **2 or 3 "Her last stand: the Administrator's power"** with the editor (Combine transformers, tinted purple, glowing faintly; in the layout list under "The Administrator's last stand", with her desk), each with its **number**, which says what guards it in her last stand (and only then): **1** a swarm of manhacks; **2** floor turrets, with shield panels falling round her office to carry as a bullet shield and ram the turrets over with; **3** hopper mines (`lc_black_mines` 4) and rollermines (`lc_black_rollermines` 3), with gravity guns left nearby (`lc_black_gravguns` 2). A transformer with no number takes the first kind not yet taken. **The guards scale with the Resistance players on the server when her stand begins** (the owner, 10 November: "8 manhacks if 1-2 resistance players ... if there are 20 ... like 100"; "if there are 20 resistance players, we need about 25 turrets"): manhacks from `lc_black_manhacks` (8) with 1 or 2 to `lc_black_manhacks_max` (100) with 20 or more, turrets from `lc_black_turrets` (4) to `lc_black_turrets_max` (25), in a straight line between (in widening rings round the transformer). The guards never harm one another (the turrets don't shoot down the manhacks). **The shield panels** (`lc_black_panels` 4, one more for every two Resistance players past 8, up to 12) are metal doors, about 56 by 108 units, enough to hide a crouching body behind, made light enough to pick up with E, and unbreakable. **When her bubble comes up**, a flash and an energy jolt (a purple screen flash for everyone near, Half-Life 2's white flash and teleport sounds, a shake), and the panels and gravity guns fall out of the ceiling out of it (each with a spark and a zap, and a clank as it lands), with a big jolt and an electric blast at each transformer as its guards appear. **Sounds throughout** (the owner, 10 November: "we need some sounds too"): her bubble hums while it's up (a force field's loop), each live transformer hums (a power transformer's loop) and goes quiet when destroyed; shots on her bubble ricochet; its pulse cracks; a transformer crackles when hit, blasts at half, explodes at the end; her shield powers down with a sound. Each transformer **faces whoever placed it, and has an unbreakable Combine glass screen on that side**, so nobody can shoot it from the doorway: they have to get round it, past its guards. Her guards can't harm a transformer, and are friendly to the Combine. Each takes `lc_black_power_health` (1250, half what it was) damage.
- **Her place:** within the shield's reach of a transformer: the "Her last stand: the Administrator's desk" point if one is placed there (stood on if it's on the floor, beside the desk if it's on top of it); else **the map's own desk** nearest a transformer, found by itself (the owner, 10 November: "most maps have a desk prop already"): any model with "desk" in its name, Breen's desk first, the map's static props included (read once from the map file's static prop list, since static props aren't entities); else beside the first transformer. Without any transformer placed, the G-Man brings one beside her desk point (removed afterwards); without that either, beside her office door, else the broadcast studio, else a Combine zone.
- **She floats** in her bubble, up to 48 units, lower under a low ceiling so she never reaches it, bobbing a little: she can turn and shoot, not walk. A player Administrator is brought there; otherwise the city's own stands there (`lc_administrator_model`, a pulse rifle). She has `lc_black_admin_health` (600) health and `lc_black_elites` (4) Overwatch Elites (one by the first transformer). `lc_black_rebels` (8) Resistance fighters storm in.
- **Overwatch squads** of `lc_black_squad_size` (4), **each led by an Elite**, come in every `lc_black_squad_gap_few` (100) seconds with 1 or 2 Resistance players attacking her (within about 200 feet of her office) down to every `lc_black_squad_gap_many` (15) with 20 or more, in a straight line between, give or take a tenth, from a path node outside the fight (out of the shield's reach, out of sight, deeper in a Combine zone if there's one; never her side rooms), running in to her.
- **Her shield** (a purple bubble, a faint stream of power running to it from each standing transformer) **deflects every bullet** (sparks and ricochets) while any transformer stands within `lc_black_power_range` (1600 units, about 100 feet) of her, and throws back anyone not Combine who comes within reach every 6 seconds.
- **The transformers** show it's working: sparks and a crackle on every hit, smoke from three quarters, heavier smoke and sparks below half with a small blast as it passes half, a big blast when it goes ("A transformer is down: 1 more stands"). The last one gone, her shield is down for good and she can be killed. Everyone sees the time left, her health, how many transformers stand, and a marker on each ("DESTROY IT (MANHACKS)", or "GUARD IT" for the Combine). Civil Protection and Overwatch can't hurt her or them. Afterwards they're whole again.
- `lc_black_stand_minutes` (15).

**Heroes** (`lc_black_heroes`, on). `lc_black_barney_minutes` (5) into the stand with her still standing, a player of the rebellion comes back in rebel territory as **Barney Calhoun**: the one with the most Civil Protection and Overwatch kills since the collapse began (at least `lc_black_hero_kills`, 2), else any Resistance player. Half the damage taken, half again the damage dealt, a crowbar, pistol, SMG, shotgun, pulse rifle and grenades, armour. `lc_black_freeman_minutes` (5) later, or as soon as Barney falls or leaves, **Gordon Freeman**: a fifth of the damage taken, twice the damage dealt, the whole Half-Life 2 arsenal (crowbar, gravity gun, pistol, .357, SMG, pulse rifle, shotgun, crossbow, grenades, RPG, bugbait), full armour, and he comes straight back in rebel territory when he dies. Each hero rallies every Resistance fighter in the city to her office. The stand runs on for `lc_black_stand_minutes` from Freeman's arrival. The hero's name shows in chat and on screen; afterwards they're themselves again (their name, model, and only what they had). No player to be one: the city's own Barney (`npc_barney`) with more fighters. Models: `lc_black_barney_model` (Garry's Mod's own Barney), `lc_black_freeman_model` (Garry's Mod has no Gordon: a rebel stands in until a Workshop model's path goes here).

**The end.**
- **She falls:** everyone not Combine who was there gets `lc_black_reward` (200) credits, the office is empty, chaos is gone, sociostability goes back to what the city makes it. `lc_black_reset_map` (0): 1 starts the map over a minute later (a fresh city).
- **Time runs out** (heroes off, or Freeman's time up): Overwatch crushes the uprising, she keeps the office, chaos is halved.
- A player Administrator who leaves mid-stand: "fled", the office empty.

**Admins.** The editor's section "The collapse (sociostability BLACK) and the Administrator's last stand" has every setting and a test button for each part: collapse now, his next step, her stand, Barney, Freeman, make me Barney or Freeman, halfway to collapse, back to normal (`lc_collapse_test black | next | stand | barney | freeman | mebarney | mefreeman | chaos | off`).

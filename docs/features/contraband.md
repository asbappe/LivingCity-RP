# Contraband, searches, the underground, and conduct

**Stop and search.** Civil Protection NPCs stop people and frisk them. It happens sometimes at checkpoints, rarely on the street, and constantly during inspection sweeps. Each person has a cooldown, so on a big server it doesn't keep landing on the same few people. When you're stopped, stand still. Walk off and you're wanted for evading inspection. They check your papers, then your pockets. Each illegal item has a chance to slip past (Guile improves it). Anything they find is confiscated, and you lose standing and become wanted. Units (players) search people with the Arrest Baton (reload) or `!search`, and get credits and XP for what they seize. A unit's search is a short **pat-down** (the owner, 7 November): see Civil Protection's tools. Whatever a search would find is spread over four spots (Jacket, Waist, Legs, Boots); only the spots the unit checks give it up, and the person searched may try, once, to slip one thing into a spot already checked (R, 60%). With `lc_jobgames` 0, or if the unit's window can't open, it's the old three-second search. That works on the city's NPC citizens too: press E to stop one, R to pat them down (sympathizers are much likelier to be carrying something), and right click to detain them. A detained citizen is cuffed for a moment and then put straight in a free cell, the same way an arrested player is, since cells are behind doors that lock; they're let out at the release point when their time is up. Getting arrested confiscates everything illegal you were carrying.

**Burning the confiscated contraband** (the owner, 7 November). What units confiscate no longer just vanishes: it goes into the evidence stock (kept in memory only, so a restart or map change empties it). That's what a search finds (by a unit or a security NPC, on a player or on an NPC citizen, and in the pat-down game), what processing takes off someone arrested, what a block raid finds lying about or in a stash, what a sweep finds in someone's home, a busted dealer's stock, a Resistance supply crate and a confiscated letter. A locker's contraband still goes into the searching unit's pockets, as before. After an inspection sweep or a block raid (25 seconds after it ends, with 3 or more items in stock), or once 8 or more have built up (at most one automatic burn in 10 minutes), the city burns it in public:
1. Up to three free Civil Protection NPCs nearby carry it in boxes to the square: in front of a loudspeaker (the PA), else a giant screen, an exercise yard, a ration dispenser, a place people stand about or a patrol point (the busiest one). With no free unit nearby, two come from out of sight, but only while there's room under `lc_npc_budget`, and they go again afterwards. With none at all the pile is made anyway (after 90 seconds at most). With nowhere at all to burn it, nothing is burned and the stock is kept.
2. The pile: each thing lies there as itself (a radio, a gun, papers). The PA calls the citizens to assemble and says what is being burned and where; up to ten citizens nearby come and stand round it; Freq 7 hears where it is. For 20 seconds, before it catches, anyone who isn't a unit can press E on something in the pile and take it back (the whole stack, or one if their pockets can't take it all; a gun one at a time). A unit who sees it (a unit NPC, or a unit player within 900 units with a clear view) makes them wanted for stealing confiscated contraband. Papers like an armband or a letter aren't worth saving.
3. It burns: fire and smoke, about 50 seconds, harmless to anyone standing near. Then the pile and the fire are gone and everyone goes back to what they were doing.

Never during a secret ending or a dream (one starting mid-burn calls it off, and the stock is put back), nor in a curfew or a lockdown. Units see the stock in Unit tools (F2): how many, what, and any burn going on. Admins have "Burn it now" there and in the admin tools (The city), which also show the stock; the console extra is `lc_burn_evidence`.

**Stash.** Eight hidden slots in your own housing block, kept in the furniture: press E on a stove, fridge, dresser, wardrobe or cupboard there and it opens in your pockets (F3) while you stand by it. Nowhere else. A street search doesn't reach it, but a raid on your block can. A Freq 7 radio warns you before a sweep.

**Keeping out of sight.** Crouch to sneak. Crouched, a Civil Protection unit or soldier sees you at half the distance, creeping slowly a little less, and in the dark less again (your own game measures how lit you are, and the server checks it: a flashlight on, or open sky over you in the daytime, is never the dark); and crouched, he doesn't see you behind his back. While you crouch, a bar at the bottom of your screen says how easily you're seen ("A UNIT CAN SEE YOU" when one does), and each unit near you has his sight painted on the ground (red if you're in it; `lc_stealth_cones 0` hides it, your own setting). It counts for everything units notice: thefts, looting a supply drop, a curfew. `lc_stealth 0` turns it off. The sight is shown in 3D: on the ground (up slopes and steps), with see-through walls about waist high and a lid, so it shows from below too.

**Running from the law.** Someone wanted who has hurt nobody is run down and cuffed: units (soldiers too) hold their fire and lay hands on you. The violent (you hurt someone in the last two minutes, are wanted for something violent, have a gun in hand, or are at a sector-wide search or above) get shot at. A curfew or lockdown called while you're wanted, or still hot from the trouble, is about you: the first unit who sees you knows you, and won't stop at a warning. A curfew in the daytime only comes as a punishment for unrest, and says so.

**Black market.** Place a dealer with the editor, or on maps without one, trade in rebel territory (`!market`). The dealer sells contraband and a crowbar, and the finished goods nobody but the Resistance lab makes (ammunition, homemade guns, armour vests, smoke bombs, jammers: everyone who isn't Resistance buys them here), and buys scrap, wedding rings, uniforms and spare papers. **Selling:** the dealer buys nearly everything in your pockets (40% of the cheapest shop price, so there's no profit in reselling; scrap, rings, radios and the like have their own prices) and the weapons you carry, unless your faction issued them to you. There's a "sell all" button for stacks.

**Lockpicks:** besides buying one from the dealer, you can **make one from four pieces of scrap** (F3, "Make pick"; scrap comes from the Reclamation job). Use it by looking at a locked door and typing `!pick`, or bind a key: `bind p lc_pick`. `!pick` also uses a keycard or bypass charge on a Combine door.

The dealer isn't a statue (the owner, 7 November): he shifts between the citizens' idles (another one every 8 to 16 seconds, so none can run out and freeze him), turns his head to whoever is nearest within 300 units, reaches to his rack when someone deals with him, and cowers when a unit takes him. A unit who walks up to the dealer busts it: it vanishes for 15 minutes, the unit gets paid, and Freq 7 hears about it. Edit `gamemodes/livingcity/gamemode/modules/shared/sh_livingcity_shop.lua` for prices and items.

Admin helpers: `lc_giveitem <name> <item> [count]`, `lc_search <name>`, `lc_burn_evidence` (burn the confiscated contraband now).

## Conduct

Every player kill is judged; unjustified ones earn strikes (2: prison, 4: kick), and leaving mid-fight (or wanted, or being chased) is remembered, but only if you chose to leave: a server restart or map change, a crash or dropped connection, or a kick isn't held against anyone. Admins clear someone's strikes from the admin tools (Handy > Clear conduct strikes) or with `lc_pardon <name>`. New spawns get 8 seconds of protection. Logged to `garrysmod/data/livingcity/conduct_log.txt`.

**Changes from the 7 November playtest.**
- **Shops** (black market, quartermaster, Union Commissary) always show the price; when you can't buy, a red note by the name says why ("Not enough credits, 25 short"). Buying or selling keeps the list where you scrolled.
- **The bypass charge** says what it does; for its first 10 seconds the door is checked every half second and opened again if it isn't open, then it stays passable for the rest of its 30 seconds.
- **Cuffs** say plainly who can be cuffed: anyone with cuffs can cuff someone whose hands are up, who is down, who a unit stopped, or from behind.
- **The call box** has a "Call for help" button in your pockets (F3); its tip points there.
- **Pockets:** a refusal now says which limit you hit, with the numbers (large items: 1, or 2 with a satchel; a uniform is a large item), not only "pockets full".
- **The CP disguise** wears the HL:Alyx metrocop playermodel when one is installed or named in `lc_pm_metrocop`; with none, nothing changes.

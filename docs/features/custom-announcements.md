# Custom announcements (Overwatch and the Administrator)

**Custom announcements (Overwatch and the Administrator).** Make your own recordings and drop them in; the city plays them when things happen. No code and no list to edit: **the file's name says when.** They live in the server's own **`garrysmod/sound/livingcity/`** (the official place), outside the gamemode's folder, so updating the gamemode never touches your recordings, in three flat folders beside `endings/`: `overwatch/` (the Combine PA), `administrator/` (the city's Administrator over the loudspeakers) and `radio/`. (The older places, `sound/livingcity/announce/<voice>/`, in the old `livingcity_sound` addon or not, are still read.) Drop the files straight in: a file's name starts with its trigger (`curfew1.wav`, `ambient2.wav`; the longest trigger that fits wins, so `curfew_soon1.wav` is the warning), and for the Administrator `firm`, `stern` or `furious` anywhere in the name is that tone (`curfew_firm1.wav`; no tone word is her warm one). `livingcity_sound/sound/livingcity/announce/<voice>/README.txt` in the project lists each voice's triggers and when they play, and `livingcity_sound/ANNOUNCEMENT_SCRIPTS.md` has every draft line with a file name for it. **The format matters: the file's extension must match what it really is.** An MP3 renamed to `.wav` is silent for everyone (rename it back to `.mp3`); a `.wav` must be 16-bit at 44100, 22050 or 11025 Hz (48000 Hz, 24-bit or 32-bit float WAVs are silent), and an announcement `.mp3` should be 44100 Hz too. Ending songs are played by the game's music player, which takes an `.mp3` or `.ogg` at any rate; `lc_endings` just points out a low-quality copy (under 32000 Hz) that will sound muffled. MP3s are the easy choice, and much smaller for players to download. `lc_announce_clips` lists every file the game can't play and how to fix it, and a superadmin is told on joining when there are any. `.wav`, `.mp3` or `.ogg`, as many as you like: one is picked each time, never the same one twice running. A `.txt` with the same name as a sound (`curfew1.wav` and `curfew1.txt`) is its subtitle, printed in chat as it plays. Triggers: the events (`rations`, `sweep`, `curfew`, `lockdown`, `raid`, `supply`, `ended` for a curfew or lockdown being lifted, and the morning exercise: `jog`, `drill`, `parade`), the two-minute warnings for the routine ones (`rations_soon`, `curfew_soon`; sweeps, raids and supply convoys are surprises and lockdowns answer violence, so they have none), `socio_green`, `socio_yellow`, `socio_red`, `dawn`, `day`, `dusk`, `night`, `outbreak`, `blackout`, `power_restored`, `skirmish_raid`, `skirmish_sweep`, `response`, `breakout`, `overwatch_drop`, `block_raided`, `territory_grew`, `train_arrival`, `welcome` (only for someone who still has to register at Civil Intake, when they join or a new character arrives; never for anyone already registered. Only they hear it, whatever else is playing, the Administrator first and Overwatch straight after her; the city's other announcements skip them until it's done, and wait for nobody) and `ambient` (general announcements, and what the Administrator's periodic addresses are made of when `lc_breen_broadcasts` is 1). A custom Overwatch line replaces the built-in one for an event or a sociostability change (`lc_announce_custom_pct`, 100, is how often); for everything else it's new. The Administrator follows Overwatch once he has finished (never over him) over the loudspeakers (each player's `lc_breencast_volume`). **Her tone escalates.** She is a kindly parent with a dash of the cheerful host, and the worse things get, the more domineering she becomes. Her tone is in the file name, as above (older layouts, a folder per trigger or per tone, still work). Each trigger has a usual tone (rations and the time of day warm, a curfew firm, a lockdown or raid stern, a breakout furious); it goes up a step while the city is on red sociostability, and up again after a run of violence (three raids, skirmishes, breakouts, outbreaks or rebel gains in ten minutes is a step, six is two). If the tone she wants has no recordings she uses the nearest lower one, then the nearest higher. Overwatch has no tones: always cold. Her name is `lc_administrator_name` ("The Administrator"), her studio model `lc_administrator_model` (Dr. Mossman), and Dr. Breen's own stock Breencasts are only used with `lc_administrator_stock_lines 1`. **Every player's own computer plays these, so each one needs the files:** on a dedicated server they're sent on joining with `resource.AddFile`, through FastDL (`sv_downloadurl`), which mirrors the server's `garrysmod/` (so `garrysmod/sound/livingcity/`, never an addon's folder). A player missing a file gets a "Sound missing" notice the first time, and `lc_sound_check` (any player, in the console) counts what their computer has, opens every ending song on it to say whether the game can play it (and how big it is: 0 KB is a download that never finished), lists the stock Half-Life 2 Overwatch lines the city uses and whether this install has each one (a missing loudspeaker version, `..._spkr.wav`, falls back to the plain one if that's there), and test-plays one of the server's own announcements. A song added to the server only reaches players after a FastDL sync, a map change, and their reconnecting. A stock Half-Life 2 line an install doesn't have is skipped quietly (the banner and the words still show). **The radios and the repair bench** use `radio/`, the same way: `static1.wav`, `union1.wav`, `freq7_1.wav` (see `radio/README.txt`). `lc_announce_clips` lists what it found and points out any file that never plays (a typo in the trigger, a retired `_soon` warning, a tone word in Overwatch's folder), `lc_announce_rescan` picks up files added while it runs, `lc_announce_say <overwatch|administrator> <trigger> [warm|firm|stern|furious]` plays one now and `lc_announce_tier <trigger>` says what tone she would use right now (superadmin), `lc_announce_custom 0` turns it all off, `lc_announce_gap` (25 s) and `lc_announce_cat_gap` (90 s) space them out. **One voice for each thing:** whatever happens is announced by Overwatch or by the Administrator, picked at random (`lc_announce_admin_share`, 50: how often it's her), never both; and every voice (Half-Life 2's Overwatch, your Overwatch recordings, the Administrator) waits its turn on each player's game, so two never play at once (how long a line runs is read from the file's own header, and each player's game takes the longer of that and its own reckoning, with a moment's gap after: a length guessed short was a voice talking over the next). The minor ones (the time of day, a train in, a skirmish, the power back) are left out straight after another announcement (`lc_announce_quiet`, 60 seconds), and no "new day" is said while a curfew or lockdown holds everyone indoors.

**Pacing (the owner, 7 November: "constant announcements ... I feel stressed just playing").** Every voice, Overwatch (his recordings or Half-Life 2's) and the Administrator, shares one PA: the next waits till the last has finished. The routine announcements (the time of day, a train, a skirmish, a rebel gain, a block raided, an Overwatch drop, power back, ambient lines, the PA's remarks on a wanted person or a sentence, and sociostability going green or yellow) are only made after `lc_announce_quiet` (now 90, was 60) seconds of quiet; in between they are left out. Events, their warnings, a lockdown, red sociostability, a response squad, a breakout, an outbreak and a blackout are always said. **Half-Life 2's own Overwatch lines are only used for something the server has no recording of its own for** (a response, the PA's remarks and the like now use the server's `response`/`ambient` recordings when there are some). A response squad is announced once in three minutes at most; the PA's remarks once in three minutes (was 45 seconds). Sociostability is announced at most once in two minutes (red, or the Administrator setting it, at once); a change in between is said when the time is up if it still stands, so a flicker from green to yellow and back is never said. The audit, with every source and its timing before and after: `docs/dev/pacing-audit.md`.

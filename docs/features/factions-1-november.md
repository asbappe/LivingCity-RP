# Factions: the 1 November playtest

**The 1 November playtest** (all asked for by the owner):
- **Infestations are no-go for everyone** but the infested: routes (the editor's paths and the map's own) go round them, and nobody takes a job inside one. Someone already inside, or sent there on purpose, still gets in and out.
- **Rebels sleep on the beds you place** (the editor's "Fixture: bed") as well as the map's beds and mattresses. The "Point: bunk" is still useful where there's no bed to put: a spot on the floor to sleep on.
- **Sweeps do the job**: a sweep team member with no rebel in sight takes the lab's scientist into custody, smashes the pots and empties the supply crates (nearest first, one unit each), then patrols.
- **Joining a sweep** (the owner, 7 November: "joined a sweep and no one showed up"): a planned sweep that comes due while another fight is on waits for it (up to five minutes) instead of quietly never coming; one that can't start at all is called off, and whoever took "Join the sweep" is told and the job ends. When it starts, the team first goes to the units on the job (up to a minute), tells them it's with them, and goes in with them.
- **Rebels at home were half as tough** (`lc_rebel_home_hurt` 2); since 9 November (the owner) they're as tough as a player's squad (`lc_rebel_home_hurt` 1, a server still on 2 moves to 1). **Rebels carry SMGs, now and then a shotgun** (never pulse rifles), and since 9 November a few crossbows (`city.md`, "Resistance fighters"); **Combine soldiers carry pulse rifles or shotguns** (never SMGs).
- **Triage**: injuries get worse at different rates (gunshot, stab wound and electrocution critical; head injury, crush, beating, burns and smoke slower; a broken arm or dislocated shoulder stable, never dying). Still three or four patients going downhill, with one stable patient on top, so it's no easier.
- **HUD, top left**: credits and standing always (even with the quiet HUD), and loyalty, sympathy and corruption under them.
- **Changing jobs takes back the old job's tools** (the medic's medkit), unless the new job uses them.
- **A bin you're standing right by** opens in your pockets (F3) even if you're not aiming at it.
- **The faction screen (F4)** is a header for each faction: click one to open it and see its ranks, what it asks of you, and its special roles (yours opens by itself). The Stalker isn't on it (a punishment, never a choice), unless it's what you are.
- **Trust before a gun:** a Resistance member is a Fighter (and up: the ranks that carry arms and wear the gear) only once they've done `lc_fighter_trust_jobs` (3) jobs for the Resistance (anything from their board, not Union shifts) as well as having the XP. Until then they're a Member at most, in their own clothes. The F4 card shows how many.
- **Proving yourself before joining the Resistance** (the owner, 9 November: "we don't want anyone to just walk into the resistance, they need to prove themselves as citizens"; `LC.ResistanceProven`): a citizen needs `lc_resistance_join_sympathy` (5) sympathy before the Resistance takes them (an editor slider; 0 lets anyone in). Otherwise the faction menu just says "The Resistance doesn't trust you yet." and they find out how in roleplay. Sympathy comes from: the quest and job choices that help the Resistance (letting someone go with a warning, delivering the pamphlets and the rest), putting food in the Resistance's supply crate, arms sold to their quartermaster (0.1 an item) or given to him (1 a gift), siding with the vortigaunts in a dream, and (new) handing a ration packet (no other food or water), hand-loaded ammunition, a gun or a grenade to a Resistance Fighter or higher (look at them up close and give it), one sympathy for each different rebel, ever (`LC.ResistanceGift`, PData `lcf_gifted`). No longer: sharing food with a citizen in the talk menu, or being turned away at the Commissary by a restriction. Also in without the sympathy: anyone a story invites (The Pamphleteer's "I'm with you", The Man Inside's "Come over": `LC.ResistanceInvite`), and anyone who has been in the Resistance before (`lcf_res_proven`, set whenever someone joins), so no member is ever locked out or kept from coming back.
- **Strangers at the quartermaster** (the owner, 9 November: selling arms to him "the same as the buying menu, none of that C menu stuff", and "a way to donate ammo and guns to the quartermaster which grants 1 sympathy, and maybe selling grants like .1 each item"): someone not of the Resistance (and not looking like the Combine) who presses E on the quartermaster gets his window, the same one his own buy from, but with nothing to buy: the guns and hand-loaded ammunition they carry, and the materials the fight needs (the owner: "scrap and rags and such": scrap metal, rags, cleaning chemicals, gun parts; `LC.QuartermasterMaterials`), each with "Sell for N" and, below it, "Donate". Selling him arms gives 0.1 sympathy an item, donating one 1 (`LC.QuartermasterDonate`); materials less (the owner: selling junk is a quarter of arms, 0.025 an item; giving it 0.5), gun parts as arms. He won't buy anything else from a stranger. His own people see the same Donate buttons beside their arms (no sympathy for them: "Every bit helps").
- **Units spawn on Combine ground:** a Civil Protection or Overwatch player spawns (and respawns) in one of the editor's Combine zones, or by the CP Commissary if there are none; never the train station.
- **The Civil Protection Commissary** never offers a unit civilian clothes; it has a **clean uniform** (5 credits) for a unit who's soiled themselves (it counts as clean clothes). It no longer sells spare cuffs (a unit's own never run out) or fishing line.
- **Everything you throw is on the Grenades weapon** (R switches between what you carry): smoke, flashbangs, chlorine, and now poison gas bombs and firebombs too (they were only throwable from F3). F3 no longer has a Throw button.
- **The 1 November playtest, round two:** the cleanup job's spray and vacuum are one step now ("Clear 3 growths": spray one, vacuum it, the next, in any order; two steps only counted the last one when done one growth at a time); its guards are soldiers first, and hit the infested four times as hard (`lc_cleanup_guard_damage`, 4); warning posts stand at the nearest path node just outside the infestation; the firebomb burns over a much wider area (400 units across, was 170); the Union Commissary's hint for a unit says it's the citizens' one; the lab's guns are just "Pistol", "Shotgun", "SMG", "Revolver" (no "Homemade"), its descriptions wrap clear of the buttons, and only Dr. Kleiner uses Dr. Kleiner's voice; the prison is guarded by **two Nova Prospekt guards only** (`lc_prison_guards`, 2), with half again a soldier's health, and nobody else is garrisoned in it; a prisoner someone's job is about isn't let out until the job's over; The Man Inside comes off a unit's board once they've killed three rebels in half an hour; a unit can set a knocked-over turret upright (E), and the city's units right one they pass; weeds come up more often and a Groundskeeper's shift marks one; the street sweep's cart stands on the floor.
- **More from the 1 November list:** sleeping rebels wake when a unit comes within about 260 units (110 creeping) and raise the alarm; the hacker's multitool unshackles the city's own shackled vortigaunts too (it only knew players), its text sits low on the screen clear of floating names, and it can hack the **Civil Protection Commissary** (10 seconds, unseen: bandages, pistol rounds, a vest cell, sometimes a smoke grenade; then locked down for `lc_cpsupply_hack_minutes`, 10; seen: wanted); shackled street vortigaunts keep their shackles on, leave the Combine be (and are left be), and sweep when they stand still; a dead vortigaunt leaves a body the morticians collect; **contraband found** by one of the city's units: five seconds to choose **FIGHT** (keep it; wanted for resisting) or **COMPLY** (it's taken; saying nothing complies) (`lc_search_fight_choice`, 1); the cleanup job's clearing step is marked on screen; **This person** in the actions menu, looking at one of the city's people: ask their name, and on rebel ground bring them into the Resistance (Resistance only) or tell them to leave; the editor's number slider shows a coloured tick under each number already used, in that zone kind's colour (a number only groups zones of one kind).
- **The City Administrator** (the owner, 1 November). Elected (by Civil Protection, the Overwatch included, and the Administration; citizens, the Resistance, vortigaunts, stalkers and the infected don't vote, the owner, 2 November): anyone with `lc_admin_hours` (8) played on the server in all (only time not idle counts) can run (C > Me > Run for City Administrator, or /run) while the post is empty (each Records Clerk shift counts as `lc_admin_clerk_minutes`, 20, towards the hours: the Administration path); a vote opens on everyone's screen for `lc_admin_vote_minutes` (2), and the winner is Administrator (staff can still whitelist as before). The **Administrator's tablet**: right click opens the office (each act with its own cooldown): call a city event (a curfew, a ration day, an inspection sweep, a Combine parade; 10 minutes apart), rations generous or austere for an hour, Commissary prices up or down by up to 25% for an hour, a Union job paying half again for an hour, and for a person: commend or censure (15 standing, 5 a day), pardon (once an hour, never for killing), a week's free housing, a curfew permit (3 a day each). **The bodyguard**: `lc_admin_guards` (2) Overwatch Elites always with the Administrator, twice the health and twice the damage; left click with the tablet at anyone and they kill them at once, and anyone who hurts the Administrator is theirs too. **Her orders** (the owner, 8 November; F2 > Command the guards, each kept per Administrator until the server restarts, the one in force marked "(now)"): **Follow distance** Close (about 40 behind her), Medium (about 70 behind, as they always followed: the default) or Far (about 240 behind, giving her room); one each side, and more than two stand in rows instead of on one spot. **When there's trouble**: *As they always have* (the default: whoever hurts her is theirs, and they fight as soldiers do); *Stay with me* (they shoot from their place beside her and are called back the moment they step off it, even after whoever hurt her: they never chase); *Stand between us* (each one stands out between her and a threat, about 110 units from her, a different threat each when there are several, two on one threat side by side; they shoot the hostile ones, and only step in front of someone who's just pointing a gun at her); *Hold fire unless fired on* (they start nothing: whoever they turn on they leave be, until someone hurts her or one of them, and then they all answer; a new order puts back how they felt about everyone else). A threat is anyone within 1000 units of her who hurt her or one of her guards in the last minute, someone wanted and violent, a Resistance fighter with a gun out, the infected, a rebel or monster NPC, or anything the guards already hate; a gun pointed at her from within 800 units counts for standing between. Only the Administrator gives her guards orders; Follow distance and When there's trouble can be given with no guards about, for whoever comes next. They step aside when she walks into them (below).
- **Combine manpower on the tablet** (the owner, 7 November): the office menu has a **Combine manpower** entry with Civil Protection's and Overwatch's reserves, the same lines as F2 > Combine manpower and the unit tools (in reserve, lost today, the next unit or soldier); it isn't there while `lc_manpower` is 0. See the city, Manpower.
- **Tonight's dream** (the owner, 7 November): on the tablet's office menu, the Administrator chooses the city's shared dream by mood (Happy, Neutral, Nightmare) or none; see the secret endings, The Administrator chooses tonight's dream.
- **The Administrator's office** (the owner, 2 November): the editor's "Door: the City Administrator's office" marks its door (in the editor's "by use" view, under Civil Protection and the Combine). Locked to everyone but the Administrator and staff, who open it with E; it locks again behind them. The Administrator's guards are brought to their side when they're stuck (not moving for 4 seconds, more than 250 units behind) as well as when they fall far behind.
- **Idle players:** no input for `lc_afk_minutes` (20): marked AFK (under their name; their time stops counting toward hours, duty, pay); `lc_afk_kick_minutes` (20): kicked (staff never). (Both 20 since 2 November, the owner asked, so a short break never counts as away; a server still on 5 and 15 moves to 20 once.)
- **The Man Inside, part 2:** the contact gives a choice: a forged **transfer order** (walking the prisoner out looks like paperwork; one unit in six who sees you looks closer), **set a fire** at the far end of the block (the guards go to it for 90 seconds), or **a false report** on the Civil Protection net (60 seconds).
- **Stamina** (the owner, 2 November; `lc_stamina` 1): running and jumping tire you. Stamina (out of 100) drains while you run (`lc_stamina_run`, 6 a second) and with each jump (`lc_stamina_jump`, 10), and comes back once you stop (`lc_stamina_regen`, 12 a second, after a second's rest). Run it all out and you're out of breath: you can only walk, and can't jump, until it's back to `lc_stamina_winded` (30). The Overwatch tire at half the rate; the infested, noclip and the endings don't tire anyone (the morning drill does: it makes it harder). A good night's sleep and being fed (hunger above `lc_stamina_fed_hunger`, 30) each bring it back faster: `lc_stamina_rested_bonus` and `lc_stamina_fed_bonus`, 25 percent each. A thin bar low in the middle of the screen shows it while it isn't full (red, "Out of breath", while winded), and standing still you can see yourself breathe, harder when you're tired. The panting you hear when you run out stops as soon as your breath is back to `lc_stamina_winded`, after 8 seconds at the most, and when you die, respawn or change faction (the owner, 7 November, twice: it had looped on until `snd_restart`). The server no longer plays it: each player's own game does, for themselves and anyone panting near them, on one sound handle per person that it stops itself, with a check every second that nothing pants that shouldn't.
- **Guns** (the owner, 2 November; `lc_brandish` 1): a gun is contraband, and the Combine won't have anyone else carrying one. *On the streets* a citizen seen by a unit with a gun in hand, raised or not, is wanted at once (`lc_brandish_wanted`, 180 seconds). *On rebel ground*, for everyone, guns are carried lowered: from the edge in (`lc_zone_mercy` short of it), while a gun is up, a prompt in the middle of the screen says LOWER YOUR WEAPON: hold R, and goes the moment it's down. While it's up, the fighters who can see you stand to and face you, guns on you, and warn you over the ten seconds in Half-Life 2's own voices ("Heads up!", "Watch what you're doing!", subtitles on screen); still up after `lc_brandish_grace` (10) seconds once you're really in, they open fire. *On the Combine's ground* the same goes for Civil Protection's recruits and metrocops (they haven't had the Overwatch's conditioning, and the Combine don't wholly trust them), with the Overwatch's soldiers and their radio voices ("Prepare for contact"); not for the Overwatch ranks or Division Leaders. Only players are ever taken for it: the city's NPCs carry their own way.
- **Obituaries** (`lc_obituary` 1): when a player character dies, everyone sees it in chat: "[Obituary] Name, citizen #12345, was killed by Civil Protection." (not during an ending).
- **Citizen numbers in admin commands:** anything that takes a player's name also takes their citizen number (`#12345` or `12345`) or their character's name.
- **Colour grading** (`lc_color_grade` 1): the city's colours a touch drained and cold, Half-Life 2's grey-green. Each player can turn theirs off: `lc_my_color_grade 0`.
- **Guns carried lowered** (`lc_lowered_weapons`, 1; only on rebel ground, and for recruits and metrocops on the Combine's, the owner, 2 November: everywhere else a gun is simply ready): there, a gun drawn comes out pointed down, no crosshair, and won't fire; hold R to raise it (and again to lower it); a left click while it's down raises it instead of firing; tapping R still reloads. Half-Life 2's own lowered animation where the gun has one (the anniversary update's), tipped down otherwise.
- **Things wear out** (`lc_wear_uses`, 10): a lockpick, a fishing line or a canister of weedkiller lasts about ten uses (a Groundskeeper's or a cleanup worker's weedkiller is topped up by the job, so they never run dry). **A legacy that shows**: a player with many lives behind them has a title under their name (3: Survivor, 6: Old hand, 10: Ghost of the old city, 20: Legend of the canals), and each new person after the first comes with the last one's ring (a keepsake). Never power or money.
- **Out of the way:** walk into one of the city's people (a unit or a citizen) and they step aside, a shuffle at once and then a few steps clear, unless they're busy with someone (a stop, a chase, an escort, a guard, asleep, in a cell). `lc_npc_make_way 0` turns it off. **The Combine's own only make way for their own side** (the owner, 8 November: "only for each other (players of the same faction) and not for players of a different faction (not for rebels, citizens, etc)"): a Metrocop NPC or an Overwatch soldier steps aside for Civil Protection players (the Overwatch ranks included: they're Civil Protection's career) and for the City Administrator; the Administrator's Elites for her and for Civil Protection, and then go back to their place beside her. None of them for a citizen, a rebel, a vortigaunt, a stalker or the infected, and never in the middle of a fight (with an enemy, after someone, or after whoever she ordered killed), escorting a prisoner, or on a march. The city's citizens still make way for anyone.
- **Lost a rank weapon?** The Civil Protection Commissary issues again any weapon your rank carries that you no longer have (taken in custody, a scene, a test). A Metrocop's pistol, for one.
- **Terminal screens** stand with their middle a hair below your eyes (they were at hip height), and the Next page / Close buttons light up when aimed at, like the rest.
- **Job items where you can reach them:** anything a job hides or drops (belongings to find, evidence, parcels) only goes where people walk: near a path node on the same floor, in plain sight of it, with room to stand over it. Never under stairs or in space the map walls off; a spot that fails is skipped, and an item that would land in one is moved to the nearest walkable spot.
- **The radio transmitter** sits higher in your view now (it was barely on screen); `lc_radio_view "forward right up"` (20 9 -3) moves it, each player's own.
- **Metrocop hands:** Garry's Mod has no metrocop arms of its own (its police model wears the Overwatch soldier's sleeves), and there's no pack for them. So a unit's own body is their first-person hands, everything but the arms and hands shrunk away: their own sleeves and gloves (`lc_cp_hands own`, the default; a hands model path for a pack's instead; empty for Garry's Mod's). The Overwatch ranks keep Garry's Mod's soldier arms, which are theirs.
- **Career paths** (the owner, 1 November). The board (F4) groups Union jobs: the **Civil Protection path** (Intake Processor, Infestation Cleanup), the **Administration path** (Records Clerk, Block Warden), and the rest of Union work. **Joining Civil Protection** needs Civil Standing 100 (`lc_cp_min_standing`) and `lc_cp_clerk_shifts` (5; the setting kept its name) finished shifts as an **Intake Processor** (an Infestation Cleanup shift counts as two). Records Clerk shifts no longer count towards it (they did until 1 November). The faction screen (F4) shows both as progress bars on Civil Protection's card. Admins are exempt; units already in Civil Protection keep their place.
- **No Union radio**: every radio that isn't Civil Protection's is a **Freq 7 radio** (contraband; the black market sells them), and the Resistance lab makes the **radio transmitter** from one (plus 2 scrap). An old Union radio still in someone's pockets becomes a Freq 7 radio.
- **Vortigaunts**: `lc_vort_slaves` (2) shackled vortigaunts sweep the streets, Combine property (they harm nobody and nobody harms them), coming in out of sight. A Resistance Member or above can cut one loose like a player vortigaunt (look at it up close, `!unshackle` or the Resistance menu, ten seconds, no unit watching): it heads for rebel ground and lives there free, charging vests. Vortigaunt players (shackled or free) have no fists: there's no vortigaunt version of them. The debug report now says how many freed vortigaunts live in rebel ground and why none came (`lc_rebel_vorts` 0, or no rebel zones).
- **Secret endings**: three hours between them (`lc_ending_cooldown` 180; a server still on 60 or 45 moves to 180 once).
- **She, not he**: a job's steps, narration and choices say she and her when the person the job is about is a woman (a sentence about a unit, a guard or a father is left alone).
- **Morning exercise**: on the jog the Civil Protection units shout "Move it!" and "Keep moving!" (not "First warning, move away"); finishing pays `lc_exercise_pay` (5) credits on top of the standing, said on screen; skipping costs 10 standing and your next ration (`lc_exercise_skip_ration` 1), with a reprimand on screen.

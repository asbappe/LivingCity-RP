# Factions and ranks

**Switching faction from the factions menu (F4) keeps you where you're standing**: the new uniform and kit go on right there, instead of sending you back to a spawn point. (A new character, another of your characters, or an admin setting your faction still starts you at a spawn point.)

**Joining Civil Protection needs good standing**: 100 or more Civil Standing (`lc_cp_min_standing`), so a new character has to earn their way in with jobs and compliance first. Admins can join regardless, to test, and `lcf_setfaction <name> <faction>` sets anyone's faction directly.

**The sides kept even** (the owner, 9 November: "keep the sides even"; `LC.SidesEven`, `LC.SideRatio` 2, `LC.SideSlack` 4): neither the Resistance nor the Combine (Civil Protection and Overwatch together) may grow past double the other side's players online, plus 4. So with 6 Combine players the Resistance takes up to 16; with none, up to 4. Only joining waits (F4 says why); nobody already on a side is ever put off it, and someone who drops out (a crash, a reboot, a power flicker) and comes back within 7 minutes (`LC.SideGrace`) is back on the side they were on, whatever the count, as is everyone in the first 7 minutes after the map starts (a restart); after longer they join like anyone, as a citizen meanwhile, told why, rank and experience kept; so if Combine players leave, the Resistance players online stay where they are, and the city's NPC Combine grows to meet them (the reserves, below in `city.md`). Citizens, the Administration and the rest are never limited; superadmins aren't either. A caught mole forced into the Resistance, and a freed vortigaunt, join whatever the count.

| Faction | Ladder |
|---|---|
| Citizen | Citizen, Union Worker, Loyalist, Model Citizen |
| Resistance | Sympathizer, Member (pass for citizens), Fighter, Lieutenant, Commander (hunted outside rebel zones) |
| Civil Protection | Recruit, Metrocop, Division Leader, then the Overwatch: Combine Prison Guard, Combine Soldier, Elite Soldier. One Elite can be the **Overwatch Commander** (F4). |
| City Administrator | whitelist only |
| Infested | Zombie, Fast Zombie, Zombine (claws only: no guns; the Zombie and Zombine are no faster than a citizen; joining from F4 puts you in a nest; what they kill isn't murder, so no wanted and no response) |

**One career, from the records office to the Overwatch.** Civil Protection is a single ladder: XP takes you from Recruit (a baton, a desk) to Metrocop (a pistol) to Division Leader (an SMG, and an officer: sociostability, power banks, city-wide broadcasts). Past that are the Overwatch ranks, and they need the **transhuman conditioning** first: the Overwatch Candidate story comes to a Division Leader (F4), and at its end a technician offers it. Accept, and the Overwatch ranks open at once (your XP already counts: enough of it and you go straight to Combine Soldier or Elite). Decline, and you keep your name, and stay a Division Leader for good (XP still counts, the bar says why it stops). Overwatch ranks don't eat, don't get stopped, have their own voice lines, and their own board of Protocols alongside Civil Protection's. **The Overwatch Commander** (one at a time, from the Elite: F4) calls in an Overwatch squad by dropship to the landing zone nearest them, once every fifteen minutes (F2 > Unit tools, "Call in a squad"). Admins promoting someone (`!setrank`, `lcf_promote`) skip the story. **The Scanner Operator** (two at a time, any rank: F4) flies a city scanner from a Combine console, a data terminal, or anywhere in the Nexus (a map with none of those: anywhere), from F2 > Unit tools. Their reports earn XP (up to `lc_scanner_snitch_cap` every ten minutes), and a rebel they photograph goes out to every unit as a **BOLO** for half an hour; anyone else they catch is reported as usual, without one.

**Half-Life: Alyx-style Combine.** Install a Combine model pack and the Overwatch wear it, players in the Overwatch ranks and the city's own soldiers alike; nothing to set up. The first choice is Workshop 3449180606 (soldier, shotgunner, prison guard, shotgunner prison guard and Elite), the fallback Workshop 2041528484 (a soldier and the red prison guard). Which model is which comes from its name and path, and players who join are sent whichever of the two the server has mounted. Metrocops stay Half-Life 2's. `lc_combine_models_list` (admin) shows what was found; `lc_pm_<kind>` and `lc_npcmodel_<kind>` (soldier, shotgunner, guard, guard_shotgun, elite) name a model by hand; `lc_combine_models 0` keeps Half-Life 2's.

**Squads.** Units hunting the same person move as one squad (`lc_squads`): the one nearest the target leads, the rest keep with him (running to catch up, walking once close) and, once with him, stand watch facing open ground instead of a wall. Whoever sees the target fights; one hurt while running stops and fights back. Soldiers call it on the radio (contact, lost contact), and they share Half-Life 2's own squad, so its tactics (taking turns to shoot, flanking) work too. Soldiers holding a landing zone face out the same way.

**The kick.** Every Civil Protection rank can kick: G (`lc_kick_key`), or F2 > Kick (3). A unit's F2 (the owner, 7 November; on F1 until the keys changed that day): 1 Voice lines, 2 **Unit tools** (everything a unit has), 3 Kick, 4 backup, 5 a BOLO, 6 their 10-20; what's in front of you, then the usual ones, from 7. Facing a locked door a kick would open, close up, a unit is reminded on screen how: "Locked. To kick it in: press G (or F2 > 3)", with their own kick keys and wherever Kick is in F2 at the time (the owner, 2 November). An unlocked door bangs open, fast, away from you; a locked one gives on the second kick (`lc_kick_door_kicks`, 2) and an apartment's tenants are told; a Combine door opens; a prison door or a "locked" door doesn't give. Someone kicked is knocked back and hurt a little (`lc_kick_damage`, 12); something loose goes flying, and a boot breaks crates, planks and anything breakable the way a crowbar does. **The Overwatch's boots are heavier:** a Combine Soldier kicks an ordinary hinged door off its hinges and it falls over where it hung; an Elite sends it flying into the room, five to ten feet (never the same twice), and whoever it hits is **knocked out** (15 seconds of black, then the room swims; never a death or a close call, so kicking doors in during a curfew can't kill a block); a door falling over only leaves you dizzy; the Overwatch Commander, eight to fourteen (`lc_kick_door_power`, 1, scales the flight; 0 turns it off). Brush doors (a sliding warehouse door, a Combine shutter, a gate) don't budge for anyone's boot. **Rehanging it:** a Maintenance Technician presses E on the fallen door to lift it, carries it back to its frame (marked on screen) and presses E there (8 seconds; `lc_rehang_pay`, `lc_combine_models`, `lc_outfits`, `lc_people_heights`, `lc_citizen_tint`, `lc_squads`, 10 credits); the city's own maintenance crews come and do it if no player has. Failing all that it's back by itself after `lc_kick_door_back` seconds (600). Everyone sees the leg come up, on any model (the thigh and knee are bent in code), and in first person you see your own leg, so it's always the right boot. `lc_kick 0` turns it off. In first person only your legs show during the kick: every bone but the hips and the kicking leg is shrunk, so the mask, head and arms (and the HL:Alyx metrocop's extra pieces) stay out of view (the owner, 7 November).

**The Smuggler** (Resistance, two at a time, Member and up: F4) moves the cell's cargo: smuggling runs on their board (F4), collected from the quartermaster and taken to three drops in the city against the clock. No questions, no sender: they know it's guns and ammunition, and so does any unit whose search finds it. Better paid than a courier's run (6 a drop, 10 more for getting it all through, and XP). A courier who knowingly carries a hot parcel is a one-off; smuggling is the Resistance's trade.

**Special roles** (Hacker, Smuggler, Father Grigori, Free Vortigaunt, Director of Propaganda) are held on top of your rank, not instead of it: a Fighter who takes up the Hacker's role is still a Fighter, keeps earning XP toward Lieutenant, and carries the role's kit (the multitool) instead of the rank's while holding it; Join the faction again from F4 to give it up and go back to the rank's kit. F4 says so under each role, and your HUD shows both ("Hacker (Fighter)"). They come back with you: leave and rejoin and you're still the Hacker, as long as there's still a place. Nobody holds a place by being offline, so if the places filled up while you were away you're back to your rank, and told so; take it again from F4 when one opens. Whitelisted factions come back too, while you're still whitelisted.

**Powered vests (what HL2 calls suit power).** Nobody in the city wears an HEV suit: the armour number is the charge in a **powered vest**, kevlar plates with charge cells, and you hold only as much as your vest takes (the VEST bar bottom left, in place of HL2's SUIT). Combine units have one built into the uniform (their rank's armour is its capacity, and the Civil Protection Commissary tops it up). The Resistance's ranks come with one (Fighter 25, Lieutenant 50, Commander 75). Anyone else holds no charge at all until they put one on: the **powered vest** is made in the Resistance lab (or sold on the black market), holds `lc_vest_capacity` (75) and comes with 30 in it; Put on, from your pockets. It's lost when you die. Only these charge it (sleeping doesn't): suit batteries and chargers, the Commissary for units, **charge cells** (Charge your vest, from your pockets: 25; the Resistance lab makes them from 2 scrap and 2 chemicals, and a dead Combine unit drops one `lc_vest_cell_drop` (25%) of the time, a downed scanner twice as often), and **vortigaunts**: E on a freed one in rebel territory and it charges you (25, once every `lc_vort_charge_minutes`, 10, not by itself while you stand or sleep beside it), and a vortigaunt player's right click charges whoever they look at.

**Father Grigori, the man himself.** While no player is Father Grigori, the NPC keeps to a rebel territory with Annabelle (the largest at first), muttering scripture; every `lc_grigori_move_minutes` (12) or so he moves on to another of the Resistance's camps, when nobody's near enough to see him go (0: he stays put). E on him: **Ask for his blessing** (25 health, and blessed for five minutes: you take 15% less harm; once every five minutes each), **Ask him to preach** (he rants, and every Resistance member within earshot is emboldened for ten minutes: their shots hit the Combine 10% harder, and a little XP; once every ten minutes), or **Talk to him** (anyone). Only the Resistance get his blessing and his sermons; a unit who walks up gets a promise of Annabelle. Killed, he's back after `lc_grigori_respawn` minutes (10); `lc_grigori 0` turns him off.

**Father Grigori** (the city's own, in rebel territory) says something once a minute at most (`lc_grigori_talk_gap`, 60), and none of the engine's idle chatter. As a player, Father Grigori is a special Resistance role: his shotgun is Annabelle, and holds **two shells** at a time (the rest stay in his pockets); The Blessing heals, preaching draws Civil Protection, and he's hunted on sight outside rebel territory. Edit `gamemodes/livingcity/gamemode/modules/shared/sh_livingcity_factions.lua` to rename ranks, change XP, models and loadouts.

**Changes from the 7 November playtest.** A special role's minimum rank now holds for superadmins too (that is how a Recruit became Overwatch Commander), and Overwatch roles need the conditioning; going back down from the Overwatch was already impossible. The Scanner Operator role is gone: any unit flies a scanner from a terminal. Turrets and power banks are the Overwatch's alone.

**Changes from the 7 November playtest (part 2).** C > **My career** (now F4 > Career) shows the whole ladder and the way to the Overwatch (civil-protection-tools). **A reassessment:** a Division Leader who turned the conditioning down can, an hour later, ask Overwatch for a reassessment from that page: the Overwatch Candidate story comes back to their board from its first part, and passing it offers the conditioning again (it used to be "a Division Leader for good").

**Changes from the 11 November playtest.** **The conditioning is only offered with the experience for it:** the Overwatch Candidate story comes to a Division Leader's board only once they have the XP of the first Overwatch rank (Combine Prison Guard, 1200); F4 > Career says how much is missing. No experience is given by it. **At the spot a soldier and a stalker wait,** and the technician's choice only comes when you have walked right up to them (within 90 units; it used to come up from 300 away, through walls and floors). **Accepting is one step:** the screen fades to black for six and a half seconds with machine sounds, you are held still, and you come out of it Overwatch with the new rank, uniform and weapons, and a notice saying so.

## 14 November (the owner's 13 November playtest)

- **Who may take a Resistance role:** Smuggler from Member, Hacker from Fighter, Father Grigori from Lieutenant (it was Member for all three). The Free Vortigaunt is still from Member until vortigaunts are chosen at the start (`TODO.md`).
- **Guns on rebel ground:** Civil Protection and Overwatch players no longer carry lowered there.
- **The infested don't hurt each other:** one infested player's blow on another does no damage and shoves them a step away.

**The Resistance's medic (the owner, 14 November).** One in each rebel territory (touching zones are one), in the medic's clothes, keeping his post like the quartermaster (never follows, squads up, raids or runs errands). E on him (anyone the Resistance doesn't take for the Combine), hurt: a quick menu, a small health vial (10 health, 3 credits) or a large medkit (40 health, 8 credits); paid, he says "I hate to charge, but these don't grow on trees." Every few seconds he tends one hurt friendly NPC within 500 units (the Resistance's people, vortigaunts, citizens on rebel ground) for 25, free. Killed, another comes after 5 minutes. `lc_rebel_medic` (1); sv_livingcity_rebelmedic.lua, cl_livingcity_rebelmedic.lua.

**A powered vest goes on fully charged** (the owner, 14 November: "it needs to be fully charged when I receive it and put it on"), to its capacity (`lc_vest_capacity`, 75).

**Carried lowered, in your own view (the owner, 14 November: "no viewmodel difference").** A lowered gun now tips down smoothly in first person (`LCL.LowerViewModel`, sh_livingcity_lowered.lua), in a work suit too; Half-Life 2's lowered animation was hidden by the server's own idle.

**The Resistance's scientist (the owner, 14 November).** He reaches out to hand something over only when he does (what you bought, or what he kept for you: `ENT:HandOver`), not for being spoken to, and doesn't fiddle with his bench with someone at his elbow. His eyes are open, follow whoever is near, and blink (no more squint); more idles are tried (`LineIdle03`, `LineIdle04`, relaxed ones) where the model has them.

**Prison guards: four jobs, then a soldier (the owner, 14 November: "make it so you can be promoted from prison guard to combine soldier after 4 successful quests").** A Combine Prison Guard's board (F4) has four jobs while the map has prison cells: Cell inspection, Question a prisoner (someone held in the cells), A break for it (catch and arrest an escaping prisoner) and Watch over the cells. The fourth done makes them a Combine Soldier: the experience still owed for that rank comes with it, so the ranks' own experience is unchanged. sv_livingcity_prisonguard.lua (`LC.PrisonGuardJobDone`, `LC.PrisonGuardJobsToPromote` 4).

**The infested, a hidden side (the owner's 13 November playtest; built 14 November).** Not on F4's Factions (but for whoever is one) and never joined by choice (an admin can still set it with `lcf_setfaction`). Put down by a zombie, a headcrab or an infested player inside an infestation zone, you're turning; got up, it passes; bled out or given up, no close call is used and you come back as one of the infested, in their nest. Killed while infested, the character is dead: no close call (the owner, 14 November: "if you die as an infested your character is dead; no close calls"); the next life is a new person. Vortigaunts and stalkers never turn. sv_livingcity_infestedside.lua.

**Lamarr (the owner, 14 November: a secret pet).** When Dr. Kleiner runs a lab, his debeaked headcrab sits on the bench: not an NPC, so nobody fights her and she fights nobody. She idles, turns, makes small noises; E on her and she lets you pet her (or doesn't). Nothing announces her. While Dr. Kleiner is taken away (arrested by a unit) or not at the bench, she's gone too, and back when he is (the owner, 14 November; `LC.LamarrSync`). entities/lc_lamarr.lua, `LC.LabLamarr`.

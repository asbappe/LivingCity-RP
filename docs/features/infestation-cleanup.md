# Weeds, the Groundskeeper and weedkiller

**Weeds, the Groundskeeper, and weedkiller** (the owner, 1 November). Weeds come up through the cracks on open ground (somewhere the sky is straight overhead, never in a camp or a Combine area), now and then: a tuft, then weeds, then a bush, a size every `lc_weed_grow_minutes` (8); a bush left alone seeds another nearby; at most `lc_weeds_max` (30). They use the first plant model the server has (an Episode 2 shrub first, then Counter-Strike: Source's small bush; since July 2025 Garry's Mod ships with both games' models built in, so every player sees them). The **Groundskeeper**, a Union job (and the city's own groundskeepers, NPCs), is issued **weedkiller** and sprays them: look at one and press E (a tuft in a moment, a bush in five seconds). The Union Commissary sells weedkiller (15). **It's poison too**: from your pockets (F3 > Weedkiller > Poison food or drink), one canister goes into the first food or drink you carry, which then looks exactly the same, to you and to whoever you give it to, and poisons whoever eats or drinks it (a unit who sees you do it calls it attempted murder). Two canisters cook down into chemicals at the Resistance lab, for its recipes. (The poison gas bomb is the Combine's now, and the lab makes a firebomb instead: see below.) `lc_weed_model` picks the weeds' model yourself (any plant model, such as another Episode 2 one). `lc_weeds 0` turns weeds off.

**Weedkiller against the infestation, and against the Combine** (the owner, 1 November). *Growths:* every infestation has growths in it (lumps of Xen growth, nests of glowing grubs, the dead it has taken), which glow a sickly green and give off spores, so you can see the infestation from the street. Spray one with weedkiller (look at it, E: four seconds) and it dies: it withers, goes dark and stops glowing, and lies there until it's taken away (a cleanup worker's hoover) or rots by itself after `lc_nest_rot_minutes` (10). Clear every growth out of a patch that grew and the patch goes: the infestation shrinks. Clear out a zone you placed and nothing grows or spawns there for a while. Growths come back one at a time, every `lc_nest_regrow_minutes` (6); `lc_infest_nests_per_patch` (4) in each patch; `lc_infest_nests 0` turns them off. With the Half-Life: Alyx Combine hazard props workshop pack (id 2043900984) on the server, they're Alyx's Xen gunk, a Combine warning post (sign, lamp, barrel) stands at the edge of each infestation (`lc_infest_hazard_posts`, 1), and the cleanup crew leaves what they spray bagged up for a minute and a half. Without the pack, they use Half-Life 2 and Episode 2 models Garry's Mod ships with. Players need the pack too (put it in the server's workshop collection). `lc_infest_nest_models` takes your own models (comma separated). *Infestation Cleanup* (a Union job, the 15th): shifts take you into the nearest infestation with a respirator (which keeps out gas, chlorine and weedkiller alike), weedkiller and a **hoover**, paid 30: spray three growths dead with the **weedkiller sprayer** (hold left click on one; E works too), vacuum them up with the **hoover** (hold left click on a dead one for a couple of seconds; it holds `lc_hoover_capacity`, 3), and empty the hoover into an **infestation control barrel** (E on it; the last step marks the nearest one). Every infestation's warning post has a barrel, and you can place your own with the editor ("Fixture: infestation control barrel"; in the editor's "by use" view, under Job: Infestation Cleanup and Infestation). Where there's no barrel near, right click with the hoover seals the load in a bag instead. The job tops up your weedkiller at each shift and whenever you run out. There are no first-person models for a sprayer or a hoover, so the thing itself (the pack's, else a stand-in) is drawn in front of you and in your hand for everyone else; each player can move them in their view with `lc_sprayer_view` (18 9 -10) and `lc_hoover_view` (26 11 -15), "forward right up". The Groundskeeper has the sprayer too. Your guards are held to the job (nothing else sends them off, and they don't leave the city), come back beside you if they fall more than 700 units behind for a few seconds (stuck, or lost), go back to guarding after a fight, and if nobody's free, dispatch sends a unit. In one of the suits, first-person hands are drawn a little nearer (the pack's sat too far out, showing the open sleeves): `lc_suit_hands "forward right up"` (-6 0 0), each player's own. Anyone missing a pack's model sees a stand-in, never an ERROR (the check asks for the model file itself: the game's own check only knows models already loaded, and showed the hoover's stand-in to players who had the pack), and players download both packs when they join (`lc_workshop_packs`, both by default; the server needs them too, in its workshop collection). Near an infestation on a shift, `lc_cleanup_guards` (2) Civil Protection units or Overwatch soldiers come and guard you and shoot the infested that come for you. The city's own cleanup crews do it too, each with a guard. Each cleanup shift counts as `lc_cleanup_shift_counts` (2) Intake Processor shifts towards joining Civil Protection, so it's the quicker, more dangerous way in. With the Half-Life: Alyx Infestation Control workshop pack (id 3185145650) on the server, cleanup workers wear its hazmat suit (the "worker" models, with a filter and an air tank), and so do the city's own cleanup crews; Morticians, Sanitation Workers and the Reclamation Crew wear its "engineer" overalls (no filter, no tank). A man always gets the male model and a woman the female one: from the character's own chosen face, never from a model's file name alone. Each person has their own skin. The suits are worn only for the work: a player only during a shift for that job, one of the city's people only while doing the job (they keep it on between one task and the next, and change back going home, at curfew, or at bedtime). These are the jobs' outfits in Job outfits until you set one of your own there. `lc_cleanup_suit` picks the cleanup job's suit (`worker`); `lc_cleanup_model` another player model instead. (In Job outfits, a model path may now say `{sex}`: male or female, to match the person.) *Barnacles:* drop weedkiller (or anything tainted with it) under a barnacle: it pulls it up, chews, and dies. *The firebomb* (the Resistance lab: two weedkiller, an empty bottle and a rag): thrown, it breaks where it lands and burns for `lc_firebomb_secs` (12). Armour doesn't keep out fire: it sets alight anyone in it, units and soldiers included, burns out power banks, forcefields and turrets for 90 seconds, and burns growths away. A unit who sees you throw it: wanted. *Poison gas is the Combine's:* the poison gas bomb (40 credits, Division Leaders and the Overwatch only, like chlorine, at the Civil Protection Commissary) is worse than chlorine, and a unit's mask keeps it out, and chlorine too, metrocops and soldiers alike. Sweeps into rebel ground open with one now and then (`lc_sweep_gas`, 0.5). *Fouled filters:* an Insider at the Commissary, with weedkiller, can soak the spare mask filters (C > Civil Protection > Inside, or /foulfilters): for `lc_fouled_minutes` (20) every unit's mask lets gas straight through, the Combine's own gas included, so a sweep that gasses rebel ground gasses itself. Or spike the station's food (/spikefood): over the next few minutes, the units who eat there are poisoned. Each once every 20 minutes.

**Changes from the 7 November playtest.**
- **The escort is two Overwatch soldiers, never metrocops:** one with a shotgun, one with a pulse rifle, both with the 4x damage of `lc_cleanup_guard_damage`. If no soldier is free the game sends them (`LC.SpawnSoldier` takes "shotgun" or "ar2"), and they walk off and are removed out of sight when the shift ends. They spawn at least 60 units apart. A guard the worker walks into steps about 70 units aside; still stuck after 2.5 seconds, it moves behind the worker.
- **They fight properly:** they only get an enemy and attack once they can see it (forcing an attack every half second had them firing into walls and restarting their burst); otherwise they go round to it, and their own AI runs the fight.
- **The quarantine post** (sign, lamp, barrel) is anchored to the nearest path node outside the zone, the sign set 100 units to the side or against the wall, the lamp and barrel along the wall; each only where a trace is clear with floor under it. Nothing sits on the marked route or inside a wall.
- **Longer each time:** a character's jobs ask for 3 growths, then 5, 7, 9, and 11 at the most, with the time limit growing to match. While a worker still needs growths, new ones grow where nobody can see.
- **The bags** left by vacuuming no longer block NPCs or players.
- **The zone is for running:** no "Stop running!" and no "move back" shove there (and no "pick up that can").
- **Your kit:** the hoover and sprayer come back on rejoining and on release from prison while you still have the job. Weedkiller comes with taking the job, at each shift, and whenever the sprayer runs empty, and the Union Commissary sells it (15 credits). Becoming a Civil Protection unit takes the job, the hoover, the sprayer and the weedkiller; becoming an anticitizen takes the job and the hoover but leaves the sprayer and weedkiller.

# Things to do as a vortigaunt, and what loyalty earns

**Things to do as a vortigaunt** (the owner asked, 1 November).
- **Shackled vortigaunts** (the Vortigaunt faction): **Reach out through the vortessence** (C menu, or `!vortcall`): eight seconds still with no unit watching, and the Resistance and every free vortigaunt hear where you are, so someone can come and cut you loose (once every `lc_vort_call_minutes`, 10; seen by a unit, the collar bites instead). **The Collar**, a story on the board: a street to sweep for the Overseer, a crate to carry to a checkpoint (fold a warning into its manifest for the Resistance, a Guile check, or hand it over), and a quiet corner to reach out from.
- **Free vortigaunts** (the Resistance's role), on the Resistance's board: **Free your kin** (a shackled vortigaunt sweeping the streets, marked: cut it loose) and **Read the Combine** (watch a checkpoint from a little way off, unseen, for 20 seconds: the vortessence reads it, and you come away with intel the quartermaster buys). The vortessence charges vests, and **mends other vortigaunts** (the one healing outside food, sleep and medicine, the owner asked): a free vortigaunt's right click on another vortigaunt, player or NPC, gathers for a second and then heals 25 (if the healer moves off or switches weapon, it fizzles). Each vortigaunt can be mended once every 10 seconds, whoever does it, and the healer waits 15 between, so a crowd of them can't keep one standing forever. Freed NPC vortigaunts in camp walk over and mend a hurt vortigaunt nearby the same way. **A free vortigaunt's bolt shorts out Combine machinery from a distance** (up to 700 units): a power bank, a forcefield or a turret goes down for `lc_vort_zap_secs` (45), and a bank or forcefield takes what hangs off it with it, then it all comes back on by itself; a unit who sees it calls it sabotage. A collared vortigaunt can do none of this: the collar holds the vortessence in, and all it lets through is the faint call to its kin.
- **The city's own**: freed vortigaunts in rebel ground walk the camp, stand by the fire and hum to themselves, instead of standing in one spot. Two in the same camp (one that's always there, and one the Resistance freed from the street crews) turn to each other every few minutes and talk in their own tongue, like the pair at Sandtraps.
- **The hive mind**: with two or more free vortigaunts in the city (the freed NPCs and Free Vortigaunt players together), every `lc_vort_commune_minutes` (15) they commune through the vortessence, and the Resistance gains by it: word on Freq 7 of the next planned sweep or raid, or else a **Combine door code** scratched on a scrap beside them in camp (or in a free vortigaunt player's pockets): it opens one Combine door, like a keycard.

**What loyalty earns** (the owner, 1 November). For citizens (and anyone passing as one) by Civil Standing tier, Trusted (150) and Exemplary (400), on top of the Commissary's tiers, breakfast after exercise and free rent from 200:
- **Fewer stops**: units stop a Trusted citizen half as often, an Exemplary one rarely, and stopping an Exemplary citizen is a glance at the papers, not a search.
- **Better pay**: Union shifts pay 10% more (Trusted) or 25% more (Exemplary).
- **A discount** at the Union Commissary: 10% (Trusted) or 20% (Exemplary).
- **More food**: an Exemplary citizen earns two ration cards for each finished shift.
- **Curfew leniency**: a minute longer to get home (Trusted and Exemplary), and an Exemplary citizen is waved through once a curfew.
- **A slip forgiven**: once an in-game day, a minor offence by an Exemplary citizen (a curfew slip, loitering, defacing, trespass) is a warning instead of wanted.
- **Public praise**: every `lc_loyalty_praise_minutes` (20) the Administrator commends an Exemplary citizen by name, on everyone's screen (and 5 standing), and their papers carry a **MODEL CITIZEN** stamp.
- **Lockers** are for citizens in good standing: below `lc_locker_standing` (150) a citizen can't open one (units always can).
- **Block Warden**, a Union job for loyal citizens only, Civil Standing 100 (was Exemplary, 400; the owner asked), and not even listed on the board below it (players, never NPCs, and never handed out): walk two corners of your own block, have a word with a neighbour, and report them or not. A report that holds up (45%) pays 10 credits and 15 standing; one that doesn't costs 10 standing.
`lc_loyalty_rewards 0` turns all of it off.

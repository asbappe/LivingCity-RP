# Permanent death, and a slower pace

### Permanent death, and a slower pace

**Close calls first.** Each person gets `lc_close_calls` (3) deaths that aren't the end: you wake in the infirmary, what was in your pockets is gone, you're still recovering for `lc_close_call_recover` minutes (10: 20 less health), and you don't remember the fight or who started it (play it that way: no going back for revenge). A used close call comes back after `lc_close_call_regen` hours (3) of that person's life; `!characters` shows how many are left. Some deaths are the end whatever's left: a stalker's, and stepping into the energizer. You can also **retire** the person you're playing (`!characters`, Retire them). Close calls are the person's, so another character has their own.

**Careers for life.** Once conditioned for the Overwatch, or made a stalker, that person can't take up another faction; the way to be someone else is another character.

**What passes on.** Credits, XP and everything else belong to the person, never to the player. When a person ends, the next inherits `lc_inherit_pct` (25%) of their savings, up to `lc_inherit_max` (200). Hoarding is taxed: every `lc_levy_minutes` (30) the Union takes `lc_levy_pct` (10%) of whatever anyone holds above `lc_levy_threshold` (2000; 0 turns it off). The player keeps only a record, their **legacy**: how many lives, the longest, and how the last few ended, shown at the top of `!characters`.

**When a person ends, they're gone.** You come back as someone new: a new name and citizen number (shown on name tags and in chat), no credits, no Civil Standing, no job, empty pockets and stash, no rank in any faction, no story, and a housing block of your own. Infested players come back as themselves, admins in build mode are exempt (you're told so in chat when it happens, so type `!build` to leave build mode and play for real), and dying in custody doesn't end or shorten a sentence. Conduct strikes belong to the real player and stay. `lc_permadeath 0` turns it off; `lc_newcharacter <name>` wipes someone by hand.

**The end of a life.** When you die the screen goes dark and a card says who that person was: their name and citizen number, how long they lived in the city, how many people knew their name, and what killed them, as they saw it ("Shot by a masked Civil Protection unit.", "A fall.", "Starved."). Put down first and finished off later, it's whoever put them down. The card stays up for `lc_respawn_secs` (12) before you can click to go on, and your eyes open on the city again. A death that doesn't end the person (build mode, custody, the Infested, permanent death off) gets a short card that says why. **Word gets around:** everyone who knew their name hears it a minute or two later, in passing ("[Word gets around] You hear that Ada Grey died. They say it was the Combine."), with what people would say did it: the Combine, the Resistance (only if the killer looked the part; a rebel passing as a citizen is just somebody), the infestation, a fall, hunger. Whoever did it isn't told. Someone who's away, or playing another of their characters, hears when they're next in the city as the person who knew them (for up to 30 days). Time lived is counted while you're alive and kept with each character; people from before this was counted show "at least". `lc_word_gets_around 0` keeps deaths quiet. Who knows each citizen number is kept in `garrysmod/data/livingcity/known.json`, the news still to be passed on in `word.json`.

**Movement is slower and grounded**, so it feels like a person walking through a city: walk 110, sprint 190 (`lc_walk_speed`, `lc_run_speed`), a normal Half-Life 2 jump (`lc_jump_power`, 200) with a pause before the next one (`lc_jump_delay`), no jumping from a crouch, no ducking in the air, and no gaining speed in the air. **You can climb:** jump at anything up to about chest height (a crate, a platform edge, out of a track bed) and you pull yourself up onto it. If you still end up somewhere you can't get out of, type `!stuck` (`lc_stuck_cooldown`, 5 minutes, not while wanted or in custody) to be put back on the street. `lc_slow_movement 0` brings the Half-Life 2 feel back.

**How it looks: a city too big for you.** The view is never wider than `lc_fov` (75, Half-Life 2's own; plenty of players run 90 or 100, which shrinks everything on screen), and your eyes sit at `lc_eye_height` (61 units; GMod's is 64), a little lower than everyone else's, so people and units stand a touch over you. The camera sways faintly in step with your feet (a grown-up's stride: running is about three steps a second, not a scamper) and dips when you land, harder the further you fell (`lc_view_bob`; `lc_view_bob_amount`, 100, sets how strong, in percent; any player can keep theirs still with `!bob`, or `lc_my_view_bob 0`). Every camera effect (holding a pose, flying a scanner, Breen's water) runs through one camera, so they all keep the field of view. **The quiet HUD** (`lc_quiet_hud`, on) keeps score off the screen: no XP bar or "+15 XP", no credits or standing (they're at the top of your pockets, F2; rank and progress on F4), no kill feed in the corner, and the hunger and thirst bars only once they start to bite (under half), or for a few seconds after you eat or drink. Editor panel: **How it feels**. The visual style (models, materials, lighting) is up to the map and content packs you run.

**Choosing who you are.** The first time you join, and again each time you start over after dying, a window asks for a **name** and a **face** (one of the citizen models, shown turning in a preview: Half-Life 2's own and any installed citizen pack, found by itself; a Workshop pack is sent to joining players, and a model nobody's computer can load is never offered, so no ERROR models), then, under the picture, that model's **face skins** and its **clothes and accessories** (bodygroups: glasses, caps, coats...), one at a time with < and >. What you choose stays with that person. An admin can narrow what's offered in **Job outfits > New characters choose from** (only the models, faces and pieces ticked there). It reminds you to pick a realistic name for a person in this city and era, an ordinary name, with no celebrities, characters from games, films or shows, memes or joke names. The server enforces it: one to three words, 3 to 26 characters, letters only, no names on the blocklist (public figures, fictional characters, memes, slurs and profanity), and nobody else in the city can share your name. "Keep what I was given (which ignores anything you typed or picked: only This is me applies the name in the box and the face you chose)" accepts the random ones. You can change them whenever you like with `!character`. Your face carries over to whatever you become: the same face in the Resistance's clothes, say. Staff: `lc_setname <player> <name>`, `lc_creator <player>`. In a city with Civil Intake (below) the window is your registration form instead, filled in at the desk.

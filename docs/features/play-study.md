# The play study

The owner, 14 November: the gamemode stands in for other players when none are on, so "record how I play, then parse and filter it down to make baselines to influence the NPC/AI behaviour ... I could literally be like a model or case-study". This is **stage 1: a recorder and a report**. Nothing about any NPC changes by itself: the report goes to the code chat, which proposes changes for the owner's OK. (Stage 2, not built: the city's people read a table of baselines directly.)

**The switch:** F4 > Factions > *Admin: play study* (superadmins: whoever may use the editor). Its window has *Start: this session counts*, and while on: *Stop and save*, *Strike the last job*, *Strike the last 5 minutes*, *Discard this whole session*, and the slider *Away from the keys after (seconds)* (`lc_study_afk` on the player's own computer, 90). One player at a time. While it's on, a small line at the top right says STUDY: counting, or STUDY PAUSED and why. With no study under way nothing is hooked or wrapped.

**What it keeps in mind, every second** (`sv_livingcity_study.lua`):
- **Who:** faction and rank (or special role). Every table is per who: a Rebel on the Combine's ground and a unit there are never merged (the owner: "a rebel in a combine zone will act differently than a combine, and vice versa").
- **Where:** the kind of zone (`combine`, `rebel`, `prison`, `block`, `infest`, `nogo`, or no zone), whose ground that is to the player (own ground, the other side's ground, the Combine's or the Resistance's ground for someone on neither side, open ground), and the nearest thing placed with the editor within 350 units (a ration line, a checkpoint, a workplace...). The owner: "what zone I am in determines my activity".
- **In what:** the city, or a dream (which one, and whether it's a test: "a dream all the same"). Dreams get their own tables, never mixed with the city's.
- **The job or story** they're on, its stages, and how it ended.
- How they move (still, walking, running, crouched still, crouch-walking, seated, dead), what they hold, the time of day, security in sight (a unit or soldier within 700 units), people within 500 units.

**What it writes down as it happens:** places entered and left (with how long), the job taken, its stages and outcome, every one of the gamemode's own `LivingCity...` events that names the player (scavenged, searched, wanted, gave, hacked...; questions like `Can...` and `Is...` are left out), things used, attacks, hits taken, kills, deaths, chat commands and talk, notices received, each **meeting with security** (how far, how long, and what the player did: ran off, moved away, closed in, kept low, stood their ground, carried on), jumps and crouches **with their map coordinates**, and a trail of map coordinates every five seconds on the move. The owner: jumping or crouching "might be dependent on map geometry and layout", so the report lists them by spot on the map, to be checked against the map before anything becomes a behaviour (and for laying over the map's own file one day).

**Paused, and not counted:** flying (noclip), holding the editor's tool, the physics gun or the camera, spectating, out of the game window or in the game's own menu, and away from the keys (no key or mouse for `lc_study_afk` seconds; the quiet time before it was noticed is taken out too).

**Kept out of the baselines:** a job that failed or was abandoned (it may have been a glitch; the owner: "I wouldn't want that data to poison the data set"), anything struck with *Strike the last job* (one that finished but went wrong) or *Strike the last 5 minutes*. It all stays in the log, marked; the report leaves it out and lists it under NOT COUNTED.

**The files**, on the player's own PC, never the server (`cl_livingcity_study.lua`): `garrysmod/data/livingcity/study/<date>_<time>_<faction>/log.txt` (written as it happens) and `report.txt` (when stopped): not counted and why; a table for each who and what, by place (time, visits, how long a visit lasts, movement, security in sight, on a job, time of day); jobs and stories; meetings with security; where on the map (256-unit squares: most time, jumps, crouches); what happened, counted by who and where. A discarded session leaves nothing.

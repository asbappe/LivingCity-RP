# Pose Recorder (a separate tool)

The `pose_recorder/` folder is its own addon (install it as `garrysmod/addons/pose_recorder/`, from `pose_recorder.zip`), made for posing the endings' dances and fit for the Workshop on its own. Tools, Poser, Pose Recorder: spawn a mannequin or pick any ragdoll, pose it with the physgun, right click to snap keyframes (or record it moving), reload to watch a blue ghost play the loop to the beat, then copy it or save it to `garrysmod/data/pose_recorder/<name>.json`. Each bone's angle is what `ManipulateBoneAngles` needs, relative to the model standing in its idle. Paste the JSON to whoever's working on the code. Details, the file format and publishing to the Workshop: `pose_recorder/README.md`.

**Pose Recorder 2 (self-serve, first update):** everything below is in the tool's panel; console commands are extras.

- **Smoothing as buttons:** a list of the keyframes; pick some (none means all) and give them a length (beats), an easing into the next (smooth, the old default; snappy; bouncy; linear), or mirror them (left and right swapped); add a mirrored copy of the whole loop; a Wrap box (on: the last keyframe eases back into the first; off: the loop stops on the last). Mirroring follows the owner's bone-axis notes (TODO.md): a limb keeps yaw and flips pitch and roll; a bone down the middle keeps pitch and flips yaw and roll.
- **Playing, seen by everyone:** "Play what I'm making on me", or a saved one or the server's from the list; loop, once or hold the last pose, at a speed. On an NPC: context menu, Play animation / Stop animation. Console: `pose_play <name> [loop|once|hold] [speed]`, `pose_stop`. The server only networks "entity E plays animation A from time T, mode, speed"; each client turns the bones itself every frame, and a late joiner is told what's playing. A player's own animation is sent up once and checked. Server settings `pose_recorder_play_self`, `pose_recorder_play_npcs` (0, 1 context-menu rights, 2 admins), `pose_recorder_uploads`; hook `PoseRecorderCanPlay(ply, ent)`. Stops when the player dies.
- **Sharing:** animations are small JSON files; packs in any addon's `data_static/pose_recorder/` (and the server's own `data/pose_recorder/`) are picked up on start (`pose_recorder_reload_packs` for admins). Lua: `PoseRecorder.Play(ent, "wave", { mode, speed })`, `Stop`, `Register`, `Get`, `PlayingOn`.
- **The file:** version 2 adds `"format": 2`, `"wrap"`, and `"ease"` per keyframe; old files (no `format`) load and play as before. Every file from a player or a pack goes through `PoseRecorder.Sanitize` (at most 600 keyframes, 80 bones, finite numbers, 256 KB).
- **The limit (say it on the Workshop page):** these are bone turns on top of the model's idle, not compiled animations, so they can't change walking or running; exporting SMD for modelers is a separate, later project.
- **Code:** `pose_recorder/lua/pose_recorder/core.lua` (the maths: easing, mirror, playing, checking files; no engine calls), `play.lua` (networking, packs, the context menu, each client's bones), `lua/autorun/pose_recorder.lua` (recording and the tool's commands), the stool (the panel). Tests: `python3 _dev/pose_recorder_tests.py` (maths, file round trip, hostile files, a fake server and two clients), and the Pose Recorder part of `_dev/client_smoke.py`.
- **Next update (approved, not built):** a timeline strip (jump to a keyframe, reorder, duplicate, delete, scrub) and putting a saved pose back onto a ragdoll.

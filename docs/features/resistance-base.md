# The Resistance's headquarters: one base at a time (`sv_livingcity_base.lua`)

The owner, 20 November: "the highest ranking Resistance player (Lieutenant or Commander) can alter the location of the resistance HQ ... which he has to visit and be present at ... it takes 5 minutes with him nearby (within 300 feet radius or so) to setup"; "setup could literally be hoisting the flag up the pole slowly"; the commander's office ("securitybank.mdl with all the camera monitors, his desk, shelves, any computers or consoles, board, even the science lab") does "not show until the base is established"; and, to stop leaders "running in a big circle around the map moving CONSTANTLY", it moves only three ways (below). Built 20 November; not played yet.

## What happens

- **Where:** any rebel territory (the zones of kind `rebel` placed with the Miscount Editor). Each territory is a possible site; only one is the headquarters. **A map can bring the territory with its pole** (21 November): an `info_target` named like `lc_zone_rebel_r700_h1800_silo` adds a rebel territory there once, as if placed with the editor (`docs/features/editor-placing.md`, "Zones a map brings").
- **A bare pole stands at every one of the map's own spots for one** (an `info_target` named `lc_hq_setup...`: the town's plaza beside the stage, the canal's shanty town), a territory over it or not (the owner, 20 November: "always stand a bare pole at the markers"); outside rebel territory nobody can raise a flag on it. **A marker can set its pole's height** (21 November, for the missile silo joined to the forest bunker: the owner wants the flag "raised from the bottom of the silo" and "flush with eye level on the top floor"): a second `info_target` named the same with `_top` added (`lc_hq_setup_silo` and `lc_hq_setup_silo_top`), straight above it, marks the pole's top. The pole stands on the floor under the first marker and reaches the second (no 330 limit; at most 4096), is raised from its foot like any pole, and the flag flies full size at the top, hanging 12 to 96 under the `_top` marker (so the map puts that marker about 120 over the top floor). A tall pole is a stack of pieces each as thick as a pole in the open (one piece scaled up would be 15 times as thick). A `_top` marker is never a spot of its own; a marker without one is as before; in a territory with both kinds, the one with a `_top` is the territory's pole. **And one stands in every rebel territory** (where a leader last planted it; else at the map's own spot, an `info_target` named `lc_hq_setup`; else the middle of the camp). Any member of the Resistance standing at one is told what E does there; anyone below Lieutenant reads "Resistance Commander or Lieutenant is required to establish a Resistance HQ" (the owner's words, 20 November).
- **Raising the flag.** **Any Lieutenant or Commander** of the Resistance (the owner, 20 November: so a Commander who leaves in the middle of a move strands nobody; no order has to be given first): **E at the pole**, or anywhere in rebel territory **F2 > Resistance > Raise the flag here**, which plants the pole where they stand. Whoever of those ranks is within reach keeps it going: the one who started can leave. A pole stands where he stood (330 tall in the open; **under a roof or a bridge only as tall as fits**, down to 110, so a base in the canal's shantytown shows no flag over the canal's walls) and a red flag climbs it for 5 minutes, but only while a Lieutenant or Commander is alive within 300 feet of it and nothing hostile (a unit, a Combine NPC, a zombie, a headcrab, an antlion) is within about 45 feet of the pole. Left alone for a minute, a half-raised flag slides back down and the pole goes. The same F2 line (or E at the pole), pressed again, gives it up.
- **Live.** At the top the base is the headquarters and **the commander's office appears round the pole**: the security monitors, a desk, a console bench, shelves, a lab bench, servers and a board. They are the camp's own things: a held E carries them, R turns them, and where each was left at a site is kept, so each site's office is laid out once by hand and comes back like that.
- **The flag is the Lambda** in its ring, orange on black, 132 by 84 on a full pole, waving, darker in its folds and in the dark (`LC_CL.BaseFlagDraw`, `cl_livingcity_base.lua`; the owner, 20 November, of the first plain red one: "that is just pathetic ... surely you can make a Lambda flag?").
- **The flag is the only message.** Nothing is written on anyone's screen: climbing is setting up, the top is live, half mast is overrun, coming down is packing or a unit tearing it down.

## The three ways a base moves

1. **Packed (their own choice).** A Lieutenant or Commander, at the live base once it has stood 10 minutes: **E at the pole or F2 > Resistance > Strike the flag**. It comes down over 3 minutes with him near; at the bottom the office is packed and a flag may be raised somewhere else (another 5 minutes). Pressed again while it comes down, the same line hoists it back.
2. **Ransacked (the Combine).** A Civil Protection or Overwatch unit presses E at the pole while no living Resistance player is within about 45 feet: the flag comes down in 20 seconds as long as the unit stays by it; a rebel coming back stops it and the flag goes up again. At the bottom the office is destroyed, **35 per cent of the Resistance's fund** is shared among the units there, and the Resistance has no base.
3. **Overrun (zombies, headcrabs, antlions; zombie players too).** On its ground for 45 seconds with no living rebel there: the flag drops to half mast and the base is not live. The office stays. Clear them out and stand there 20 seconds and it is live again (or strike it then). No flag goes up anywhere else while it is overrun; a unit can still tear the half-mast flag down.

## What changed for what was there before

- **The security monitors** stood in every rebel camp (19 November). With this on they are part of the office and stand only at the live base. `lc_base 0` turns the whole feature off and puts them back in every camp.
- Nothing else of the camps changes: the quartermaster, the supply crate, the fund's screen and the rest work everywhere as before. The later stages (below) were described to the owner and built on 22 November.

## The later stages (the owner, 22 November; `sv_livingcity_hq.lua`, `cl_livingcity_hq.lua`)

**What only the live HQ does** (the owner: "yes to all of"):
- **Respawn there.** The live HQ counts as one of the Resistance's spawn points: a Resistance player last nearest it (on its ground) comes back there, as at the rebel spawn point they were last nearest. Gone, they come back at a spawn point as before.
- **Its lab.** Bypass charges and IEDs are made only by a scientist whose lab stands on the HQ's ground (`LC.LabHQOnly`); anywhere else the lab shows "Only the headquarters' lab makes this." With no HQ up nobody makes them. Everything else the lab makes, it makes anywhere.
- **The wounded heal.** Rebel NPCs on the HQ's ground get 5 health back every 5 seconds.

**What the Combine know of it.** A hidden figure for each site, back to nothing when the base moves, never a number: the Administrator's F2 > City orders shows "The Resistance's headquarters: nothing / rumours / a district / the street / the building". Only players move it (the owner: "a player must inform ... only players will be leaving the city"):
- **Informing:** a player who has been on its ground tells a unit (the Inform list: "Where the Resistance's headquarters is"), once per site; a district's worth; 40 credits.
- **Questioning:** a Resistance player in the cells who has been there and tells the truth about the hideout names the HQ (and it is marked for the units, as any hideout told is).
- **Scouting:** "Scout rebel ground" for units (F4): go to a rebel territory and search it by hand; if it is the HQ's, they learn.
- **A scanner a unit sends** from the radio's own menu ("Send a scanner to scout", once every 5 minutes): it flies to where they aim for a minute and a half and learns while the HQ's pole is in its sight. The city's own scanners never do (the owner: random scanners must not give it away).

**The raid.** At "the building", the Administrator's City orders > the Resistance's headquarters > **Raid it** (with nobody playing her, the city's own orders it two minutes on, if its authority is 50 or more). Every unit gets **Raid the Resistance headquarters** (F4: get there, tear the flag down as a ransack does), up to six of the nearest NPC units go and tear it down themselves when no rebel is by it, and the Resistance's lookouts warn them on Freq 7, with **Defend the headquarters** on their board. Torn down: a ransack as before, and the Administrator gains authority. Beaten off (the raiders dead and no unit near for 20 seconds) or not done in 8 minutes: it fails, costs her authority, and no raid can follow for 20 minutes.

**A ransack's papers** (the owner: only this of the intel): up to three of the city's sympathizers are named on the CP net and are suspects from then on (a unit's search finds things on them far more often).

No new settings. Tests `Nov22_HQTests`.

## Settings (all new)

`lc_base` 1 (on) · `lc_base_setup_time` 300 · `lc_base_pack_time` 180 · `lc_base_stay` 600 (before it may be struck) · `lc_base_radius` 4800 (300 feet: how near the leader stays) · `lc_base_core` 700 (the base's ground) · `lc_base_teardown` 20 · `lc_base_ransack_share` 35 · `lc_base_overrun` 45.

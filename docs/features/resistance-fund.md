# The Resistance's fund (18 and 19 November)

The owner, 18 November, on the leader's order to kill the Administrator: "Only the first should be paid that high, the rest maybe 1/2 or less. Also I think we need to add a limiter to all of this: the resistance leader can only order this if they've saved up enough money themselves to put into this extra pay, or have gathered enough funds in a Resistance fund, of sorts, that they can increase by having successful supply convoy raids and such; I suppose I am requesting that various successful missions where supplies are raided or people saved (who then donate small amounts of credits that they may have saved) would all be spit between the partakers of the activities and a general resistance fund, which is used to supply the resistance and then eventually can get large enough to fund these operations."

The owner, 19 November: "I think the funds should be for the equipment issued to fighters, such as ammo, weapons, etc. obviously those are provided by the fund, which 'purchases' them at a reduced price versus what a player pays." "I would prefer food coming from supply raids, donations (does not need to be a proper mechanic, just in my head that's the lore / roleplay), or scavenging; no to cheaper quartermaster prices." "can we please have an option for people to donate to the resistance by talking to resistance members? ... I don't want to make MORE windows or more menus or more complications/complexity, just a way for random citizens and resistance fighters to donate credits somehow in a simple manner". When it is empty: "only what the fund can afford; if the fund cannot afford it then give them a cheaper/downgraded weapon that can be afforded. Crowbars are free (or rather, incur no cost from the fund). There needs to be ways for the spawning player to understand this; we want to imply such as to why they are not receiving a gun without spamming more text at a player. I am thinking maybe we have the Resistance fund numbers on the small TV as an entity? and pressing E on it lets you review the charges/costs and additions to the fund and where they are coming from as a little popup window? ... our little small TV with the fund amount on it can also have an option for 'Donate'". And: "please also add some lines for the resistance leader/cubbage if the fund is down to 0 for more than 10 minutes, or if it's hovering extremely low (under 50) for that long, basically begging for donations both to any player interacting and his speeches/announcements".

`sv_livingcity_resistancefund.lua` (the fund and what feeds it), `sv_livingcity_fundgear.lua` (what it buys, its record, the screen, donating), `sv_livingcity_fundplea.lua` (asking for it when it runs dry), `entities/lc_fundtv.lua` and `cl_livingcity_fundtv.lua` (the screen and its window), `sv_livingcity_adminplot.lua` (the order's price and the half pay), the row and the price on F2 (`cl_livingcity_quick.lua`, sent by `sv_livingcity_quickmore.lua`). Tests: `Nov18_ResistanceFundTests`, `Nov19_FundGearTests`, `Nov19_FundPleaTests`, `Nov17_AdminPlotTests`, the client smoke test.

- **The fund** is a whole number of credits that belongs to the Resistance and to no player. It is kept with the state of the city (`LC.Ledger`: `fund` in `data/livingcity/<map>_city.json`) and written the moment it changes, so it is there after a restart or a map change. No new data file.
- **The starting fund** (19 November): 1000 credits are put in once, the first time a map's ledger is seen without a record (below), so a server does not send every fighter out with a crowbar the day this is installed. Its record says "+1000  What the cells had put by". Not again after a restart. (A constant, `START` in `sv_livingcity_fundgear.lua`.)
- **The take, and the split.** Each success below has a take in credits. **Half goes to the fund; the other half is shared equally among the Resistance's players who took part**, each paid as any pay is: "[Credits] +10 (your share)". What does not divide equally stays with the fund (three sharing 20 have 6 each, and the fund 22). When the city's own fighters did it with no player of ours in it, the fund has its half and the other half is the fighters' own. **The take is on top of what the success pays or gives already: no reward that was there is changed.**
- **Supplies raided:**
  - **A load pried out of a supply convoy's crate** by a Resistance player (`LivingCitySupplyLooted`): 20 for each load; the one who pried it. A load taken by someone who is not of the Resistance gives nothing.
  - **Our fighters' raid on a supply drop that got loads out of the crate** (`LivingCityRebelOpDone`, "supply": the city's own operation, or a planned one): 40; those who joined the plan and were there (alive, within 1500 units, as the plan itself pays them). A crate already cleaned out, or not down yet, is no success.
  - **A crate of the Union's supplies stolen and brought home** (the board job `res_supplyrun`, finished): 30; everyone on the job.
- **People saved.** Someone saved "donates" a small sum: 10 to 30 credits, random, whoever they are. **A player who is saved is never charged for it: nothing leaves their pockets.**
  - **A prisoner out of the cells** (`LivingCityBreakout`): whoever got the cell open and those on that job with them (`res_breakout`). Let out by the fighters' own operation, with nobody named: those who joined the plan and were there, once the operation says it worked. The one let out never shares in their own rescue.
  - **A vortigaunt cut loose by a Resistance player** (`LivingCityVortFreed`: one of the city's street crews, or a player in shackles): the one who cut. The city's own team freeing a vortigaunt nobody came for gives nothing.
  - **A citizen broken out and led to safety, a fugitive brought home, a shackled vortigaunt led home** (the board jobs `res_jailbreak`, `res_rescue` and `res_freevort`, finished): everyone on the job.
- **Not counted** (no supplies taken, nobody saved, or nobody's doing): a data terminal raided, a screen hijacked, an Overwatch landing ambushed; the supply run from a contact (`res_supply`), smuggling, scavenging; a prisoner who slips away from a march by themselves; someone uncuffed; someone healed or got back on their feet.
- **Donations** (19 November): anyone can give credits to the fund, at the screen in the camp or in the quartermaster's window (below).

## What it pays for

- **The fighters' guns and rounds** (19 November). Each time a Resistance player is issued their rank's guns and rounds (on spawn; and the guns a promotion adds), **the fund buys each gun and its rounds at what a player pays the quartermaster, times `lc_resistance_issue_rate` (50: per cent)**. A gun he does not sell (the pulse rifle) is priced at the unit counter's (300, its rounds 30 for 60). Rounds are priced by his box: its price over the rounds in it. At 50, with the market as it is by default: a pistol 36 and its 90 rounds 63; an SMG 60 and its 180 rounds 80; a shotgun 63 and its 24 shells 33; a revolver 72 and its 12 rounds 33; a pulse rifle 150 and its 60 rounds 15. (Those round prices were the first version's.) **Rounds are almost free to the fund (19 November, the owner: "rounds should be MUCH cheaper, almost free"): a tenth of the guns' rate (`ROUND_SHARE` 0.1), so at 50 a round costs the fund 5 per cent of the quartermaster's price. A Fighter's full kit then costs the fund about 111, a Lieutenant's 178, a Commander's 363**; Hacker and Smuggler about 40, Father Grigori about 66. The quartermaster's prices move with the market, and so do these. `0` issues everything free, as before, and the fund pays nothing.
  - **In order, the most important first:** the dearest gun with its rounds, then the next. **When it cannot pay for a gun, they get the best cheaper gun it can pay for instead** (pulse rifle, revolver, shotgun, SMG, pistol, dearest first), **and when it can pay for none, the crowbar alone.** The crowbar, fists and a role's own tool (the multitool, the Blessing, the vortessence) cost nothing; a role whose kit has no crowbar is given one then. Rounds: what the fund can pay for, up to the usual number (90 pistol, 180 SMG, 24 shotgun, 12 revolver, 60 pulse rifle), less what they carry already. Nothing is ever taken back, and nothing they carry is charged (a promotion buys only the gun it adds). The rounds of kinds their rank's guns do not use come as before, free, as for every player.
  - A cheaper gun the fund bought outside their rank's kit counts as issued: it cannot be sold to a dealer or the quartermaster for the fund's credits.
  - **The city's own Resistance NPCs cost it nothing,** and nor does anyone else's kit.
  - **Nothing is said** to the spawning player, in chat or on screen. The screen's record (below) is where it shows: a fighter who went out short has an entry of their own.
- **The leader's order.** Each member the leader sends for (F2 > Resistance > **Order: kill the Administrator (300 each)**) costs `lc_resistance_order_cost` credits (300; 0 makes it free), charged at the moment they are sent for: **out of the fund when it holds enough; otherwise out of the leader's own credits** (all of it from one or the other); when neither can pay, nobody is sent for and the leader reads one line, "Neither the fund nor you can pay 300 credits." A leader who picks themselves pays the same. Nothing else is said, to anyone.
  - **It comes back** when the order ends before that member has made the attempt: taken back by the leader, the member arrested, someone new, out of the Resistance or below a Fighter, an hour gone by sent for and never spoken with, the leader gone with nobody leading. It goes back where it came from: the fund, or the leader who paid it, if they are still that person, of the Resistance and playing; else the fund. What was paid is what goes back, whatever the setting says by then.
  - **Made, the attempt spends it for good.**
  - Where it came from is kept with the order (PData `lc_adminplot_price`: `300:fund`, or `300:<the leader's SteamID64>:<their citizen number>`), so a reconnect or a restart of the server loses nothing.
  - **The job on the board (`res_regicide`, while nobody leads) costs nothing.**
- **Half after the first.** At one address the first under orders whose attempt counts (the job's holder, or a member under the order) is paid in full: 250 experience, 100 credits, and 250 more if she dies of it. Anyone else under the leader's order whose attempt counts at that same address is paid half of each part: 125, 50 and 125. Her next address starts again. The job from the board is never halved. (`docs/features/city.md`, "An ordered attempt is a coordinated attack".)
- **Not food.** Food comes from supply raids, donations and scavenging, as it always has; the quartermaster's prices are not cheaper for members (the owner, 19 November: "no to cheaper quartermaster prices").

## The record

Every change to the fund is one entry: when, how much, and a short line in plain words, with the names (character names) of the Resistance players it was for or because of: one "(Kerr)", two "(Kerr, Vance)", more "(Kerr and others)". Kept with the fund in the ledger (`fundLog` in `<map>_city.json`), **the newest 50**, so it is there after a restart. The lines:

- "+10  A load from a supply convoy (Kerr)", "+20  A supply drop raided", "+15  A crate of the Union's supplies, brought home (...)", "+12  A gift from someone we got out (...)"
- "-300  An order, paid for", "+300  An order called off, paid back"
- "-111  Guns and ammunition for a fighter (Kerr)"; with one gun, "A pistol and ammunition for a fighter (Kerr)" (or "An SMG", "A shotgun", "A revolver", "A pulse rifle"); "A pistol for a fighter" when no rounds were bought; "Guns for a fighter"; "Ammunition for a fighter"
- at no cost: "A fighter went out with only a crowbar. The fund was empty.", "A fighter got a pistol, not an SMG. The fund was short.", "A fighter went without a shotgun and an SMG. The fund was short."
- "+50  A donation", or "+50  A donation (Kerr)" from one of the Resistance
- "+1000  What the cells had put by" (the starting fund)

**A donation earns sympathy toward joining (19 November, the owner: "yes yes yes, scaling (without allowing abuse) to the amount donated"):** one sympathy for every 25 credits given, at most two in a real day (what is given past the cap earns its sympathy on a later day), for anyone not of the Resistance or the Combine and not a burned informant; kept with the character, and cleared for someone new (`LC.FundDonationSympathy`; PData `lc_fund_given`, `lc_fund_symp`, `lc_fund_symp_day`, `lc_fund_symp_n`). Five sympathy lets a citizen join, so money alone takes at least three days. **The record names Resistance members to anyone who reads it, units included** (the owner: "that would be awfully suspicious if found by a CP unit"): nothing is done to them for it; what a unit makes of it is roleplay.

## The screen in the camp

**Fixture: Resistance fund screen** (editor kind `fundtv`, entity `lc_fundtv`): the apartment's small TV (`models/props_c17/tv_monitor01.mdl`), set on a table, facing whoever places it. Its glass shows the fund's number, big and plain, with "credits" under it, kept up to date (a dead screen when the district's power is out). **E on it, anyone who can reach it** (within 150 units; citizens and units too): one small window, "Resistance fund", with the number ("1040 credits"), the newest 15 entries of the record, newest first (gains green, costs red, the no-cost lines grey), and three buttons, **Donate 10, Donate 50, Donate 100**. The credits leave the donor's pocket and go to the fund; the window refreshes. Refused with one line in the window, "You don't have that much.", when the pocket is short; silently from too far away, faster than one a second, or an amount that is not one of the three.

## Donating by talking to them

The Resistance's fighters and lookouts have no menu (E on one brings them along), so nothing was added there. **The quartermaster's own window** (E on him, a member's or a stranger's) has one more row, "**10 credits for the cause**", with a **Donate** button (greyed when the pocket is short): 10 credits to the fund, the entry "A donation" (named for a member). A unit never gets his window.

## Asking for it when it runs dry

When the fund has been **under 50 credits for 10 minutes in a row** (0 counts), the Resistance asks for it, in each one's own way. It stops as soon as the fund holds 50 or more, and the ten minutes start again the next time it drops. The moment it went under is kept with the fund in the ledger (`fundLowSince`, the clock's own time), so a restart does not reset it.

- **The Colonel** (the ghost only the Resistance sees, `docs/features/city-people.md`): E on him and he asks for it in place of his greeting, at most once a player every five minutes, never the same line twice running. "The fund is empty. Our fighters are going out with crowbars. Give what you can at the screen in the camp." / "I can't arm anyone on promises. If you have credits, the fund needs them." / "There's nothing left for rounds. Not a credit. Whatever you can spare, put it in at the screen." / "Another fighter just went out with nothing but a crowbar. Every credit at that screen helps." (`sound/livingcity/colonel/fund_empty1.wav`, `fund_promises1.wav`, `fund_rounds1.wav`, `fund_crowbar1.wav`)
- **Colonel Cubbage** answering her address (`docs/features/city.md`): his answer ends, after his closing, with one more piece, heard as the rest is: "One more thing. Our fund is empty, and our fighters are going out with crowbars. If you have credits, bring them to the camp." / "Before I go. We have almost nothing left to arm anyone with. Whatever you can give, give it at the screen in our camp." / "And one last word. I can't arm people with speeches. If you have credits to spare, the fund needs them." / "One more thing. Some of ours are going out with crowbars, some with nothing at all. Give what you can, at the camp." (`sound/livingcity/cubbage/answer_fund_empty1.wav`, `answer_fund_nothing1.wav`, `answer_fund_promises1.wav`, `answer_fund_crowbars1.wav`)
- **Freq 7** (`docs/features/radios.md`): at most once every twenty minutes, its call cuts in the way its warnings do: `sound/livingcity/radio/freq7_fund1.wav` to `freq7_fund4.wav`, one take at random (the suggested words are in `VOICE_SCRIPTS.md`). With none recorded, nothing.

No notice, no popup: spoken lines, shown as each one's own lines are.

## What players see, and nothing more

On F2 > Resistance, one row for every member, "Fund: 120 credits", under the page's entries and above the fighters left; the price on the leader's entry; the one refusal of the order; "your share" beside the credits of whoever took part; the screen in the camp, its window and its one refusal; the quartermaster's row. No chat line, notice or radio line when the fund grows or is spent, or when a fighter goes out short. Since 19 November every job on the boards that feeds the fund says, beside its pay, what it can put in ("fund +5-15") and counts its share in the credits it says (`docs/features/jobs-and-quests.md`; the owner: "this means all players feel responsible for helping grow the resistance fund").

## For other code

`LC.ResistanceFund()` (how much), `LC.ResistanceFundAdd(n, why, who)` (n more; `why` is for the server's log and the record, `who` the players it was for), `LC.ResistanceFundTake(n, why, who)` (true, or false when it holds less), `LC.ResistanceFundSuccess(kind, players)` (a success counted: `"load"`, `"raid"`, `"run"` or `"saved"`), `LC.AdminPlotCost()` (the price); for the job boards, `LC.ResistanceFundJobKind(id)`, `LC.ResistanceFundStepKinds`, `LC.ResistanceFundShares(kind, n)`; `LC.FundIssue(ply, kit, keep)` (what a Resistance player is given, called by the loadout in `sv_livingcity_factions.lua`, whose usual rounds are `LC.SpawnRounds`), `LC.FundGunPrice(cls)`, `LC.FundRoundPrice(kind)`, `LC.ResistanceFundRecord(n, why, who)`, `LC.ResistanceFundLog(n)`, `LC.FundSeed()`, `LC.FundScreenOpen(ply, tv)`, `LC.FundDonate(ply, amount, atQuartermaster)`; `LC.FundLow()`, `LC.FundPleaLine(ply, place)`, `LC.FundPleaPiece(place)`, `LC.FundPleaBroadcast()`. Every movement of the fund is one line in the server's log (the debug report). Net: `lc_fundtv` (the window), `lc_fund_donate` (a donation).

## Settings

`lc_resistance_order_cost` (300), `lc_resistance_issue_rate` (50). The takes and the split are constants at the top of `sv_livingcity_resistancefund.lua`; the half for later shooters is `LATER` in `sv_livingcity_adminplot.lua`; the starting fund, the record's size, the screen's amounts and the quartermaster's 10 are constants in `sv_livingcity_fundgear.lua`; when it is low and how often each one asks, in `sv_livingcity_fundplea.lua`.

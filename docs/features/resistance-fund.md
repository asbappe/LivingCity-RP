# The Resistance's fund (18 November)

The owner, 18 November, on the leader's order to kill the Administrator: "Only the first should be paid that high, the rest maybe 1/2 or less. Also I think we need to add a limiter to all of this: the resistance leader can only order this if they've saved up enough money themselves to put into this extra pay, or have gathered enough funds in a Resistance fund, of sorts, that they can increase by having successful supply convoy raids and such; I suppose I am requesting that various successful missions where supplies are raided or people saved (who then donate small amounts of credits that they may have saved) would all be spit between the partakers of the activities and a general resistance fund, which is used to supply the resistance and then eventually can get large enough to fund these operations."

`sv_livingcity_resistancefund.lua` (the fund and what feeds it), `sv_livingcity_adminplot.lua` (the order's price and the half pay), the row and the price on F2 (`cl_livingcity_quick.lua`, sent by `sv_livingcity_quickmore.lua`). Tests: `Nov18_ResistanceFundTests`, `Nov17_AdminPlotTests`, the client smoke test.

- **The fund** is a whole number of credits that belongs to the Resistance and to no player. It is kept with the state of the city (`LC.Ledger`: `fund` in `data/livingcity/<map>_city.json`) and written the moment it changes, so it is there after a restart or a map change. No new data file. It starts at 0.
- **The take, and the split.** Each success below has a take in credits. **Half goes to the fund; the other half is shared equally among the Resistance's players who took part**, each paid as any pay is: "[Credits] +10 (your share)". What does not divide equally stays with the fund (three sharing 20 have 6 each, and the fund 22). When the city's own fighters did it with no player of ours in it, the fund has its half and the other half is the fighters' own. **The take is on top of what the success pays or gives already: no reward that was there is changed.**
- **Supplies raided:**
  - **A load pried out of a supply convoy's crate** by a Resistance player (`LivingCitySupplyLooted`): 20 for each load; the one who pried it. A load taken by someone who is not of the Resistance gives nothing.
  - **Our fighters' raid on a supply drop that got loads out of the crate** (`LivingCityRebelOpDone`, "supply": the city's own operation, or a planned one): 40; those who joined the plan and were there (alive, within 1500 units, as the plan itself pays them). A crate already cleaned out, or not down yet, is no success.
  - **A crate of the Union's supplies stolen and brought home** (the board job `res_supplyrun`, finished): 30; everyone on the job.
- **People saved.** Someone saved "donates" a small sum: 10 to 30 credits, random, whoever they are. **A player who is saved is never charged for it: nothing leaves their pockets.**
  - **A prisoner out of the cells** (`LivingCityBreakout`): whoever got the cell open and those on that job with them (`res_breakout`). Let out by the fighters' own operation, with nobody named: those who joined the plan and were there, once the operation says it worked. The one let out never shares in their own rescue.
  - **A vortigaunt cut loose by a Resistance player** (`LivingCityVortFreed`: one of the city's street crews, or a player in shackles): the one who cut. The city's own team freeing a vortigaunt nobody came for gives nothing.
  - **A citizen broken out and led to safety, a fugitive brought home, a shackled vortigaunt led home** (the board jobs `res_jailbreak`, `res_rescue` and `res_freevort`, finished): everyone on the job.
- **Not counted** (no supplies taken, nobody saved, or nobody's doing): a data terminal raided, a screen hijacked, an Overwatch landing ambushed; the supply run from a contact (`res_supply`), smuggling, scavenging; a prisoner who slips away from a march by themselves; someone uncuffed; someone healed or got back on their feet.
- **What it pays for, for now:** only the leader's order to kill the Administrator (below). The owner also means it to supply the Resistance: that is not built yet.
- **The order's price.** Each member the leader sends for (F2 > Resistance > **Order: kill the Administrator (300 each)**) costs `lc_resistance_order_cost` credits (300; 0 makes it free), charged at the moment they are sent for: **out of the fund when it holds enough; otherwise out of the leader's own credits** (all of it from one or the other); when neither can pay, nobody is sent for and the leader reads one line, "Neither the fund nor you can pay 300 credits." A leader who picks themselves pays the same. Nothing else is said, to anyone.
  - **It comes back** when the order ends before that member has made the attempt: taken back by the leader, the member arrested, someone new, out of the Resistance or below a Fighter, an hour gone by sent for and never spoken with, the leader gone with nobody leading. It goes back where it came from: the fund, or the leader who paid it, if they are still that person, of the Resistance and playing; else the fund. What was paid is what goes back, whatever the setting says by then.
  - **Made, the attempt spends it for good.**
  - Where it came from is kept with the order (PData `lc_adminplot_price`: `300:fund`, or `300:<the leader's SteamID64>:<their citizen number>`), so a reconnect or a restart of the server loses nothing.
  - **The job on the board (`res_regicide`, while nobody leads) costs nothing.**
- **Half after the first.** At one address the first under orders whose attempt counts (the job's holder, or a member under the order) is paid in full: 250 experience, 100 credits, and 250 more if she dies of it. Anyone else under the leader's order whose attempt counts at that same address is paid half of each part: 125, 50 and 125. Her next address starts again. The job from the board is never halved. (`docs/features/city.md`, "An ordered attempt is a coordinated attack".)
- **What players see, and nothing more:** on F2 > Resistance, one row for every member, "Fund: 120 credits", under the page's entries and above the fighters left; the price on the leader's entry; the one refusal; and "your share" beside the credits of whoever took part. No chat line, notice or radio line when the fund grows or is spent. Since 19 November every job on the boards that feeds the fund says, beside its pay, what it can put in ("fund +5-15") and counts its share in the credits it says (`docs/features/jobs-and-quests.md`; the owner: "this means all players feel responsible for helping grow the resistance fund").
- **For other code:** `LC.ResistanceFund()` (how much), `LC.ResistanceFundAdd(n, why)` (n more; `why` is for the server's log), `LC.ResistanceFundTake(n, why)` (true, or false when it holds less), `LC.ResistanceFundSuccess(kind, players)` (a success counted: `"load"`, `"raid"`, `"run"` or `"saved"`), `LC.AdminPlotCost()` (the price). For the job boards: `LC.ResistanceFundJobKind(id)` (how a finished job is counted, or nil), `LC.ResistanceFundStepKinds` (a job step that is a success each time: a load pried out, a collared vortigaunt cut loose), `LC.ResistanceFundShares(kind, n)` (the least and the most the fund keeps and each of n has). Every movement of the fund is one line in the server's log (the debug report).
- **Settings:** `lc_resistance_order_cost` (300). The takes and the split are constants at the top of `sv_livingcity_resistancefund.lua`; the half for later shooters is `LATER` in `sv_livingcity_adminplot.lua`.

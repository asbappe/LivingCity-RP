# The Resistance Insider and the loyalty test

**The Resistance Insider** (designed with the owner, 31 October). A unit who stays inside as the Resistance's spy (The Man Inside) gets their own menu, which only they see: C > Civil Protection > Inside. Each only works where nobody can see or hear them (no other player in sight within `lc_insider_privacy`, 700, or within 300; no unit watching), so they have to slip away first:
- **Tip off Freq 7**: a message on the Resistance's band, unsigned, with the next planned sweep and how many BOLOs are out (once every 2 minutes).
- **Lose a record**: a wanted person isn't wanted any more. Costs 25 Civil Standing: they look careless, never disloyal.
- **Supplies for a dead drop**: at the Civil Protection Commissary, two bandages and a box of pistol rounds that aren't issued, so they can be passed on (once every 10 minutes; -10 standing).
- **Copy the records**: "Copied city records", the best intel (the quartermaster pays 60; once every 15 minutes).
- **Reveal myself**: the codeword, to the one person you're looking at.
Ordinary Resistance players aren't told who the Insider is; the Resistance's top two ranks hear when one is recruited and when they come on. **Caught** (the Mole Hunt): that character is wanted and hunted with no surrendering, can never join Civil Protection, any Combine role or the city administration again, can't take Union jobs, and is Resistance for good. Opening Combine doors for a raid needs nothing new: units already can. `lc_insider` 0 turns it off.

**The loyalty test** (the owner, 1 November). When an Insider's suspicion gets too high, the Mole Hunt starts in the cells (a map with no cells has the old Internal Affairs briefing instead): a Resistance prisoner in front of you, your weapons taken (your fists only), and two Nova Prospekt guards with shotguns either side of you. **With Resistance players in the city, the prisoner is one of them**: the test waits (up to `lc_loyalty_wait`, 15 minutes) for a Resistance player to be caught and put in a cell, and meanwhile Civil Protection quietly goes looking (a unit near a Resistance player stops and searches them 90% of the time). The moment one is in a cell, the Insider is pulled into the loyalty test with that player in front of them, held still; both are told on screen that the other is a real person, and the prisoner that they can beg, bargain or refuse to talk. Nobody caught in time: an NPC prisoner, as before. Beat answers out of him (six punches; he stays on his feet) and then kill him, and your suspicion is cleared and your weapons handed back. Refuse, leave the cell, raise a hand to a guard or run out of time, and the guards shoot you down: you're left bleeding on the cell floor, carried out, and made a **stalker**. **Killing him has consequences, and there's no ignoring them**: the Resistance shuts you out (no Inside menu, no Inside Work), and **The reckoning** starts by itself a few seconds later (whatever job you were on is dropped). Your contact wants answers, and you give one of three: **pay the blood debt** (it starts at once: free one of theirs from the cells and walk them to safety, or on a map without cells get seized supplies back to them), which lets you back in; **take off the mask for good** (you leave Civil Protection and become an ordinary Resistance fighter, never a unit or anyone's man inside again; not wanted for it); or **tell her you're done with the Resistance**: the Combine want proof first (**Proof**, which starts by itself: go in with a sweep team on the Resistance's ground and put down two of their fighters), and then put you through the soldier conversion whether you like it or not (the transhuman conditioning and the Soldier rank, for good).

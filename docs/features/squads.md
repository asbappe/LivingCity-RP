# Squads: sizes, leading units, backup by radio

Asked for by the owner, 9 November: "if a resistance human player has a freq7 radio, can there be an option both in the SWEP and in their F2 menu to call for backup? then it pulls NPCs out of a rebel zone/territory and spawns them nearby ... also, can we have scaling squad sizes for resistance and metrocops/overwatch/combine? ... this limitation needs to be somehow inferred/shown to the player when those limits are reached and in effect." Code: `sv_livingcity_squads.lua` (with `sv_livingcity_rebels.lua`, `sv_livingcity_quickmore.lua`, `sv_livingcity_war.lua`, the radio `weapon_lc_radio.lua`). Tests: `SquadSizeTests`.

**How many NPCs one player may lead** (`LC.SquadLimit`, `LC.SquadSteps`) depends on the human players on their side, the Resistance or the Combine (Civil Protection and Overwatch together): 1 or 2 players, 5 each; 3 or 4, 3; 5 or 6, 2; 7 and more, 1. The point: with more players about, they team up with each other. Two leaders always lead at least 4 (`LC.SquadLeaderMin`): the Resistance's Commander, and Overwatch's Elite Soldiers (and the Overwatch Commander). Before, a Resistance squad was 3 and a unit's backup 3, whatever the numbers.

**When the limit is in effect, the player knows.** F2's "Call for backup" says "(squad 2 of 3)" for both sides. Asking one more NPC to follow, or calling backup with a full squad, says "Your squad is full: 3 of 3 (5 players in the Resistance: the more of you, the smaller each squad, so team up with them)" (or, for a leader, that they always lead at least 4). When players join and the limit drops below what someone already leads, nobody is sent away: they keep their squad, can't add to it until they're under the new limit, and get a notice saying so; when it rises, a notice says that too.

**Backup needs a radio, and works from the radio itself.** The radio transmitter's left click calls for backup on the band it's on: Freq 7 (the Resistance's call, below) or the CP net (a unit's "Call for backup"). A Resistance player needs a Freq 7 radio to call, from F2 too.

**The Resistance's backup comes out of rebel territory.** Idle fighters nearby (within about 4000 units) come first, as many as the squad has room for. If that isn't enough, idle fighters in rebel territory that nobody can see leave it: each turns up out of sight 700 to 2600 units from the caller, on the territory's side of them, somewhere with a way through to them (`LC.RebelBackupSpot`), runs in and joins the squad. The caller is told how many, and how many come from the territory.

**Combine players lead units too.** A Civil Protection or Overwatch player presses E on one of the city's units: "Follow me" (`LC.UnitRecruit`); E again sends him back to his duties. Metrocops and up lead metrocops; Overwatch ranks lead soldiers; an Elite follows only the Overwatch Commander (nobody else can recruit one, and Elites don't answer anyone else's backup call). Units on other orders (a garrison, a supply guard, a prison guard, an escort, a scripted event, a quest, a fight) say so. A recruited unit is in the same squad as backup that answered a call (with you, on whatever you fight), for an hour or until sent back, the leader leaves the Combine, dies or leaves. A unit already in someone's squad doesn't answer another unit's call.

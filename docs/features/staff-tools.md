# Staff tools

**Safeguards for a public server.** A player's game can send the server messages (from windows, menus and chat) as fast as it likes, so the server keeps everyone to a sensible pace: each kind of message `lc_net_rate` a second (10, in bursts of up to `lc_net_burst`, 25), all of them together four times that; anything more is ignored, and someone who keeps at it (`lc_net_kick`, 400 ignored in ten seconds) is kicked, with a line in the staff log. Chat is `lc_chat_flood` lines in three seconds (6), then "Slow down". Things dropped from your pockets: `lc_drops_kept` (30) of your own on the ground at once, then the oldest is cleared away. Two people pressing E on the same thing at the same moment: only one gets it. Nothing at the Resistance lab costs less than it sells back for (no credits out of thin air), and cuffs sell for 1 (units never buy them: their own never run out). A new name and face (the registration form) can't be had while you're wanted or in custody. The day's log file is written every few seconds instead of once a line. Staff are never held back by any of it, and nobody playing normally comes near it. Admin-only menus, settings and commands already check who's asking, building (props, physgun, tools, noclip) is for build mode only, and no player can name a file the server reads or writes.

`!staff` opens the staff panel (admins).

- **Logs.** Chat (in and out of character), kills and who did them, arrests, wanted, warnings, tickets, staff actions and connections. The last 2000 lines are in the panel, and every line also goes to `garrysmod/data/livingcity/logs/<date>.txt`.
- **Tickets.** A player asks for help with `@ <message>` (or `!ticket <message>`). Staff online hear it; the panel lists open tickets to claim, answer, go to, bring the player, and close. Another `@` adds to your open ticket (one every three seconds, thirty to a ticket, so nobody can flood staff).
- **Warnings.** `!warn <name> <reason>`. The player sees it across their screen; it's kept (`garrysmod/data/livingcity/warnings.json`) and listed in the panel, where it can be struck off.
- **Character bans.** `!charban <name> <reason>`: that person, not the player, is gone for good. The character is wiped as if they'd died, and nobody can take the name again. Lifted from the panel.

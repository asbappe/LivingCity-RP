# Survival, social credit and your characters

- **Hunger and thirst** drain while the city runs. At zero you start dying. On the quiet HUD the bars show once either is under half (or for a moment after you eat or drink). Prisoners are fed; Overwatch and the Infested don't eat.
- **Pockets** (F3): eat, drink, cook, fill, drop. What you carry is limited by **weight and size**: `lc_carry_kg` (12 kg) and `lc_carry_bulky` (1 large thing: a shotgun, an SMG, a uniform, a smuggled crate, a melon). A **satchel** (Commissary, black market) adds 8 kg and one more large thing while you have it. Carry more than you can manage (say, after putting the satchel down) and you're slowed until you drop something. Weights are in `LCS.Weights` in `gamemodes/livingcity/gamemode/modules/shared/sh_livingcity_shop.lua`. `lc_inventory_weight 0` goes back to counting items (`lc_inventory_size`, 12).
- **Places to keep things.** Looking at a bin or dumpster, your pockets screen shows what's in it: hide things there, and anyone who looks can take them (a dead drop; thrown out after `lc_bin_minutes`, 40). **Lockers** are placed with the editor ("Fixture: locker"): press E, put something in and it's yours, locked to everyone else; give it up when it's empty. A **storage crate** (Commissary) is set down from your pockets ("Set down") and opens only for you; pack it up when it's empty (two each, `lc_crates_per_person`). Units can open any locker or crate: they see what's in it and confiscate the contraband, and the owner is wanted and it goes on their record. Lockers and crates keep their contents across restarts (`<map>_storage.json`). Capacity: `lc_bin_kg` 10, `lc_locker_kg` 25, `lc_crate_kg` 35.
- **Water:** wade in and press E to drink. Untreated water can give you **parasites** (hungrier, thirstier, coughing; each cough costs health, never armor).
- **Toilets.** Parasites also build an **urge** (`lc_urge_minutes`, 6, from nothing to can't-hold-it; a line under your needs says how it's going, and What now? points to the nearest toilet). Sit on a toilet (E on it: the map's own, or the editor's "Fixture: toilet"; eight seconds) and it's gone, for now (a splash, then the flush: your own recording, if you put one at `garrysmod/sound/livingcity/flush1.wav`), but it **dehydrates you**: drink something after. Only antiparasitic tablets end it for good. **Leave it too long and you soil yourself**: a stain on your trousers and a mess on the ground under you. Then you're **filthy** until you've done all three: the toilet, **a wash** (E on a bathtub or a shower, the map's or the editor's "Fixture: bathtub"; ten seconds) and **clean clothes** (spare citizen clothes or Union overalls from your pockets: put them on, or What now? > Change into clean clothes). Filthy, you stink: flies follow you, the city's own people step back from you, players near you are told why, and anyone who stays close may catch your parasites (`lc_stink_spread`, 3% every five seconds). The **mess on the ground** stays until it's scrubbed: E on it (five seconds; a Sanitation Worker is paid 5 credits, and is told when there's a new one; anyone who does it risks catching something), or the city's own sanitation crew walks over and scrubs it. `lc_hygiene 0` turns it all off.
- **Sinks** the map has work with E even when the map gives them nothing solid to aim at (many do); one the other side of a wall doesn't.
- **Sleep.** Everyone needs a night's sleep, once in every in-game day (`lc_tired_days`, 1). Go without and you're **tired**: your eyelids keep drooping shut (all the way, a moment, then open with a start), the world swims a little, you can't run, and you've 25 less health at most until you sleep. **Sleep in a bed** (E on it: the editor's "Fixture: bed", a bunk point, or any bed or mattress the map has, a cell's too) at night, or any time you're exhausted: you lie still (you can't move; **Jump** gets you up), the screen dark, and heal as you sleep, to full by the end of `lc_sleep_seconds` (60); then you stay asleep till you press Jump. Get up before then, or get hurt, and you've not slept. The drooping eyelids have soft edges, and the world blurs as they close. How long you've been awake is kept over a server restart. **Lights out** (the owner, 8 November: "all human players get sleepy/exhausted at the same time and all need to sleep"; "the administrator needs to go to bed just like citizens every night. Even in nightmares. Same for administration staff"; `sv_livingcity_bedtime.lua`, `lc_bedtime` 1): every player (citizens, the Resistance, Civil Protection, the Administration and its staff, vortigaunts; not a stalker or the infested) is due in bed at the good night (21:00), whatever they slept in the day, warned about six and two real minutes before ("Lights out soon", "Lights out"); past it, out of bed, they're tired until they sleep. They're done for the night once they've slept after the good night, or woken from tonight's dream, or (on a night with no dream) slept since dusk. The Administration now gets tired like everyone (before, it never did). The only ways round it: a stimulant shot (lights out waits while it lasts; one only, until you've slept and dreamt properly), or a unit's night shift (their board, F4). **Woken in your sleep** (the owner, 8 November): hurt while asleep, you wake, as before; dreaming, your sleeping body stays in the bed (a still copy, eyes shut), and if it's hit you're pulled out of the dream and wake in your bed, taking the hit. Civil Protection or Overwatch (players or the city's own units) firing within `lc_raid_wake_range` (700) of rebel ground, or hurting anyone on it, wakes everyone asleep there, players (out of a dream too) and the camp's own people, so a raid can't catch a camp in its sleep. Anyone woken like that has `lc_startled_minutes` (10) before lights out can tire them. **Hunger and thirst wait while you sleep or dream**: they don't drop, and never hurt or wake you (nor does the parasites' cough) until you're up. **No chip** (pulled at the lab): a night's sleep takes at most `lc_chipless_sleep` (90) seconds and you get up on your own (no dream to wait for). A good night leaves you **well rested** for the next in-game day: hunger and thirst come on a quarter more slowly. You can sleep again 18 in-game hours after a night's sleep (the owner, 7 November); before that, the bed says about how many in-game hours are left ("You slept not long ago. You could sleep again in about 4 in-game hours."). What now? tells you when you need sleep and finds a bed. **Warned first** (the owner, 7 November): a notice about six real minutes before you're exhausted ("Getting tired") and another about two minutes before ("Very tired"), and **from the first one on a bed lets you sleep**, day or night. **A citizen keeps the city's hours:** tired at the soonest three quarters of the way through `lc_tired_days`, and then only in the evening or at night (21:00 to 5:00); only someone who went a quarter more than `lc_tired_days` without sleep (a night skipped) gets exhausted in the daytime. Civil Protection, the Resistance and the rest just count the time awake, as before. Exhaustion never starts in the middle of a fight (hurt by or hurting anyone, or firing, in the last 30 seconds): it waits till the fighting's over, five minutes at most. The eyelids are drawn under the rest of the screen, so a job's objective and its marker stay visible, and on a job the job stays your next step on screen (sleep is still in F1 > What now?). `lc_sleep 0` turns it off. An admin holding the Living City editor tool is never tired, and the time it's out doesn't count as time awake (the owner, 7 November). They never wear off by themselves: only antiparasitic tablets from a medic or the black market dealer clear them. A sink only counts if you can see it: a sink behind a wall (the other side of a ladder, say) is not one you can drink from.
- **Breen's Private Reserve** from the vending machines quenches thirst and leaves you dizzy. Sometimes you forget what you were doing, and so does your quest tracker. You keep the empty can.
- **Cigarettes, alcohol and stimulants** (the owner, 7 November; `sv_livingcity_vices.lua`). Pockets (F3), the item's button: Smoke, Drink or Take. **Cigarettes:** Union cigarettes (Commissary, 5 for 12 credits, standing 75, citizens) or hand-rolled (black market, 5 for 10, contraband). A cigarette steadies the shakes, takes the edge off hunger (+5, hand-rolled +10) and dries your mouth (thirst -3, -6); hand-rolled are harsher (more coughing). Three inside ten minutes: a coughing fit (4 health, 12 thirst). Anyone nearby sees the smoke. **Alcohol:** homebrew beer (black market 8, contraband; leaves the bottle; +8 thirst), pre-war vodka (Commissary 45 at standing 150, citizens; black market 70), moonshine (black market 10, contraband; one in five a bad batch: 15 health and a few seconds blind). Each drink adds to how drunk you are (beer 1, vodka 2, moonshine 3), wearing off by one every two minutes, and gives back a little health (3 a strength). Tipsy (2 and up): a tenth less from every hit. Drunk (4 and up): the screen sways and blurs, no running. Past 7: you throw up (25 hunger, 30 thirst) and drop to 3. Spirits make you thirstier (vodka -6, moonshine -10). **Stimulant shot** (black market 35, contraband): 15 minutes when you can't fall asleep, so no dream (not even a nightmare) can take you; it steadies the shakes, costs 8 thirst, and the screen is sharper. A second while the first is in you: 10 health. When it wears off: 3 minutes too heavy to run. **After a dream you played** (the owner, 8 November: nightmares are the Administrator's punishment, so nothing the Combine would give, no credits, standing or experience): **won**, fully healthy, plus for one in-game day **Unbroken** (Breen's water can't fog you or make you forget; no nightmare can take you) and **Second wind** (stamina drains at 60%, hunger and thirst at 75%); a citizen also gets **a Vortigaunt's whisper** (a black market dealer or a rebel cache, at random, marked on screen for 10 minutes; nothing if the map has neither), the Resistance **a Vortigaunt's gift** (3 hand-rolled cigarettes, a moonshine or a stimulant shot). **Still in it when the time ran out:** nothing either way. **Lost every life:** 30 off your health limit until your next full night's sleep (one begun after the nightmare; dying and respawning doesn't clear it, nor does reconnecting), and 3 minutes of a trembling, colourless view (a cigarette or a stimulant steadies it).
- **A full stomach** (hunger 85 or more): jobs and shifts give 15% more XP. `lc_fullstomach 0` turns it off.
- **Rain** washes blood, scorch marks and bullet holes off open ground near you while it rains (`lc_rain_wash`, 1; never graffiti, and nothing under a roof).
- **Combine turrets on a walkway:** the city's people route round them (any step of a path within reach of a turret is left out when there's another way).
- **Healing** comes only from sleeping (in a bed), eating (food mends you as you eat it, a quarter of what it fills you: a ration, 40 hunger, is 10 health; drinks don't) and medicine (medkits, bandages, the clinic, Father Grigori). Nobody mends on their own while awake.
- **Bottles and cans:** fill them in water, then boil them over a stove or fire.
- **Hunting:** birds and headcrabs leave carcasses. Cook them at a stove or burning barrel (any stove, oven or fire already on the map works too). Raw meat is a gamble.
- **Stoves, sinks and vending machines** the map already has work as fixtures, including ones baked into the map as static props: the game reads their positions straight from the map file. Stand by a stove to cook (F3). Press E at a sink you are looking at to drink (a small parasite risk) and fill bottles and cans from your pockets, then boil them. Press E at a vending machine to buy a can. The map's **dumpsters and bins** are read the same way: jobs send you to real ones (the Union bin round, and "Something you saw", where two people are swapping a bundle behind a dumpster; if the map has none nearby, a dumpster is put out for it), and a can put in one counts as put in the trash. `lc_scanprops` rescans the map and says what it found.
- **Fists:** everyone has them. One punch kills a bird. A headcrab takes several. People barely notice. Don't go AFK in an alley with headcrabs around.
- **Ration dispensers** give a ration packet once per allocation (`lc_ration_interval`, 25 minutes; twice as often during distribution), unless your standing is too low.

## Social credit

**Getting your standing back:** anyone below zero gets **community service** on their board (F4), no job needed: litter sweeps and bin rounds that restore 40 standing each. Serving time also pays it back: a quarter of what you owe plus 100, and 75 more if you turned yourself in. It never takes you past zero, though. Quiet compliance adds 10 every 10 minutes while you're below zero.

**Credits** are money: earned from shifts and jobs (a ration comes with no credits), spent at the Commissary (a ration, a loaf of bread or milk is 15 credits each, from 7 November when the owner asked for food to be scarcer; they were 5 and 8) and vending machines. **Civil Standing** runs from -1000 to 1000: work, compliance and reporting raise it; being wanted, jailed, escaping, curfew violations and conduct strikes lower it. Both are at the top of your pockets (F3), and on the HUD too if the server turns the quiet HUD off. Changes are told in one line once things go quiet for a moment, added up, with what each offence came to ("-150: Assaulting Civil Protection (x5, -125), Murder of a Civil Protection officer (-25)"), not one line per offence. Your tier (Anticitizen, Probationary, Citizen, Trusted, Exemplary) decides what the **Union Commissary** will sell you, from rations and water for everyone, to medkits for the Trusted, to watermelon and real coffee for Exemplary citizens. Units can buy ammunition and batteries. Edit `gamemodes/livingcity/gamemode/modules/shared/sh_livingcity_shop.lua` for items, prices and tiers.

## Your characters

You can have more than one person in the city (`lc_char_slots`, 3). `!characters` lists them: switch to one, start someone new in an empty slot, or delete one you're not playing. Each is a whole life of its own: name, number and face, description, who they know, how long they've lived in the city, credits, standing, faction and rank in every faction, job, pockets, stash, housing block, story and traits, and their Civil Protection record. Switching is like one person walking off and another walking on: you respawn as them. Not while you're wanted, in custody, down, tied up, in a fight or on a job, and not more than once every `lc_char_switch_cooldown` seconds (300; admins can switch freely). Permanent death still applies to whoever you're playing; the others aren't touched. What belongs to you rather than the person stays the same whoever you play: conduct strikes, tips you've seen, whitelists. (A stash is kept per map, and only this map's is swapped.)

**Changes from the 7 November playtest.**
- **The ration line moves:** the citizen at the front walks to a spot on the floor in front of the dispenser (it hangs on the wall, and the old spot was in the air or the wall), collects and goes; if no walk is possible they're served where they stand. During a distribution, a short line calls the nearest idle citizen every 6 seconds.
- **Your ration:** a dock for a nearby Resistance attack takes the next ration, a new character's first one included (the owner, 7 November: no exception for the first). A player at the counter holds the line only while being served, about 5 seconds.
- **Clothes you have on keep their room:** a bought disguise you wear (a CP uniform, say) still counts toward the large-things limit and the weight, so taking it off always puts it back in your pockets, never on the ground (the owner, 7 November).
- **Beds:** a bed is taken only while someone lies in it or is walking to it. A sleeper gets up on the bed's own floor, in view of the bed (no more hallway through the wall, no feet in the ceiling). Citizens and rebels lie as a still copy with eyes shut and no gun showing, the real person hidden beside the bed, so nobody stands on a bed marked "Asleep"; looking at the copy shows their name and "asleep".
- **Meat and fish spoil:** raw and cooked headcrab, bird and fish go off one city day after you got them (even while you're offline), with "spoils in 7h" beside them in your pockets and in lockers and crates. Cooking gives a fresh day.
- **Birds:** a crow leaves a raw crow, a gull a raw gull, a pigeon a raw pigeon (all cook to roast bird). Birds only appear under open sky. There are `lc_birds` (4) about; a bird or roaming headcrab that is killed is replaced only after a minute and a half, one of each kind per 90 seconds at most (the owner, 7 November: food scarcity).
- **Fish:** raw fish is a grey bait pod (HL2 has no fish model), fried fish a takeout carton.
- **A fishing line** lasts 4 to 8 casts, warns when it's fraying, then snaps. A cast takes 25 to 45 seconds and brings up a fish 30% of the time (it was 10 to 22 seconds and 58% until 7 November, the owner: food scarcity).

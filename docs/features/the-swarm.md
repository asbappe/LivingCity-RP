# The Swarm: the city falls to the antlions (`sv_livingcity_swarm.lua`, `sv_livingcity_swarmcore.lua`, `cl_livingcity_swarm.lua`, `entities/lc_swarm_extract.lua`)

The owner, 27 November, on a second way into the chapter [the city falls to the Resistance](chapter-city-falls.md), the Half-Life 2 way: "we'll want the highest ranking player on the server to get the pheropods, and if they leave or disconnect, then the next highest ranking player gets them, and they function just like in HL2: you can control antlions and the mission becomes 'disable the thumpers' and then once the player with the pheropods is inside the city, antlions function just like in Nova Prospekt and spawn near the pheropod equipped player and attack the combine/metrocops automatically and just like in HL2: throwing a pheropod on an NPC or player spawns extra antlions that then swarm the target and any other enemies nearby. And have it on by default ... then they can turn purple like in HL2 and start chanting ... also the antlion nest needs to have the EP2 antlion guardian spawn and come straight out of the gate when it is opened and another when the extract/honeycomb prop thing where the essence is is reached and harvested." And later that day: the pheropods destroyed once the city is taken, "so that they don't eternally have access to infinite antlion armies ... they would need a quest to re-enable the combine thumpers"; the Combine get rid of the extract simply ("they just confiscate it ... destroy it if it's in their pockets, if it's on the ground and they shoot it, or if they kick/stomp/hit it"); the ritual's five conditions; and "killing the glowing antlion guardian" as what ends the hive's hunt.

The lore: the Resistance's freed vortigaunts make pheropods (bugbait, as at Nova Prospekt) from the nest's larval extract (Episode Two); with the city's thumpers stopped, the antlions overrun the Combine. **The rocket's way stays as it is.**

**It is on: `lc_swarm` is 1** (the owner's choice). With 0 nobody can harvest the extract, no ritual starts and the city does not fall to the swarm by itself; the door's guardians and the map's own wiring still run, and staff's lines (below) still work.

It works only on a map with the forest cave's nest (`_dev/tools/map_forest.py`, `docs/dev/map-building.md`) and, for the mission, the canal ring's thumpers and, where the map has one, the suppression core under the Nexus (below); on a map without these names nothing of it happens.

## What happens, step by step

1. **The emergency door's guardian.** When the nest's emergency door opens (the map's relay `lc_nest_opened`), an Episode Two cavern guardian (`npc_antlionguard`, the cavern breed as Garry's Mod's own "Antlion Guardian") stands on Valve's planks just behind the shutter, at the door's own floor (never down in the cleft), and as soon as the shutter is high enough (4 seconds) it charges out through the doorway at whoever opened it (whoever pressed one of the two buttons last). The shutter's opening, 192 wide and 152 high when up, takes the guardian (80 by 100). This one may charge: Garry's Mod's "Antlion Guardian" is also "in cavern", which stops it charging, so this one is not. Never two from the door at once, and none for 10 minutes after one dies. It comes whatever `lc_swarm` says.
2. **The harvest.** Anyone but the Combine holds E on the larval extract (the map's `lc_nest_extract`, the essence floating over Valve's honeycomb) for 5 seconds, looking at it from within 300 units (the essence has no collision of its own: E on it is a look). No bar: the essence brightens as it is drawn out and a sound rises with it. The nest wakes: the grubs squeal, the ground stirs, and two antlions dig up round the harvester. Let go or look away and it stops. At 5 seconds the essence is gone from the honeycomb, the harvester carries it (glowing), and **a second guardian bursts up out of the chamber's floor** (this one Episode Two's own way: poison, no charge) and goes for them. Once for each harvest.
3. **The hunt.** For as long as that guardian lives, the hive hunts the essence wherever it is (carried, dropped, or in a vortigaunt's hands, even at the headquarters): antlions dig up near it, behind the carrier, every 15 to 20 seconds, 4 out at most, and go for whoever has it first. Kill the glowing guardian and the hive's claim is broken: no more come, and those left dig away half a minute later.
4. **Fragile.** The carrier dies or leaves the server: the extract lies glowing where they fell (`lc_swarm_extract`). Anyone not Combine picks it up with E. It is destroyed, with a wet burst and no words, when:
   - **it is in a Combine player's pockets:** a Combine player's E on it on the ground; a unit's search, pat-down or cuffs on the carrier; a carrier who joins the Combine;
   - **the Combine hurt it on the ground:** shot, struck, blown up (damage from Combine players or Combine NPCs), or a unit's boot on it. Anyone else's damage does nothing to it, so a rebel's stray shot does not throw the run away.
   - **Combine NPCs who see it go for it:** units and soldiers within 1500 units who can see it drop what they are doing, shoot it where they have a clear shot (an invisible `npc_bullseye` on it, which only they are told to hate) and run to it otherwise; whichever reaches it first ends it.
   Destroyed, or left untouched for 10 minutes, it is back in the nest (the essence shows again).
5. **The keeper.** The carrier hands it to a freed vortigaunt with E. The vortigaunt holds it as Episode Two's does (ep2_outland_02: the sequences `nectar_recieve`, then `idle_nectar`, the little essence on its `nectar` attachment) and stays put until the ritual. If it dies, the extract falls where it stood, with the rules above.
6. **The ritual.** A Resistance player's E on that vortigaunt starts it once **all of these** are true; otherwise one short line says what is still missing ("Still needed: ..."), and nothing else happens:
   - the Resistance's headquarters is live at the mine (its territory is that of the cave map's `lc_hq_setup_cave`, Ravenholm's big room);
   - at least 3 freed vortigaunts, alive (the city's freed ones and Free Vortigaunt players together, as the hive mind counts them);
   - the ledger's Resistance strength at 70 or more;
   - the Resistance holds at least 2 districts;
   - the mine's flag has flown 2 hours of server time (a ransack, a strike or a move starts the count again; overrun at half mast, it stands; kept over restarts);
   - and the city can fall: not locked, not cooling down, not the Resistance's already, no rocket on its way.
   The freed vortigaunts within 1500 units (up to six) gather round the keeper, turn blue-purple (their own `TurnBlue`, and Valve's `turn_blue` and `surgery_sustain_idle` animations where the model has them; without them they walk into the ring) and chant (Half-Life 2's `vortigese` lines) for 15 seconds; Free Vortigaunt players near glow with them. Then the extract is used up and **the pheropods go to the highest-ranking Resistance player online**, not necessarily whoever brought it: a tie goes to whoever brought it, then to the one longest online. **One a map.**
7. **The pheropods** are Half-Life 2's own `weapon_bugbait`, in the holder's hands only: nobody else can pick them up and none is left lying about. Death keeps them (they are in hand again on coming back); leaving the server or the Resistance hands them to the next highest. Thrown, where the pod bursts up to 3 more antlions dig up and the swarm goes for the person or NPC it hit (never one of the Resistance's own), then for any enemy near; thrown at nothing, the swarm goes there and fights what it finds. Squeezed (right click), the swarm comes to the holder. The swarm's antlions are the allies of Resistance players, fighters and vortigaunts, and the enemies of Combine NPCs, units and the Administrator; citizens they leave alone. (Each antlion is told so itself: Half-Life 2's own `antlion_allied` would make every antlion love every player, units too.) While the pheropods are out, the trickle of antlions at a stopped thumper (`wildlife.md`) is the swarm's too, and never comes for the Resistance's own.
8. **The thumpers, then the swarm.** One notice when the pheropods are given (to the holder: "The pheropods are yours. Stop the city's thumpers."; to the rest of the Resistance: who has them). The thumpers are stopped with their own buttons. On a map with the suppression core (below) the core must be stopped as well: with the canal thumpers down and the core still running, nothing new is written; the city's heartbeat goes on, and that tells them. When every thumper on the map is stopped (one more notice to the Resistance: "The thumpers are down. Take the swarm to the Nexus."; on a map with the core, only once the core is stopped too: "The city's defences are down. Take the swarm to the Nexus."), or the map has none, and the holder is in the city (in or within 2500 units of a housing block, a Combine-only zone, the prison or the infestation; a map with none of them: anywhere), antlions come as at Nova Prospekt: they dig up near the holder, up to three at a time every 20 seconds, 6 at most (10 with what thrown pods bring), follow the holder and go for the Combine near them on their own. A thumper started again drives them off near it (the game does that itself) and none come within its reach.
9. **The fall.** The holder in the Nexus (the chapter's: the Combine-only zone with the Administrator's desk) with the swarm near, every thumper down (and the core, where the map has one), and the city able to fall: antlions burst up on the Combine's NPCs and units across the city (16 at most) with dust and the ground shaking for everyone, no words; two and a half seconds later the chapter begins with why `"swarm"`, whatever `lc_chapter` says (that is the rocket's switch). From there the chapter is as it is: units die and come back as before, no character ends, doors open, machines go dead, the districts and the ledger swing; one line in the City Bulletin ("Antlions overran the Combine. The city has fallen to the Resistance."). The Combine NPCs die of claws, not light.
10. **After the fall.** The pheropods are **destroyed for good**: taken from the holder, nobody gets them after that, and none can be made again this map. The antlions are **nobody's now**: the Resistance are their enemies too; those out carry on as wild ones, and the thumper module keeps bringing them at stopped thumpers. Every thumper left stopped is **broken**: its button only fizzles until it is mended; the core's button does the same while any of its consoles is cut. (The same if the rocket's wave takes the city while the pheropods are out.)

## Restart the thumpers (the Resistance's job)

On the Resistance's board (F4) at once when the city falls to the swarm, and for as long as the city is the Resistance's after it and any thumper (or the core) is stopped. For each stopped thumper, nearest first: get to it (its button, marked), mend its wiring (the wiring game), start it with its own button. **The suppression core counts as one of them** (the Resistance hold the city now and the antlions are wild): each of its cut consoles (marked), its wiring, then its button. When every thumper runs, and the core, the job pays (40 plus 20 a thumper, and 60 for the core). Nothing is stood through: antlions may come while you mend, as they do. The robot players do it (`res_thumpers`, with two thumpers and the core). When the Combine take the city back, they see to their own thumpers (nothing stays broken); the core stays as it is, and their own job restarts it (below).

## The suppression core under the Nexus (`sv_livingcity_swarmcore.lua`)

The owner, 27 November, agreed to it: a Combine suppression core under the Nexus, the city's own Citadel-style thumper (in Half-Life 2 the Citadel works as a giant thumper). The machine is Valve's giant digger pistons from `d3_citadel_01`. On a map without `lc_core_machine` nothing of it happens and the Swarm is as above. It works whatever `lc_swarm` says, like the nest's guardians.

- **The city's heartbeat.** While the core runs, everyone in the city (the swarm's own "in the city": in or within 2500 units of a housing block, a Combine-only zone, the prison or the infestation) hears a low, deep thud every 4 to 6 seconds, quiet, with a very faint shake. Nobody in the forests or the caves. When the core stops, the thud stops for everyone: that silence is the message. Nothing is written.
- **The swarm's second gate.** The swarm comes up into the city, and the city falls to it, only with every canal thumper stopped **and** the core stopped. The canals' trickle at a stopped thumper is not the core's (it is as it was). A thrown pheropod still brings its three where it bursts, as before.
- **Shutting it down (the Resistance): sabotage, and it lasts** (the owner, 28 November: "they want it to be permanent, or at least extremely delayed"). Each of the core's three consoles is cut one of two ways only. **A Hacker:** a Resistance player with the multitool, by E within about 110 units of the console's mark, or the multitool's left click there (its own line says "Sabotage the core's console" while they stand at one): the needle game (`hack`: stop the needle in the band four times, never while the scanner looks; seen twice and the alarm goes, the console whole). **An IED:** a blast within 220 units of the console itself (it stands 72 units in front of its mark) wrecks it, whoever set the charge, at any time; it is scorched as well. A cut console dies with a sound and **smokes and sparks for as long as it is cut**, and it stays cut: there is no ten minutes any more. When all three are cut the core winds down: the map's `lc_core_stop` (its beams go, the reactor turns unstable, the pistons stop), a huge wind-down at the machine, the heartbeat stops, and **the whole city goes dark** (the power grid's city-wide blackout, [city-people.md](city-people.md), "The whole city goes dark": the lights out, every Civil Protection door's lock dead; staff's own blackout switch still wins both ways). Any time, the pheropods out or not. Nothing is stood through, nothing paid and no notice (it is no shift's work). A Resistance player without a multitool, a console already cut, or anyone else's E: a dead click.
- **Restarting it (the Combine): extremely delayed.** The Combine can mend a console only **45 minutes after it was cut**; before that their E at it is a dead click. Once the core is down and every cut console can be mended, their board (Civil Protection's and the Overwatch's) has **Restart the suppression core** (`cmb_core`, posted quietly): go down by their way (marked), each cut console (marked) and its wiring (the wiring game), then its button; it pays 70. A Combine player's E at a console that can be mended mends it without the job too, and anyone's press of the button starts the core once all three consoles are whole (the map's `lc_core_start`, a deep start-up at the machine, the heartbeat again a few seconds later, and the city's lights back); with one still cut the button only fizzles, and so does the map's own start. A mended console can be cut again, the same two ways, and waits its 45 minutes again. While the city is the Resistance's, the Combine's job is not offered and their E does nothing; the Resistance mend at once (no wait for them) and start it inside "Restart the thumpers" (above), or by E. Staff's "Core: start it" works at once.
- **Saved** with the Swarm's own file (below): running or not, which consoles are cut, and how long each has been cut (a restart does not shorten the Combine's wait). The map starts with its machine running and its consoles whole: saved stopped, the gamemode triggers `lc_core_stop` again, the cut consoles smoke and spark again (after a map cleanup too) and the city is dark again.
- **A map prop at another size.** The map's reactor core is Valve's, drawn smaller: a prop with the key `lc_scale` (0.05 to 10) is drawn at that size once it has spawned (the map sets the engine's own `modelscale` too; this is the guarantee).

The numbers (4 to 6 seconds, 110, 220, 45 minutes, 70) are constants in `sv_livingcity_swarmcore.lua` (`CORE_BEAT`, `CORE_REACH`, `CORE_BLAST`, `CORE_MEND_AFTER`, `CORE_REWARD`); the 45 minutes is a first guess at "extremely delayed".

## What the map gives it (found by name)

- **The nest** (the forest cave): `lc_nest_door` (the shutter), `lc_nest_opened` (the relay the gamemode hears), `lc_nest_count` (the two buttons' counter: who pressed), `lc_nest_rally` (the holdout's side of the door), `lc_nest_extract` (the essence), the grubs (`npc_antlion_grub`) near it; the mine's `lc_hq_setup_cave`.

- **The suppression core** (under the Nexus): `lc_core_machine` (only that the map has a core, and where: Valve's digger, a `func_tracktrain`, or an `info_target` at it; never driven by the gamemode; the other pistons are `lc_core_digger_2` and `_3`), `lc_core_stop` and `lc_core_start` (logic_relays: everything the machine does, its animation, sound and lamps; the gamemode triggers them and hears them, however they are triggered), `lc_core_panel_1` to `_3` (info_targets 72 units out from the three consoles, facing them: the gamemode puts a cut console's smoke and sparks, and measures an IED's blast, 72 units in front of the mark and 40 up), `lc_core_button` (the main console's button: the gamemode decides what a press does), `lc_core_room` (the chamber's centre: where the big sounds come from), `lc_core_combine_way` (the Combine's way in: their job's first mark), `lc_core_breach` (the Resistance's secret entrance: the map's own, nothing in the gamemode needs it).

## Staff

**F4 > Factions > Admin: testing tools > The city** (superadmins), under the chapter's lines:

- **Swarm: give me the extract**: you carry it (the essence leaves the nest).
- **Swarm: the vortigaunts' ritual now (pheropods to the highest rank)**: at the freed vortigaunt nearest you (straight to the pheropods with none), whatever the conditions.
- **Swarm: the city falls to the swarm now**: the burst and the fall, whatever the lock or the cooldown (not when the city is the Resistance's already).
- **Swarm: end it (pheropods back, antlions away)**: everything back: the pheropods taken, the antlions dig down, the extract in the nest, nothing broken. (The core is left as it is: it has its own two lines.)
- **Core: stop it** and **Core: start it** (only on a map with the core): it winds down with every console cut, as the Resistance leave it (the city goes dark); or it runs again with every console whole, at once. "Core: stop it" pressed again while it is stopped ends the Combine's 45 minutes, so their mending and their job can be tried.
- A line under them says how it stands (where the extract is, what the ritual still needs, the holder and the thumpers, the guardians, the mine's flag, and the core: running or stopped, how many consoles are cut or whole, and in how many minutes the Combine can mend).

There is no console command.

## Saved

`data/livingcity/<map>_swarm.json` (through the safe file helpers): the mine's flag clock, written every minute while it runs, when it starts again and at shutdown; and the suppression core (`core`: `running`, `cut` (which consoles), `cutAge` (for each of those, the seconds since it was cut); an older file without `cutAge` is read with its consoles cut as of now), written when it changes and at shutdown. Everything else starts afresh with the map (a restart brings the extract back to the nest; the map's thumpers run again).

## Guessed, or not seen in the real game

Nothing here has run in Garry's Mod: it is tested in the offline stub only. To check in the game:

- **The guardians:** that `cavernbreed` alone gives the glowing cavern look, that one with `incavern` 0 charges when told (`SetChargeTarget "!activator !activator"`, and a chase), and that it fits out through the shutter as the numbers say. Whether the chamber's one digs up (`startburrowed`, `Unburrow`) and finds room near the harvester.
- **The essence:** that E on it (a look within 300 units) feels right with the honeycomb round it; the rising sound is the vortigaunt's charge (`npc/vort/health_charge.wav`) at rising pitches; the grubs' squeal is a guess by name (`NPC_Antlion_Grub.Alert`).
- **Antlions digging up:** Valve's own `startburrowed` and `Unburrow`, as the thumper module uses; the spots are found by traces near whoever has the essence.
- **The dropped extract:** Episode Two's essence three times its size in a box that shots and E find; whether a kick or a baton swing reaches such a box (a unit walking onto it bursts it anyway); the wet burst (`BloodImpact` in the antlion's colour, `physics/flesh/flesh_squishy_impact_hard`).
- **Combine NPCs going for it:** that an `npc_bullseye` that is not solid and takes no damage draws their fire into the extract (Valve's card: "an NPC attacking the bullseye will do so with perfect accuracy").
- **The keeper's animations:** the sequence names come from Valve's ep2_outland_02 and ep2_outland_04 (`nectar_recieve`, `idle_nectar`, `turn_blue`, `turn_blue_vort1` to `3`, `surgery_sustain_idle`, the `nectar` attachment); Garry's Mod's vortigaunt model may be Half-Life 2's, without them, and then the vortigaunt only stands (they are looked for, never assumed). The drip particle (`larval_extract_drip`) and Valve's sound `outland_04.vort_remove_extract_fx` are guesses by name; the glow in its hands is ours (a light).
- **`TurnBlue`:** the vortigaunt's Episode Two input for its blue-purple skin; whether Garry's Mod's vortigaunt has it.
- **The pheropods:** `weapon_bugbait` thrown makes `npc_grenade_bugbait`, which takes itself away where it lands (that is where the swarm comes); whether Half-Life 2's own response to it (antlions running to where it lands) also happens without `antlion_allied`. From memory of Valve's code, `antlion_allied` makes each antlion like every player online when it starts, which is why it is not used.
- **The fall:** the dust (`ThumperDust`) and the shake; the rumble is Half-Life 2's thunder.
- **The numbers** are first guesses for the owner to change (the ritual's five are the owner's).
- **The core's sounds (guessed):** the heartbeat is the thumper's own thud (`ambient/machines/thumper_hit.wav`) at pitch 46 to 52 and a quarter of its volume, played at the listener, with a shake of 0.5 for 0.8 seconds: whether it is deep enough, and quiet enough never to annoy. The wind-down is the thumper's shutdown (`thumper_shutdown1.wav`, level 120, pitch 55) with `ambient/machines/spindown.wav` (level 110, pitch 60) and a shake near the machine; the start-up the thumper's own start-up and `spinup.wav` at pitch 60. A console cut: `ambient/energy/powerdown2.wav`; mended or whole again: `buttons/combine_button1.wav`; nothing to do: `buttons/combine_button_locked.wav`. The big sounds come from `lc_core_room` (else the machine's middle) through `sound.Play`; the map's own relays may add their own.
- **The core's map piece:** that the relays stop and start everything the machine does, that the map starts with the machine running, and that 110 units of a console's mark is a sensible reach for E.
- **The core's sabotage (28 November, guessed):** how the smoke and sparks look at a cut console (an `env_spark` and a small dark `env_smokestack`, 72 units in front of the console's mark and 40 up: whether that is on the console), the scorch on a blown one, whether 220 units is the right reach for an IED and 45 minutes the right wait, and how the needle game's scanner looks in the chamber.

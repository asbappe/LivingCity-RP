# Vortigaunts, and day, night and weather

**Freed vortigaunts** live in rebel territory: `lc_rebel_vorts` (1) of them from the start, more when the Resistance free one (the "Free a vortigaunt" job). They charge a Resistance vest when asked (E). **Shackled vortigaunts** are their own faction ("Vortigaunt", ranks Laborer, Trusted Laborer, Overseer's Hand): Combine property, working the Union's shifts like citizens, with a collar that holds their vortessence in (the Vortessence weapon only hums). A Resistance Member or above can cut the shackles: `!unshackle`, looking at one up close, ten seconds, with no unit watching. The vortigaunt joins the Resistance as a **Free Vortigaunt** (two at a time): left click throws a bolt of vortessence (25 shock damage, a three-second recharge), right click charges the powered vest of the person in front of you, or your own (25, every 15 seconds; it charges a vest, it doesn't heal flesh), and Civil Protection hunt it on sight outside rebel territory. Vortigaunts use the vortigaunt model with its own standing, walking and running animations; strangers see "a shackled vortigaunt" or "a vortigaunt" rather than a name. The "Free a vortigaunt" job: it waits at the work site shackled and harmless, with its keepers. Cut the shackles there (they may see), or walk it out still shackled as if you were moving Combine property (a unit may stop you and ask where you're taking it: then it's a fight), and the shackles come off in rebel territory, any of it.

## Day, night and weather

- **Night maps.** On a map that's always night (its sky says so: `lc_daynight -1`, the default, decides from the map's sky; `lc_daynight 0` forces it), the map's lighting is left alone, the screen isn't tinted, and there's no overcast weather (it comes as fog instead). The clock still runs, for curfews and the like.
- **Day and night.** A city day lasts `lc_day_minutes` (60) real minutes, with the clock on everyone's HUD. Night (21:00 to 05:00) is dark: the map's own lighting dims at dawn, day, dusk and night (`lc_daynight_light`; clients have a short hitch when it changes, four times a day), and the screen is tinted. Curfew is three times as likely at night (21:00 to 05:00, not at dusk: `LC.IsDeepNight`; bedtime and the rest count dusk as night too, `LC.IsNight`), rations half as likely.
- **The city's day.** The city keeps to the clock (`lc_daily_rhythm`, on). Morning (05:00 to 07:00): people come out and go to work, about seven in ten of `lc_citizens` out. The work day (07:00 to 12:00) and afternoon (14:00 to 18:00): everyone's out and most of the employed are at their jobs. Midday (12:00 to 14:00): the ration lines fill up. Evening (18:00 to 21:00): work winds down, people sit, talk and wander. Night (21:00 to 05:00): most go home, `lc_night_population` (40%) of `lc_citizens` stay out, and only the night shift works (maintenance, radio repair, the mortuary, the clinic, sanitation). When there are more people out than the hour calls for, some head home. The HUD clock says what time of day it is.
- **Weather.** Clear, overcast, fog, rain or a storm, changing by itself every so often (`lc_weather_auto`). Rain and storms fall around you outdoors (thin streaks, lots of them), with the sound (30% quieter since 7 November: the owner found it far too loud), and a mist closes in around you while you're out under the open sky, clearing again a couple of seconds after you step under cover; storms bring thunder and lightning (the flash only where you can see the sky; indoors you just hear the thunder); fog closes in. (Rain no longer fills bottles and cans: the owner asked. Rainwater already carried still drinks.)
- **Music.** The city's music now and then: calm by day, tense under curfew or yellow sociostability, urgent in a fight or a red one. A lockdown starts its music the moment it begins. Urgent music (an alarm, a fight) fades in about five seconds after it starts. **Civil Protection and the Overwatch's soldiers get theirs too** (the owner, 7 November: "as the combine nothing plays at all"): in a firefight, on a violent job, in a raid, a sweep or a lockdown, or near a skirmish, Half-Life 2's combat pieces play the same way a wanted rebel's urgent music does (in about five seconds, then the usual quiet between pieces). The music plays at 80% of `lc_music_volume`. Players: `lc_music 0` turns it off, `lc_music_volume` sets the level.
- A change of weather is no longer said in chat (the owner, 7 November: it was only noise); you see and hear it. `!time` tells you the time and the weather. Admins: `lc_time <hour>`, `lc_weather <clear|overcast|fog|rain|storm|auto>`. `lc_atmosphere 0` turns day, night and weather off.

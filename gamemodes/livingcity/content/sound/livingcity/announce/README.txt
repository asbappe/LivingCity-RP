Custom announcements for Living City.

  overwatch/<trigger>/   the Combine PA
  breen/<trigger>/       Dr. Breen, over the loudspeakers

Put sound files (.wav, .mp3, .ogg) in a trigger's folder and the city plays one
when that thing happens. Every folder has a README.txt saying when it fires and
giving draft lines to record. An optional .txt with the same name as a sound is
its subtitle. In the game: lc_announce_clips lists what it found,
lc_announce_rescan picks up new files, lc_announce_say <overwatch|breen> <trigger>
plays one now (superadmin). Full details: README.md, "Custom announcements".

TRIGGER: blackout
WHEN: Power fails across the city.
VOICE: The Administrator, tone firm (firm: the kindly parent losing patience)

This folder is her FIRM tone (the usual one for this trigger).
If the tone she wants has no recordings she uses the nearest lower tone, then the nearest higher.
Drop .wav, .mp3 or .ogg files in this folder. One is chosen each time.
Optional subtitle: a .txt with the same name as the sound (01_x.wav + 01_x.txt).

DRAFT LINES TO RECORD:
  1. Oh dear, the lights have gone out. Stay calm and stay where you are. We are working on it, and it will be back before you know it.
  2. A small power problem, everyone. Please be sensible and don't wander about in the dark.

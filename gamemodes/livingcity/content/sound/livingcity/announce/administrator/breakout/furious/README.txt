TRIGGER: breakout
WHEN: A prisoner is broken out by the Resistance.
VOICE: The Administrator, tone furious (furious: domineering, the worst of her)

This folder is her FURIOUS tone (the usual one for this trigger).
If the tone she wants has no recordings she uses the nearest lower tone, then the nearest higher.
Drop .wav, .mp3 or .ogg files in this folder. One is chosen each time.
Optional subtitle: a .txt with the same name as the sound (01_x.wav + 01_x.txt).

DRAFT LINES TO RECORD:
  1. A prisoner has escaped, and whoever helped him will answer to me personally. Find him. Hide him, and I will find you.
  2. You have seriously misjudged me. Every one of you who touches this escape will be made to regret it. Bring them back!

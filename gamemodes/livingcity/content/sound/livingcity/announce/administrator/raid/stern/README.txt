TRIGGER: raid
WHEN: A housing block inspection (block raid) begins.
VOICE: The Administrator, tone stern (stern: a displeased, commanding parent)

This folder is her STERN tone (the usual one for this trigger).
If the tone she wants has no recordings she uses the nearest lower tone, then the nearest higher.
Drop .wav, .mp3 or .ogg files in this folder. One is chosen each time.
Optional subtitle: a .txt with the same name as the sound (01_x.wav + 01_x.txt).

DRAFT LINES TO RECORD:
  1. Residents of the block concerned, open your doors. This inspection will happen whether you cooperate or not, and I know which you will prefer.
  2. Everyone has had ample warning. Whatever you are hiding, I will find it. Step aside and let my officers work.

TRIGGER: welcome
WHEN: A few seconds after a player joins, only they hear it.
VOICE: The Administrator, tone warm (kindly parent with a cheerful host's sparkle)

This folder is her WARM tone (the usual one for this trigger).
If the tone she wants has no recordings she uses the nearest lower tone, then the nearest higher.
Drop .wav, .mp3 or .ogg files in this folder. One is chosen each time.
Optional subtitle: a .txt with the same name as the sound (01_x.wav + 01_x.txt).

DRAFT LINES TO RECORD:
  1. Welcome to the city, dear. I'm the Administrator. Do keep your papers with you, be kind to your neighbours, and you'll get on splendidly.
  2. Hello and welcome! I'm so glad you're here. We all look after each other in this city. Do come to me if there's trouble.

The radio repair bench (Radio Repair's "Tuning" shift).

As the worker turns a set's dial, these play from the set, mixed by where
the dial is: static between stations, fading into a station as they get close
(the two overlap; the hiss never quite goes until they're on it). Anyone
near the set hears it too, fading out by about twenty feet.

  static/   the hiss between stations
  union/    an ordinary station on the Union band
  freq7/    the Resistance's pirate broadcast, on the sets tuned to it

Long files that loop cleanly work best: every track keeps playing while the
dial moves and only its volume changes, and a file that ends starts again.
.wav, .mp3 and .ogg all work; one is picked per set.
lc_announce_rescan picks up new files; lc_announce_clips lists them.

The radios and the radio repair bench. Put every recording straight in this
folder; the name starts with what it is:

  static1.wav, static2.wav   the hiss between stations
  union1.wav, union2.wav     an ordinary station on the Union band (none: the
                             Administrator's ambient recordings play there)
  freq7_1.wav, freq7_2.wav   the Resistance's pirate broadcast; its .txt is the
                             line shown on the repair bench's dial panel

Long files that loop cleanly work best: every track keeps playing while the dial
moves and only its volume changes, and a file that ends starts again. Static
fills the gaps between stations and blends into them as you get close. Anyone
within about twenty feet of a set hears it, quieter further away.
.wav, .mp3 and .ogg all work. Draft Freq 7 lines: ANNOUNCEMENT_SCRIPTS.md.

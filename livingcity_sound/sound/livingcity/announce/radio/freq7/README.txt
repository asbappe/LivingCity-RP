radio/freq7/: the Resistance's pirate band. Now and then a set on the bench
is tuned to it; the worker hears it come through the static, then decides:
report the frequency, write it down, or tune past it. Anyone within about
twenty feet of the set hears it too.

An optional .txt with the same name is the line shown on the dial panel
(otherwise it says a voice comes through the static).

Draft lines to record (a tired, careful voice, a little too close to the mic):
  "This is Freq 7. They count the rations and they count you. Count them back."
  "The third shift at the station is short two units on Tuesdays. Freq 7, out."
  "If you can hear this, you are not alone. Freq 7."
  "The Administrator's broadcasts are recorded. She is not in the city."
  "Block 4: the inspection is tomorrow. Whatever you're keeping, keep it somewhere else."
  "Don't answer this. Just listen. And then turn it back to the Union band."

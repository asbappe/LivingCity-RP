TRIGGER: night
WHEN: Night falls: dark streets, curfew likelier.
VOICE: The Administrator

Her usual tone for this one: WARM. It goes up a step while the city is on red, and up again after a run of violence.

THE TONE IS IN THE FILE NAME. Put every recording for this trigger in this folder:
  a name with firm, stern or furious in it is that tone (curfew_firm_01.wav, furious_2.wav);
  any other name is her warm tone (curfew_01.wav).
If the tone she wants has no recordings she uses the nearest calmer one, then the nearest angrier one,
so one warm recording on its own covers everything (it just won't escalate).
.wav, .mp3 or .ogg. Optional subtitle: a .txt with the same name (curfew_firm_01.wav + curfew_firm_01.txt).

DRAFT LINES, WARM (THE KINDLY PARENT, THE CHEERFUL HOST):
  1. Goodnight, everyone. Sleep well and lock your doors. I'll be watching over you all.
  2. Sweet dreams, lovelies. We'll do it all again tomorrow.

DRAFT LINES, FIRM (LOSING PATIENCE):
  1. It is night. Streets are for officers now. Be indoors, and do not make me come looking for you.

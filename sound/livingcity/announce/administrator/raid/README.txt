TRIGGER: raid
WHEN: A housing block inspection (block raid) begins.
VOICE: The Administrator

Her usual tone for this one: STERN. It goes up a step while the city is on red, and up again after a run of violence.

THE TONE IS IN THE FILE NAME. Put every recording for this trigger in this folder:
  a name with firm, stern or furious in it is that tone (curfew_firm_01.wav, furious_2.wav);
  any other name is her warm tone (curfew_01.wav).
If the tone she wants has no recordings she uses the nearest calmer one, then the nearest angrier one,
so one warm recording on its own covers everything (it just won't escalate).
.wav, .mp3 or .ogg. Optional subtitle: a .txt with the same name (curfew_firm_01.wav + curfew_firm_01.txt).

DRAFT LINES, STERN (DISPLEASED):
  1. Residents of the block concerned, open your doors. This inspection will happen whether you cooperate or not, and I know which you will prefer.
  2. Everyone has had ample warning. Whatever you are hiding, I will find it. Step aside and let my officers work.

DRAFT LINES, FURIOUS (DOMINEERING):
  1. Do not insult me by pretending. Every drawer, every floorboard. You have brought this on yourselves, and you will sit quietly while it is undone.
